Toyota investigates bioethanol production in Fukushima for carbon neutral future

Toyota investigates bioethanol production in Fukushima, signaling a shift toward biomass fuels for carbon neutrality. This initiative highlights the complexity of defining sustainable energy sources, as reliance on specific crops raises questions about land use and true emission reductions. Such projects often risk greenwashing by emphasizing local recovery narratives while potentially obscuring the broader environmental costs of biofuel supply chains.
