Spotting Fake Eco-Claims: How to Buy Truly Sustainable Products

Shoppers want to buy products that are truly good for the earth. They look for this when they first hear about a product and when they decide what to buy. But this search can be confusing. Some companies use tricks to look eco-friendly. These tricks are called greenwashing. They make buyers feel safe when they make ethical choices.

Companies make more money with these tricks. A 2022 study by the European Commission found that 42% of green claims were false or exaggerated. This helps these firms beat honest competitors. They save money because they do not fix the actual environmental damage. Shoppers feel good because they think they are helping. This feeling stops them from checking if the brand is really green.

These tricks are risky for buyers. False claims hide real environmental problems. This makes shoppers support companies that still harm nature. A 2021 investigation by the UK’s Competition and Markets Authority found that 36% of green claims had no proof. This leaves shoppers open to manipulation. It also stops people from making smart choices. Over time, this hurts trust in all green products. When buyers find out a label is just a trick, they stop trusting all eco-claims. This makes it hard to find truly responsible companies later.

The Edinburgh International Book Festival controversy shows this problem. Author Mikaela Loach joined Greta Thunberg and over 100 other writers in a protest. They opposed the event’s sponsor, Baillie Gifford. They argued that the sponsor got a “social license” to keep investing in fossil fuels. This shows how companies use culture to clean up their image. People who support such events might accidentally help these tricks. They may think they are supporting good groups, while the money still goes to harmful practices.

Shoppers must look past simple labels to see the true impact of their purchases. Real sustainability needs proof and action, not just clever ads. By asking for evidence and supporting companies with a good record, users can drive change. This way, your money helps save the planet instead of supporting fake marketing.
