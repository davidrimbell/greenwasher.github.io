Carbion Dioxide Does Not Cause Warming

The article argues that mainstream climate science is fundamentally flawed, asserting that carbon dioxide does not cause global warming. The author distinguishes between academic theory and industrial engineering, claiming that private-sector gas meters and physics tests provide empirical proof that gases cool rapidly and do not retain heat. By citing historical temperature data and laboratory experiments, the text concludes that prolonged warming is driven by the Sun, not greenhouse gases, and that the scientific consensus is based on untested, false theories. This perspective is directly relevant to greenwashing because it validates the narratives used by industries to resist environmental regulations. By framing climate science as a "false notion" driven by academic corruption and funding bias, the article provides a pseudo-scientific basis for rejecting carbon reduction policies. It suggests that efforts to lower emissions are not only ineffective but economically destructive, allowing companies to dismiss sustainability initiatives as based on lies rather than rigorous scientific evidence. Ultimately, the text serves as a blueprint for denialism, encouraging skepticism toward established climate metrics. It implies that regulatory frameworks like net-zero policies are built on fraudulent premises, thereby justifying inaction on emissions. This approach contributes to greenwashing by shifting the debate from factual climate impacts to disputed scientific interpretations, enabling entities to continue high-emission practices under the guise of following "real" physics rather than adhering to global environmental standards.
