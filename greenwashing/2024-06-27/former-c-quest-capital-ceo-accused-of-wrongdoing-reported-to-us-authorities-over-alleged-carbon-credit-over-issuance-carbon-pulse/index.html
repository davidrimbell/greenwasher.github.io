Former C-Quest Capital CEO accused of wrongdoing, reported to US authorities over alleged carbon credit over-issuance « Carbon Pulse

C-Quest Capital discovered that its former CEO allegedly manipulated operations to massively over-issue voluntary carbon credits. The company has reported these serious irregularities to US authorities and dismissed several senior executives responsible for the misconduct. This incident exposes severe governance failures within the voluntary carbon market, where internal controls can be bypassed to inflate credit supplies artificially. Such actions undermine the integrity of carbon offsetting programs by creating surplus credits that lack genuine environmental value. This case is highly relevant to greenwashing as it demonstrates how corporate fraud can generate invalid offsets. Companies purchasing these credits may inadvertently claim false climate neutrality, misleading stakeholders and damaging trust in sustainability initiatives through deliberate deception.
