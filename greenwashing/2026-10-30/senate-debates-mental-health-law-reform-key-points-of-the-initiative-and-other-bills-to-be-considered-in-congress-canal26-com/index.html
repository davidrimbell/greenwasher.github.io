Senate debates mental health law reform: key points of the initiative and other bills to be considered in Congress | canal26.com

The parliamentary debate addresses the reform of the Mental Health Law, which allows involuntary hospitalizations to be ordered by a general physician and expands psychiatric classifications to include addictions such as gambling disorder. These modifications facilitate discharge from traditional institutions, raising concerns about how the real quality of outpatient or community-based treatments will be ensured without falling into institutional abandonment disguised as healthcare modernization. Additionally, the Voluntary Carbon Market and an incentive regime for investments that offers tax advantages for emission reduction projects are being discussed. This legislation establishes a regulatory framework for the generation and sale of carbon credits, allowing certain activities to generate economic benefits derived exclusively from their capacity to offset pollution. This news is relevant to greenwashing because it legitimizes the use of voluntary carbon markets as the primary solution to climate change, diverting attention from the urgent need to reduce emissions at their source. By offering tax incentives to those who purchase or generate these credits, the practice of offsetting pollution rather than eliminating it is normalized, allowing companies to maintain high levels of emissions as long as they acquire "pollution rights," thereby creating a false perception of environmental sustainability.
