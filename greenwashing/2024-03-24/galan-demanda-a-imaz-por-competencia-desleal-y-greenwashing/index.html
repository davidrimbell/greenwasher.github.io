Galán demanda a Imaz por competencia desleal y greenwashing

Iberdrola has filed a lawsuit against Repsol for unfair competition and misleading advertising, alleging that the oil company engages in “greenwashing.” The core accusation is that Repsol falsely promotes its environmental commitment and sustainability efforts, while its commercial offerings continue to incentivize the massive use of fossil fuels. Iberdrola argues that this disconnect between corporate image and operational reality constitutes consumer deception. This case is crucial for understanding greenwashing, as it exposes how companies can distort ecological truths to gain market share, thereby eroding public trust in genuine sustainability initiatives. The dispute is not only legal but also conceptual, since Iberdrola seeks to prevent marketing strategies perceived as superficial or deceptive from undermining the efforts of those genuinely working toward the energy transition. Repsol’s response, which dismisses the lawsuit as baseless and driven by competitive anxiety, illustrates the current tension between traditional business models and new sustainable approaches. By demanding the withdrawal of advertising materials and the publication of the court’s ruling, Iberdrola aims to set a legal precedent against “eco-posturing.” This conflict underscores the urgent need for genuine transparency, where corporate actions precisely align with environmental messaging to prevent widespread discredit.
