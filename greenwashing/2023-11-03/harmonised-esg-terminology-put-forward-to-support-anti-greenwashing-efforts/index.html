Harmonised ESG terminology put forward to support anti-greenwashing efforts

Major financial institutions have jointly established harmonized definitions for key responsible investment terms to address widespread confusion and improve industry communication. By clearly distinguishing strategies such as ESG integration, screening, and impact investing, these organizations aim to create a consistent global framework. This initiative responds to regulatory pressure for standardization, ensuring that market participants can accurately describe their methods and objectives without ambiguity. A critical component of this effort is providing practical guidance on how these terms are applied in real-world scenarios. For instance, investors are advised to use plain language for general audiences and restrict specific terminology to contexts where it strictly aligns with defined value-protection goals. These nuances ensure that responsible investment approaches are not conflated, allowing for a deeper understanding of how different strategies function across public and private markets. This development is highly relevant to combating greenwashing because it removes the ambiguity that often allows misleading claims to persist. When standardized definitions become the industry norm, deviating from them constitutes bad practice, thereby holding firms accountable for transparent and accurate reporting. Ultimately, this harmonization strengthens the integrity of responsible investment by making it harder for actors to obscure their true practices under vague or misused terminology.
