2023 Year in Review: Regulatory Round-Up - Global Cosmetics News

The articles highlight a significant global surge in regulatory scrutiny targeting environmental marketing claims, with major jurisdictions like the EU and UK actively enforcing anti-greenwashing laws. This shift signals a move from voluntary sustainability pledges to mandatory transparency, where companies face legal consequences and unannounced inspections if their ecological assertions are deemed misleading. This enforcement creates a more rigorous compliance landscape that directly impacts how brands communicate their environmental impact to consumers. Parallel to this regulatory crackdown, there is an increasing legislative focus on banning harmful substances such as PFAS, microplastics, and animal-tested ingredients across the US, Europe, and Asia. Governments are implementing stricter safety standards and ingredient disclosures to protect public health and the environment, effectively closing loopholes that previously allowed questionable products to remain on the market. These measures underscore a broader industry transformation where scientific safety and ethical sourcing are becoming non-negotiable requirements for market access. This content is crucial to understanding greenwashing because it demonstrates the evolving legal framework designed to punish deceptive environmental practices. As regulators strengthen their powers and expand the definition of permissible claims, the risk for brands engaging in superficial or false green marketing rises dramatically. The combination of harsh penalties, mandatory disclosures, and bans on controversial materials forces the beauty industry to align its marketing realities with verifiable scientific and ethical standards.
