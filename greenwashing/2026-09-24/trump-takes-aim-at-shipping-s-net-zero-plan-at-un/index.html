This strategic framing is designed to sow doubt among member states and obstruct the implementation of binding environmental standards. This narrative shift is particularly relevant to the study of greenwashing because it demonstrates how political actors can misrepresent comprehensive sustainability frameworks to serve protectionist or anti-regulatory interests. This deliberate mischaracterization creates confusion, potentially allowing fossil-fuel-dependent interests to resist necessary transitions by appealing to economic nationalism rather than engaging with the environmental realities of the shipping industry. Ultimately, the ongoing debate underscores the tension between genuine decarbonization efforts and political resistance disguised as fiscal conservatism. The controversy reveals how industry opponents may exploit terminology to delay or weaken regulations, thereby maintaining the status quo for high-emission technologies.
