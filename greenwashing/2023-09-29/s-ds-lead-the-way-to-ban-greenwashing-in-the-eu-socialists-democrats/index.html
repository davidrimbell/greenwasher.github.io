S&Ds lead the way to ban greenwashing in the EU | Socialists & Democrats

EU legislators have reached a pivotal agreement to shield consumers from unfair commercial practices, directly targeting the pervasive issue of greenwashing. By mandating that any product marketed as ‘green’ must possess verified excellent environmental performance, the new rules eliminate vague generic claims. This shifts the burden of proof onto manufacturers, ensuring marketing aligns with scientific reality rather than misleading perceptions. The deal specifically bans claims like ‘climate neutral’ or ‘positive’ when based solely on carbon offsets, a common tactic used to mask unsustainable practices. Furthermore, it prohibits advertising for products designed with features that intentionally limit durability. This legislative move confronts early obsolescence, forcing companies to prioritize long-lasting goods over planned failure, thereby restoring integrity to environmental marketing. Beyond stricter labeling, the agreement empowers citizens by clarifying legal guarantees for a minimum two-year period. Mandatory reminders in stores and on packaging will highlight durability rights, helping shoppers identify truly sustainable products. This transparency fosters informed choices and holds corporations accountable, marking a significant victory in the fight against deceptive environmental advertising.
