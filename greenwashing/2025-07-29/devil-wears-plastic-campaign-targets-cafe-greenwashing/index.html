Devil Wears Plastic: Campaign Targets Café Greenwashing

Devil Wears Plastic: Campaign Targets Café Greenwashing

Terra Sol Studio’s campaign exposes how "compostable" plastics are primarily greenwashing tools that mislead consumers into believing they are biodegradable when they often end up in landfills. The artwork satirizes the fashion industry’s influence on packaging narratives, revealing the stark reality behind glossy eco-labels and fake sustainability claims. This is relevant to greenwashing because it highlights the dangerous gap between marketing imagery and actual environmental impact, forcing a reevaluation of accepted sustainable practices.
