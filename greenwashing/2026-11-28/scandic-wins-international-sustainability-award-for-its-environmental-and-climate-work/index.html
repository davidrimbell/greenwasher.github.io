Scandic wins international sustainability award for its environmental and climate work

Scandic Hotels Group has been recognized with the Best Environmental and Climate Impact award, highlighting its successful integration of sustainability into core business operations. This accolade underscores the industry's growing demand for verifiable climate action, where external validation serves as a critical shield against accusations of superficial environmental claims. A primary driver of this recognition is the validation of Scandic’s climate targets by the Science Based Targets initiative, providing a rigorous framework for emission reductions across its entire value chain. By aligning with scientific standards and aiming for net-zero emissions by 2050, the company demonstrates that credible sustainability requires measurable, science-backed commitments rather than vague promises. Furthermore, the widespread adoption of the Nordic Swan Ecolabel for nearly all its Nordic hotels illustrates a commitment to comprehensive operational standards covering energy, waste, and procurement. This relevance to greenwashing lies in the contrast between certified, transparent actions and unverified marketing; Scandic’s approach shows how robust certification and third-party validation can distinguish genuine environmental stewardship from deceptive greenwashing practices.
