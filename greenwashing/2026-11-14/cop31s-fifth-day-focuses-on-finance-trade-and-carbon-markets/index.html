COP31’s fifth day focuses on finance, trade, and carbon markets

COP31 shifts toward financial mobilization and green trade, prioritizing the attraction of investments for the climate economy. This approach highlights how negotiations focus on market mechanisms and capital flows, exposing the tension between environmental objectives and profitability-driven logic. A deeper look at carbon markets reveals critical dependencies on collaborative instruments and replicable financing schemes. These technical strategies seek to validate climate action through certifications and standards, which often dilute the actual responsibility of the most polluting countries and sectors. This dynamic is relevant to greenwashing because it transforms climate urgency into tradable commodities. By emphasizing financial instruments and promises of future investment, it obscures the lack of immediate emissions reductions, allowing private actors and governments to project an image of sustainability without concrete, measurable actions.
