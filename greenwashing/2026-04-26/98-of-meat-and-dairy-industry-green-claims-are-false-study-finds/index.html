98% of Meat and Dairy Industry Green Claims Are False, Study Finds

A comprehensive study reveals that nearly all environmental claims by major meat and dairy corporations are misleading, highlighting a systemic reliance on greenwashing tactics. These companies use vague, unverifiable language to create an illusion of sustainability without committing to concrete, evidence-based actions. The research shows that these firms predominantly make future promises lacking realistic roadmaps, often substituting genuine emissions reductions with carbon offsets. This strategy mirrors historical tactics used by fossil fuel giants to delay meaningful change, allowing corporations to expand production while maintaining a deceptive eco-friendly public image. This deception is critical to understanding greenwashing because it stalls necessary transitions in the global food system. By misleading consumers and investors, these corporations preserve their profits at the expense of urgent environmental accountability. Recognizing this pattern empowers individuals to demand transparency and support only those entities engaged in authentic, transformative ecological practices rather than superficial marketing.
