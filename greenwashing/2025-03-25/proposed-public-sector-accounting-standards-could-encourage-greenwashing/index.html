Proposed public sector accounting standards ‘could encourage greenwashing’

A joint proposal warns that the International Public Sector Accounting Standards Board’s narrow definition of climate-related policies risks enabling greenwashing. By allowing policymakers to easily shift objectives to exclude certain measures, the current framework could obscure genuine environmental impact and undermine accountability. Broadening the scope to include all material policies contributing to climate targets is essential for comprehensive reporting. This approach would capture both positive and negative impacts, facilitating meaningful comparisons of trade-offs and ensuring transparency. Such inclusivity prevents manipulation and provides a clearer picture of actual progress toward emissions goals. Ultimately, robust climate reporting in the public sector is vital for driving genuine sustainability and maintaining public trust. Stakeholders urge against pausing implementation, advocating instead for phased adoption to build necessary capacity. Strong standards are crucial to demonstrate real commitment and prevent the sector from misleading the public through selective disclosure.
