COP meeting “greenwashing” politicians and countries abusing human rights – Greta Thunberg - Public Radio of Armenia

Greta Thunberg argues that United Nations climate summits have become performative greenwashing platforms, where host nations use high-profile diplomacy to mask severe human rights abuses and fossil fuel expansion. By framing repressive regimes as leaders in sustainability, these conferences create a misleading narrative of progress that obscures the lack of meaningful environmental action. This double greenwashing distorts reality by allowing authoritarian states to legitimize their policies while claiming moral authority over planetary wellbeing. The summit fails to address the urgent crisis, instead serving as a stage for political posturing rather than delivering the exponential changes required to prevent climate catastrophe. The article highlights how greenwashing extends beyond corporate eco-messaging to encompass state-level hypocrisy. It underscores the critical need to recognize when international processes prioritize image over substance, revealing how political agendas can exploit environmental concerns to justify oppression rather than protect the planet.
