Government slammed for 'net zero wokery' and 'betraying British workers'

Government slammed for 'net zero wokery' and 'betraying British workers'

The article highlights tensions between national security interests and environmental commitments, revealing that the UK Ministry of Defence imported foreign steel instead of sourcing it domestically. This decision has sparked political criticism, with lawmakers arguing that prioritizing net zero goals undermines British industry and leads to job losses. Critics suggest that the push for decarbonization effectively deindustrializes the UK, forcing reliance on higher-polluting overseas suppliers while neglecting local skilled workers. The narrative implies that current green policies are not truly sustainable, as they destroy domestic production capacity rather than fostering resilient, low-carbon industries within the country. This case is relevant to greenwashing because it exposes the contradiction between political rhetoric on sustainability and actual procurement practices. It suggests that environmental mandates may be used to justify outsourcing, potentially masking the true carbon footprint of supply chains while claiming ecological responsibility.
