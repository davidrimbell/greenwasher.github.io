Greenwashing
ES EN

Global debt reached unprecedented levels, highlighting the hidden financial fragility underlying market narratives. This record liability burden often serves as a backdrop for corporate greenwashing, where companies obscure true environmental costs while expanding operations. Investors must recognize how such vast debt structures enable entities to promote eco-friendly initiatives without addressing systemic ecological or financial impacts. The focus should remain on transparency, exposing how superficial sustainability claims mask deeper, unsustainable business practices and resource exploitation.
