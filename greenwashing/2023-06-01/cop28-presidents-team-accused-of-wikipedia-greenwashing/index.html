"Cop28 President’s Team Accused Of Wikipedia ‘Greenwashing’"

Critics accuse UAE officials of manipulating Wikipedia to sanitize Cop28 President Sultan Al Jaber’s image by removing records of his oil industry ties. This deliberate editing suggests an attempt to obscure his role as ADNOC CEO, highlighting a transparent effort to control the narrative surrounding the climate summit. The incident demonstrates how fossil fuel leaders exploit digital platforms to airbrush criticism and reframe their involvement as beneficial. By selectively altering public records, executives can present a sanitized persona that contradicts their corporate reality, effectively managing public perception through misinformation. This case is relevant to greenwashing as it reveals a new tactic where environmental hypocrisy is digitally concealed. It shows that greenwashing extends beyond marketing claims to include active disinformation campaigns, allowing oil industry figures to maintain influence over climate discourse while hiding their contradictory interests.
