Council aims to fight greenwashing with new rules for companies in the EU

Council aims to fight greenwashing with new rules for companies in the EU

The EU is advancing strict regulations to combat greenwashing by requiring independent verification for all voluntary environmental claims. This legislative push mandates that explicit statements regarding sustainability, such as being "biodegradable" or "carbon neutral," must be backed by robust scientific evidence before reaching consumers. To ensure transparency, the rules introduce third-party expert checks for both company assertions and public labeling schemes. While microenterprises receive temporary relief to manage compliance burdens, the core intent remains holding brands accountable for the factual basis of their eco-marketing, thereby preventing misleading narratives. This initiative is vital to greenwashing discussions as it shifts the burden of proof onto corporations. By banning vague terminology and demanding detailed carbon offset disclosures, the directive aims to restore consumer trust and ensure that green choices are genuinely informed by accurate environmental data.
