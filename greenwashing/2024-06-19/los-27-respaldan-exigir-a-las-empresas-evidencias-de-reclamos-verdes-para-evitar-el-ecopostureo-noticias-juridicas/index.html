Los 27 respaldan exigir a las empresas evidencias de reclamos verdes para evitar el ecopostureo · Noticias Jurídicas

Los 27 respaldan exigir a las empresas evidencias de reclamos verdes para evitar el ecopostureo · Noticias Jurídicas

The European Union has mandated that companies must substantiate environmental claims with credible scientific evidence, effectively combating greenwashing by eliminating vague or misleading marketing. This regulation ensures transparency by requiring verifiable data for any eco-friendly assertions, preventing businesses from using unsubstantiated "green" labels to manipulate consumer perception. Furthermore, the directive imposes strict rules on comparative statements and carbon credit disclosures, demanding equivalent data for comparisons and detailed proof regarding the permanence of carbon offsets. By enforcing these rigorous verification processes, the EU aims to establish clear accountability standards across member states. This development is crucial for preventing greenwashing as it shifts the burden of proof onto corporations, ensuring that environmental benefits are real and measurable. It protects consumers from deceptive practices while promoting genuine sustainability efforts through transparent, evidence-based communication.
