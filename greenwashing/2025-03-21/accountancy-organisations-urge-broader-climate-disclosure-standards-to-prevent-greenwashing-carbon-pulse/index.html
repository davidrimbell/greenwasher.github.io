Accountancy organisations urge broader climate disclosure standards to prevent greenwashing « Carbon Pulse

Accountancy organisations urge broader climate disclosure standards to prevent greenwashing « Carbon Pulse

Chartered accountancy bodies urge the IPSASB to expand climate disclosure requirements for public sector entities. They warn that the current limited scope creates gaps, potentially enabling greenwashing by allowing institutions to hide their full environmental impact. This intervention highlights the critical link between rigorous financial reporting standards and effective climate accountability. Without comprehensive, standardized disclosures, stakeholders cannot accurately assess an organization’s true progress toward net zero goals. The article is relevant to greenwashing because it identifies a specific regulatory failure risk. It demonstrates how incomplete or narrow accounting rules can inadvertently facilitate misleading sustainability claims in the public sector.
