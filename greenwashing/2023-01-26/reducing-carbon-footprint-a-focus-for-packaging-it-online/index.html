Reducing carbon footprint a focus for packaging - IT-Online

Reducing carbon footprint a focus for packaging - IT-Online

As many sustainable packaging targets fail due to operational and financial barriers, organizations are pivoting from recycling goals to reducing carbon footprints by 2026. This strategic shift allows companies to address realistic constraints while still demonstrating meaningful progress toward environmental sustainability. Early adopters who educate stakeholders about Life Cycle Assessment are better positioned to defend their climate contributions. By highlighting reductions in greenhouse gas emissions through efficient material use, these entities can mitigate the reputational damage associated with missed pledges and build trust through transparent, data-driven metrics. This transition is critical to understanding greenwashing, as companies may appear to abandon previous commitments under pressure. However, shifting to realistic, science-based metrics like LCA offers a legitimate path to accountability. Organizations that embrace these verifiable frameworks are less likely to face backlash for unfulfilled promises, whereas those maintaining denial risk exposing superficial efforts as deceptive greenwashing.
