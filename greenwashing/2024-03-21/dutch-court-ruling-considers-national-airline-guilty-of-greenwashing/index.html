Dutch court ruling considers national airline guilty of greenwashing

A Dutch court has ruled that KLM engaged in greenwashing by misleading consumers with advertisements suggesting its operations are sustainable. The judgment found the airline’s “Fly Responsibly” campaign created a false impression that flying is environmentally friendly, despite measures like sustainable aviation fuel only marginally reducing its negative impact. This landmark decision emphasizes that companies cannot claim to tackle climate change while continuing to fuel the crisis through mass aviation. The court declared terms like “sustainable” too absolute and lacked sufficient concreteness, establishing a legal precedent that environmental marketing must be honest and transparent rather than offering an overly optimistic narrative. The ruling is highly relevant to greenwashing as it demonstrates growing regulatory scrutiny of corporate environmental claims. It signals that vague marketing language will no longer suffice, forcing brands to provide concrete evidence for their ecological assertions or face legal consequences, thereby raising the standard for truthful corporate communication.
