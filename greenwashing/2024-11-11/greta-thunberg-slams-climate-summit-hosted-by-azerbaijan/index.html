Greta Thunberg Slams Climate Summit Hosted By Azerbaijan

Greta Thunberg condemns Azerbaijan’s hosting of COP29 as a deceptive publicity stunt. She argues the summit whitewashes the host nation’s harmful actions while pretending to prioritize environmental care. This highlights how major climate events can mask serious ecological violations through superficial green commitments.
