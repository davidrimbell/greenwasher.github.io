SDR seen as effective at curbing greenwashing – IA survey

The UK’s Sustainability Disclosure Requirements are successfully establishing clearer standards and enhancing transparency, which serves as a crucial defense against greenwashing by reducing misleading claims in sustainable finance. Although regulatory hurdles caused many firms to abandon or delay labeling, the regime has effectively forced the industry to update investment strategies and explicitly define sustainability objectives, thereby creating a more honest market environment. By setting a minimum baseline for what constitutes sustainable investing, the framework helps distinguish genuinely eco-friendly products from those merely using vague ESG marketing tactics. This standardization empowers investors to differentiate between labeled funds and non-labeled products that utilize specific environmental techniques, ensuring that consumer confidence is not eroded by ambiguous or exaggerated environmental assertions in the broader market. Ultimately, the article highlights that while adoption rates were lower than expected, the core mechanism of the regulation remains vital for combating deception. The focus on naming and marketing rules ensures that even non-labeled funds face stricter scrutiny, preventing them from exploiting sustainability trends without substantive commitment, which is essential for maintaining integrity in green financial products.
