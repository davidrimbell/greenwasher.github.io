Greenwashing
ES EN

Artificial scarcity drives subscription sales. This tactic exploits consumer urgency. It obscures actual product value through deceptive marketing pressure. The urgency created is temporary. Consumers feel compelled to act quickly. This psychological manipulation prioritizes revenue over transparency. This relates to greenwashing as it uses misleading urgency to secure compliance. Just as fake eco-claims deceive environmental concerns, fake deadlines deceive purchasing decisions. Both rely on creating false narratives to influence behavior.
