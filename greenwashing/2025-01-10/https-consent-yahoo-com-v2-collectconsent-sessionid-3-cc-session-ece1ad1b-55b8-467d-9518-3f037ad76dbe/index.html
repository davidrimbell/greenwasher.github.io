Greenwashing
ES EN

Cookie consent banners often masquerade as simple privacy controls, obscuring the extent of data harvesting. This complexity enables companies to normalize pervasive tracking under the guise of transparency. Such practices constitute greenwashing by disguising invasive surveillance as user empowerment, misleading consumers about their actual loss of autonomy.
