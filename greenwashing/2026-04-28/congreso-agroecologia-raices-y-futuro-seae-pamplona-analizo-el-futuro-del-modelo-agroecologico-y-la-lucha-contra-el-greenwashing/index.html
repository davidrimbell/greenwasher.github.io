Congreso agroecología raíces y futuro SEAE Pamplona analizó el futuro del modelo agroecológico y la lucha contra el greenwashing

This article highlights a critical debate within the agroecology sector regarding the integrity of sustainable agriculture labels. The main conclusion is that large agribusinesses are increasingly engaging in greenwashing by co-opting terms like "regenerative" for marketing purposes, thereby confusing consumers and diluting the meaning of genuine ecological practices. The event underscores the urgent need to protect official European organic certification from commercial interests that seek to exploit vague environmental terminology. Experts argue that without strict definitions and rigorous scientific criteria, the distinction between truly sustainable agroecology and industrial models disguised as green collapses, ultimately undermining consumer trust and market fairness. The relevance to greenwashing lies in the sector’s defensive strategy to combat this deception. The article advocates for stronger political influence and stricter European regulations to ensure transparency. It emphasizes that combating greenwashing is not just about labeling, but about establishing a legally robust framework that preserves the credibility of the ecological sector against corporate manipulation.
