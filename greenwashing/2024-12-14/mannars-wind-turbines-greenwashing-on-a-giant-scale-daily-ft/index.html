Mannar’s wind turbines: Greenwashing on a giant scale | Daily FT

Mannar’s wind turbines: Greenwashing on a giant scale | Daily FT

The article critiques the development of massive wind farms in Mannar, Sri Lanka, as a prime example of greenwashing where "climate mitigation" rhetoric masks significant ecological and social costs. Despite being hailed as clean energy, these projects impose severe disruptions on local communities, including persistent vibrations, noise pollution, and the destruction of livelihoods through reduced fish catches and damaged agriculture. The narrative highlights how the noble goal of energy security is undermined by the actual degradation of living conditions for residents who are left to manage the unintended consequences of industrial intrusion. Furthermore, the environmental impact is disproportionately high due to the location’s status as a critical stop on a major migratory bird flyway. The installation of towering turbines and service roads threatens biodiversity by blocking natural drainage channels, which has already led to severe flooding and habitat destruction. The project violates international conservation treaties and ignores internal environmental assessments that flagged the fragility of the landscape. This demonstrates how green energy initiatives can become environmentally destructive when scientific backing for site selection is ignored in favor of rapid deployment. Finally, the case reveals economic and geopolitical risks, such as predatory pricing by the involved multinational corporation and questions regarding national sovereignty. By locking the country into high-cost, long-term contracts with a firm facing legal scrutiny for alleged misconduct, the project potentially traps the nation in debt rather than alleviating it. This situation underscores the danger of prioritizing foreign investment over community dignity and ecological integrity, proving that not all green projects are genuinely sustainable or beneficial when their broader implications are overlooked.
