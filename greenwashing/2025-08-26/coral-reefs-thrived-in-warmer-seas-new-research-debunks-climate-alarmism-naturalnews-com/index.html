Coral reefs thrived in warmer seas – new research debunks climate alarmism – NaturalNews.com

The article argues that modern coral reef decline is primarily driven by local human impacts, such as pollution and overfishing, rather than global climate change. By citing historical data showing significantly faster coral growth during ancient warmer periods with higher CO2 levels, the author claims this evidence directly contradicts mainstream climate warnings. This perspective suggests that current fears regarding ocean warming are exaggerated or misdirected, shifting the blame away from atmospheric carbon and toward immediate, manageable local environmental degradation. Furthermore, the text posits that the emphasis on climate crises serves a political agenda aimed at centralizing power and restricting individual freedoms. It asserts that the promotion of carbon dioxide as a harmful pollutant is a strategic narrative used to justify economic controls and technological surveillance, rather than genuine ecological conservation. By portraying environmentalism as a tool for totalitarian control, the article contends that established institutions exploit fear to enforce policies that would otherwise face public resistance, undermining the credibility of climate science in the process. This article is relevant to greenwashing because it exemplifies "reverse greenwashing," where climate action itself is framed as the deceptive practice. It attempts to discredit legitimate environmental concerns by labeling them as fabricated crises designed to manipulate public opinion. By arguing that the push for net-zero policies is a cynical power grab rather than a scientific necessity, the text encourages viewers to reject established environmental consensus. This approach mirrors greenwashing tactics by replacing substantive ecological stewardship with ideological resistance, ultimately obscuring the real need for sustainable local management practices.
