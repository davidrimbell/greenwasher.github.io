Tough for Malaysians to detect greenwashing, say experts

Tough for Malaysians to detect greenwashing, say experts

Companies exploit environmental concerns with vague, uncertified eco-labels to attract conscious consumers. This practice constitutes greenwashing by misleading buyers who trust packaging claims lacking credible certification. Consumers often fall victim to these deceptive marketing tactics, believing products are truly sustainable when they are not.
