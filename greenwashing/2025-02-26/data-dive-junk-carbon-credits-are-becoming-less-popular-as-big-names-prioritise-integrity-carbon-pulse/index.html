DATA DIVE: Junk carbon credits are becoming less popular, as big names prioritise integrity « Carbon Pulse

DATA DIVE: Junk carbon credits are becoming less popular, as big names prioritise integrity « Carbon Pulse

Corporations are increasingly shifting away from low-quality carbon credits, favoring those with verified high integrity. This trend signals a growing corporate demand for authenticity in climate investments, moving beyond superficial environmental claims. The rejection of dubious projects highlights a critical distinction between genuine sustainability efforts and deceptive marketing tactics used to appear eco-friendly. This shift is relevant to greenwashing because it demonstrates that companies are facing greater scrutiny and are actively avoiding the low-integrity credits often associated with misleading environmental assertions.
