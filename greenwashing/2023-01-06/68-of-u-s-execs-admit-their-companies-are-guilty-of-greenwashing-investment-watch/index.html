68% of U.S. execs admit their companies are guilty of greenwashing – Investment Watch

68% of U.S. execs admit their companies are guilty of greenwashing – Investment Watch

Many executives prioritize sustainability, yet a significant majority admits their companies engage in greenwashing. This admission highlights a critical disconnect between corporate public relations and genuine environmental action, exposing superficial claims as deceptive marketing tactics. Despite widespread intent to improve, most leaders lack the knowledge to implement authentic change. This ignorance allows firms to maintain vague promises while continuing misleading practices, thereby perpetuating the cycle of environmental misrepresentation that defines greenwashing. Consequently, executive self-assessments often rate sustainability efforts highly despite internal doubts about their genuineness. This article illustrates how corporate leaders often confuse performative commitments with real impact, demonstrating how greenwashing thrives when transparency is replaced by self-congratulatory narratives.
