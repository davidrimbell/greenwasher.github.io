El regulador de Reino Unido prohíbe los avisos de lavado verde de Shell, Repsol y Petronas | Diario Financiero

The UK advertising regulator has banned advertisements from Shell, Repsol, and Petronas for misleading the public about their environmental credentials, setting a critical precedent against greenwashing. The decision was based on the finding that these companies omitted essential information about the predominance of their polluting activities, falsely presenting their operations as sustainable. This ruling demonstrates that authorities are willing to penalize the disconnect between corporate promotion and the reality of fossil fuel-based businesses. The sanctioned entities argued that their investments in clean energy represented a significant part of their strategies, but the regulator dismissed these claims by pointing out that oil and gas extraction remained at the core of their business models. By ignoring this fundamental reality, the advertisements created a misleading impression that the companies were leading an ecological transition, when in fact they continued to expand their most climate-damaging operations. This intervention is relevant to understanding greenwashing because it illustrates how companies use selective narratives to distort perceptions of their environmental impact. By banning promotions of "net zero" targets without context, the regulator exposes the deceptive practice of concealing the scale of pollution to appear environmentally responsible. This growing scrutiny reflects a global trend toward demanding transparency and preventing environmental claims from masking the ongoing extraction of non-renewable resources.
