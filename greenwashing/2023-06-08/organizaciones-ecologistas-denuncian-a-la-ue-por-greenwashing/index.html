Organizaciones ecologistas denuncian a la UE por 'greenwashing'

Environmental groups argue that the European Commission is engaging in greenwashing by falsely labeling natural gas and nuclear energy as sustainable investments. This misleading classification creates a false perception of ecological responsibility for harmful energy sources, effectively prioritizing economic and transitional interests over genuine environmental protection. The core issue lies in validating fossil fuels based on arbitrary emission thresholds rather than scientific reality. By equating gas and nuclear power with renewables, the Commission undermines efforts to drastically reduce carbon emissions. This approach contradicts climate laws and ignores expert advice, suggesting a systemic bias toward transitional technologies that continue to release significant greenhouse gases. This case is crucial for understanding institutional greenwashing, where powerful entities manipulate sustainability labels to maintain funding and public support for polluting industries. It highlights how regulatory frameworks can be exploited to legitimize environmentally damaging activities under the guise of a green transition, eroding trust in official sustainability claims and hindering effective climate action.
