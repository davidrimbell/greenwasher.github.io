Reino Unido veta anuncios de petroleras como Shell, Repsol y Petronas por incurrir en 'greenwashing'

The UK Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) has banned advertisements from Shell, Repsol, and Petronas for engaging in greenwashing. The regulator determined that these campaigns mislead consumers by promoting eco-friendly initiatives while concealing the fact that their core businesses remain heavily dependent on harmful fossil fuels. This action sets a firm precedent against corporate environmental misinformation. The companies defended their messaging as efforts to raise awareness about low-carbon options, arguing that highlighting their high carbon content would be counterproductive. However, the regulator demanded balance, pointing out that omitting the scale of total emissions distorts the companies’ actual contribution to sustainability. The environmental benefits were deemed negligible in light of the overall impact of their operations. This case is crucial for understanding greenwashing, as it exposes the strategy employed by major oil companies to cleanse their public image. By banning these ads, the regulator acknowledges the harm of portraying these firms as agents of climate change when, in reality, they sustain the crisis. This regulatory approach threatens the sustainability narratives used by other sectors, such as aviation, to evade environmental responsibility and manipulate public perception.
