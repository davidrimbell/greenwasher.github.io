S&Ds on new Green Claims Directive: No more misleading claims and greenwashing for eco labels | Socialists & Democrats

The European Parliament has adopted a pivotal position on the Green Claims Directive, establishing strict rules to force companies to substantiate voluntary environmental assertions. This legislative move directly targets the prevalence of vague, misleading, and unfounded green marketing practices that currently confuse consumers and erode trust in eco-friendly labeling. A central focus of the directive is the implementation of ex-ante verification by independent bodies before any claim or label is approved. By requiring pre-certification, the regulation aims to prevent unsubstantiated "green" or "eco-friendly" marketing, ensuring that environmental labels are not merely deceptive tactics but are backed by credible, verified evidence of sustainability. This development is highly relevant to greenwashing as it shifts the burden of proof onto traders, compelling them to provide concrete data rather than relying on ambiguous buzzwords. Ultimately, the directive seeks to protect consumers from deceptive environmental marketing while fostering a genuine transition toward a cleaner, more transparent market where ethical consumption is supported by verified facts.
