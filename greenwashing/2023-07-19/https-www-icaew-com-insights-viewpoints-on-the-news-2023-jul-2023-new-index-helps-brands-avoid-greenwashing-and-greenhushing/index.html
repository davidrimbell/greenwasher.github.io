Greenwashing
ES EN

This report introduces the Sustainability Gap Index, a tool designed to measure the discrepancy between public perception and actual corporate sustainability performance. By mapping consumer attitudes against rigorous data, it identifies whether brands are overstating their efforts, which constitutes greenwashing, or underplaying them, known as greenhushing. This distinction is critical for understanding how misaligned communication can either expose companies to reputational backlash or cause them to miss valuable opportunities for engagement. The findings demonstrate that closing these gaps presents a significant financial imperative. Companies with high perception but low actual performance risk substantial devaluation as consumers react to exposed greenwashing. Conversely, firms with strong tangible results but weak visibility can unlock billions in value by effectively communicating their genuine commitments. This suggests that accurate storytelling is not just a marketing tactic but a core driver of brand equity and shareholder value, requiring strategic investment in both action and narrative. This analysis is highly relevant to greenwashing because it quantifies the financial risks associated with misleading sustainability claims. It underscores that greenwashing is not merely an ethical failing but a vulnerable business strategy that can lead to immediate economic losses when public opinion corrects itself. Furthermore, it highlights the emerging danger of greenhushing, where fear of scrutiny causes companies to hide real progress. Ultimately, the report urges financial leaders to balance authentic performance with clear communication to mitigate risks and maximize returns in an increasingly scrutinized market.
