UK competition regulator clamps down on greenwashing in fashion sector « Carbon Pulse

Major UK fashion retailers are reforming how they market environmental claims following regulatory pressure against greenwashing. This shift specifically restricts the sector’s reliance on carbon offsets for sustainability messaging. The crackdown demonstrates increased enforcement against misleading eco-labels, signaling stricter scrutiny of corporate environmental assertions. Consequently, companies must ensure their green credentials are substantiated and transparent rather than exaggerated. This case highlights the growing risk for brands using vague or unverified sustainability narratives. It serves as a critical warning that greenwashing tactics will face significant legal and reputational consequences in the marketplace.
