Greenwashing
ES EN

India’s consumer protection authority proposes strict guidelines to regulate environmental marketing claims. These rules mandate that terms like “eco-friendly” or “carbon-neutral” must be substantiated with credible certification or scientific evidence, preventing vague or misleading assertions. Companies are required to provide transparent, verifiable data for all comparative or specific environmental statements. By linking substantiation via digital means, the framework aims to empower consumers to identify deceptive practices and file complaints under existing consumer protection laws. This initiative is crucial for combating greenwashing by enforcing accountability in sustainability marketing. It shifts the burden of proof onto advertisers, ensuring environmental claims are truthful rather than purely promotional. This regulatory approach strengthens consumer trust and reduces the prevalence of exaggerated ecological benefits in the market.
