Women are the new target of Canada's fossil fuel greenwashing machine

The article exposes "Canada Powered by Women" as a fossil fuel-backed organization that uses gender-focused narratives to undermine climate action. By publishing content highlighting the drawbacks of electric vehicles and net-zero policies, the group employs a strategy often seen in greenwashing: using socially progressive themes, such as women’s empowerment and affordability concerns, to mask the promotion of harmful industries. This approach distracts from the environmental necessity of transitioning away from oil and gas, effectively disguising anti-climate messaging as a defense of women’s interests. The organization’s credibility is further compromised by its opaque funding and leadership. Its board consists entirely of oil and gas executives, and while it claims to be funded by individual donations, it also receives support from energy and financial sectors. The group recently removed controversial content from its website following the introduction of Canada’s anti-greenwashing legislation, an act that mirrors tactics used by other major oil lobby groups to evade regulatory scrutiny and hide their true agenda. Experts argue that this campaign is a disinformation tactic designed to create a false consensus against climate policy. Academic research consistently shows that women are disproportionately harmed by climate change and generally support stronger environmental protections. By manufacturing opposition and hiding its corporate ties, the organization exploits gender dynamics to slow the clean energy transition, illustrating how greenwashing evolves to co-opt social justice movements for industrial preservation.
