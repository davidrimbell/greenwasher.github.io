Greenhushing: el silencio sostenible de las marcas

Greenhushing: el silencio sostenible de las marcas

Companies are increasingly engaging in greenhushing, deliberately concealing their sustainability initiatives to avoid accusations of greenwashing. This silence undermines genuine progress, as effective communication is essential for validating environmental actions. As a result, brands risk obscuring their contributions to sustainability amid growing public scrutiny.
