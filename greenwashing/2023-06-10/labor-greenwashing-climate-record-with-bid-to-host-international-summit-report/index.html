Labor 'greenwashing' climate record with bid to host international summit: report

The Australia Institute accuses the Albanese government of greenwashing by attempting to co-host COP31 while continuing to approve new coal and gas projects. This strategy is criticized as reversing cause and effect, suggesting the state seeks to repair its international reputation through hosting privileges rather than substantive policy changes. The report argues that granting hosting rights without prior action rewards decades of inaction. It frames the bid as a potential mechanism for Pacific nations to demand accountability from Australia, leveraging the summit to address the existential climate threats facing the region. This dynamic shifts the burden onto vulnerable neighbors to force responsible citizenship from a major fossil fuel exporter. This issue is critical to understanding greenwashing because it highlights how nations may use high-profile international events to mask poor environmental records. By prioritizing image management over immediate emission reductions, the government risks using the COP platform to legitimize continued fossil fuel expansion under the guise of regional cooperation and climate leadership.
