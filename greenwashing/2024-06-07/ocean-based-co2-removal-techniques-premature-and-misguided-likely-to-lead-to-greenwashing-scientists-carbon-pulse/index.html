Ocean-based CO2 removal techniques "premature and misguided", likely to lead to greenwashing -scientists « Carbon Pulse

Ocean-based CO2 removal techniques "premature and misguided", likely to lead to greenwashing -scientists « Carbon Pulse

Ocean-based carbon removal efforts face critical knowledge gaps that threaten the integrity of voluntary carbon markets. These scientific uncertainties undermine the reliability of offset schemes claiming climate benefits from marine solutions. This highlights a significant greenwashing risk, as unverified removal claims may mislead consumers and investors. The lack of robust data allows potentially ineffective projects to gain credibility under the guise of environmental stewardship. Consequently, the article warns that expanding ocean-based methods without rigorous verification standards compromises the broader net-zero narrative. It underscores the urgent need for transparency to prevent misleading environmental marketing in emerging carbon removal sectors.
