McDonald's und die Initiative Tierwohl: Warum wir die Greenwashing-Keule stecken lassen sollten - HORIZONT

McDonald's announced a partnership with Initiative Tierwohl to enhance product sustainability, sparking attention. However, this cooperation cannot obscure the company's other critical issues. The author argues that while the move signals progress, it fails to address broader corporate flaws. This highlights a common greenwashing tactic where isolated initiatives mask deeper environmental or ethical problems. Ultimately, such partnerships serve more as marketing tools than comprehensive solutions. This case underscores the need to scrutinize whether sustainability claims reflect genuine systemic change or merely superficial adjustments.
