Carbon begins to pay in the field | El Litoral

The carbon market is evolving from an abstract promise to a real source of income for South American ranchers. This shift enables producers to receive direct compensation for adopting regenerative practices, thereby economically validating soil restoration. Ruuts stands out as a pioneer by issuing the first large-scale verified carbon credits in the region, demonstrating that regenerative livestock farming generates tangible productive and environmental benefits through an adaptive methodology. This initiative is relevant to the debate on greenwashing, as it exemplifies a rigorous verification mechanism that channels real resources into climate action, distinguishing itself from empty claims and showcasing the economic viability of genuine environmental sustainability.
