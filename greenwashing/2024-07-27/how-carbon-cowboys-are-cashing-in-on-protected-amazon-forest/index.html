"How ‘Carbon Cowboys’ Are Cashing In On Protected Amazon Forest"

"How ‘Carbon Cowboys’ Are Cashing In On Protected Amazon Forest"

Investigation reveals carbon credit projects often overlap significantly with protected public lands, allowing companies to profit from areas they do not own. This practice raises serious concerns about the integrity of greenwashing in environmental markets. The ventures claim vast tracts of land, generating millions in revenue while failing to share profits with local forest guardians. This highlights how "green" labels can mask exploitation of public resources. This matters for greenwashing because it shows how carbon markets can be manipulated. It warns against trusting corporate environmental claims without verifying land rights and benefit sharing.
