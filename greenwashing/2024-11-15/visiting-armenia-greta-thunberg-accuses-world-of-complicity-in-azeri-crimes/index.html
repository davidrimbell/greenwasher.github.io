Visiting Armenia, Greta Thunberg Accuses World Of Complicity In ‘Azeri Crimes’

Visiting Armenia, Greta Thunberg Accuses World Of Complicity In ‘Azeri Crimes’

Greta Thunberg argues that hosting the UN climate summit in Azerbaijan exposes global hypocrisy, as the host nation’s severe human rights abuses and ethnic cleansing are being ignored. She asserts that climate conferences in such contexts fail to deliver meaningful change, instead providing a platform that legitimizes oppressive regimes under the guise of environmental cooperation. The article highlights how international silence allows states to "greenwash" gross violations of human rights. By participating in these summits, countries buying Azerbaijani fossil fuels become complicit in these crimes, using the climate agenda to deflect attention from military aggression and the suffering of displaced populations. This narrative is crucial for understanding greenwashing because it reveals how environmental platforms can be exploited to sanitize political misconduct. It demonstrates that legitimacy gained through global climate summits can mask systemic injustice, proving that environmental rhetoric often serves to obscure rather than address fundamental ethical breaches by host nations.
