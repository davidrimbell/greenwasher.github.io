Baku + Cali = La Doble Farsa del Ecologismo. Cumbres del Negacionismo al Servicio del Lobby Ganadero-Pesquero y del Greenwashing - Tercera Información

Baku + Cali = La Doble Farsa del Ecologismo. Cumbres del Negacionismo al Servicio del Lobby Ganadero-Pesquero y del Greenwashing - Tercera Información

The article argues that major UN summits on climate and biodiversity serve as elaborate farces, prioritizing cosmetic measures over the systemic changes required to address ecological crises. It asserts that mainstream environmental organizations are complicit in this deception by refusing to discuss the root causes: the necessity of shifting to plant-based diets and implementing deep degrowth. By ignoring these critical issues, these groups inadvertently support the very industries driving environmental destruction, allowing harmful practices to continue under the guise of protection. This silence is identified as a central mechanism of greenwashing, where superficial solutions like green bonds and renewable energy projects mask the continued expansion of fossil fuel use and industrial consumption. The text highlights the hypocrisy of protecting ecosystems in theory while permitting destructive activities like industrial fishing and deforestation in practice. Furthermore, it critiques the reliance on renewable infrastructure, arguing that these technologies often cause significant land-use damage and remain dependent on fossil fuels, thereby perpetuating a model of unsustainable growth rather than facilitating a genuine transition. Ultimately, the article contends that the failure of environmental movements to challenge the status quo constitutes a form of denialism that enables corporate lobbies, particularly in the meat and fishing sectors, to dominate policy. It posits that without addressing the immense impact of animal agriculture and curbing global consumption levels, technical fixes are merely window dressing. The author calls for an urgent rejection of this silence by major NGOs, urging a shift toward radical transparency about the true drivers of the climate and biodiversity emergencies.
