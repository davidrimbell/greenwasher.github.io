Daughter of imprisoned Azerbaijan activist campaigns for release amid ‘greenwash’ COP

Daughter of imprisoned Azerbaijan activist campaigns for release amid ‘greenwash’ COP

Azerbaijan’s hosting of the UN climate summit is widely criticized as greenwashing, using the event to mask severe human rights abuses. The government has intensified its crackdown on dissent, imprisoning hundreds of activists, including prominent economist Dr. Gubad Ibadoghlu, under questionable charges. This strategic use of a global environmental platform to improve its international image highlights how authoritarian regimes exploit green initiatives to divert attention from repression. The disparity between hosting climate talks and silencing critics underscores the insincerity of such environmental commitments. The article is relevant to greenwashing because it demonstrates how fossil-fuel-rich nations leverage climate summits for reputational laundering. It reveals that the primary goal may not be environmental action, but rather distracting from poor human rights records through superficial eco-friendly posturing.
