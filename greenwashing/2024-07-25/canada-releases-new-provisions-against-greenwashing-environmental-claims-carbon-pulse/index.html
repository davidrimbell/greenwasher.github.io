Canada releases new provisions against greenwashing, environmental claims « Carbon Pulse

Canada’s Competition Bureau released new provisions targeting environmental claims and greenwashing. These guidelines aim to help companies avoid misleading advertising and deceptive marketing practices under current laws. This regulatory shift is crucial for enforcing transparency and holding brands accountable for false eco-friendly assertions.
