Greenwashing
ES EN

This article outlines how major companies in Argentina are integrating sustainability into their core strategies, aligning their operations with the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). These firms emphasize that environmental and social commitment is no longer optional but a fundamental pillar of their business model and decision-making processes. However, from a greenwashing perspective, it is crucial to distinguish between intentional statements and verifiable actions. Although companies present progressive initiatives, such as the use of renewable materials or carbon neutrality targets, many admit they are only at intermediate stages of their ambitious projects. This discrepancy between the projected image and current operational reality highlights the need to assess the authenticity of these strategies in the face of informed consumer skepticism. The relevance to greenwashing analysis lies in transparency regarding real challenges versus publicized achievements. By openly acknowledging significant gaps in recycling or greenhouse gas emissions, these companies provide a case study for determining whether they communicate honestly or use technical complexity to mask the slow pace of transformation. This debate is essential to protect stakeholders from superficial narratives and to demand genuine accountability.
