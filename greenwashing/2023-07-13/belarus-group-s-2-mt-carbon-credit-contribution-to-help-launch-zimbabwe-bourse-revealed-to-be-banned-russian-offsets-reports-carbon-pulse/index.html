Belarus group's 2-Mt carbon credit contribution to help launch Zimbabwe bourse revealed to be banned Russian offsets -reports « Carbon Pulse

A Belarusian donation to Zimbabwe’s new carbon exchange was exposed as consisting of banned Russian offsets. This revelation threatens to deter future climate investment in the region, highlighting significant governance failures in emerging markets. This incident illustrates greenwashing risks within voluntary carbon markets, where dubious origins can be disguised through donations. It underscores the urgent need for rigorous verification to prevent tainted credits from undermining market integrity. The story warns that weak oversight allows illicit assets to enter legitimate trading platforms. Such practices erode trust, demonstrating how inadequate governance can facilitate the laundering of reputation alongside carbon credits.
