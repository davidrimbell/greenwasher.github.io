Verdes Equo denuncia el greenwashing y pide más avances en la separación de residuos - El Faradio | Periodismo que cuenta

The Government of Cantabria is promoting waste separation campaigns despite lacking the necessary infrastructure for most citizens, a disconnect that environmental group Verdes Equo condemns as greenwashing. With the vast majority of municipalities and residents, including those in the capital, Santander, unable to use the new organic waste bins, these awareness efforts are perceived as contradictory and financially wasteful rather than genuinely helpful. Furthermore, the actual environmental benefit is severely undermined by the logistical reality that collected organic waste is transported to a facility in another region. This externalization generates significant carbon emissions and costs, effectively canceling out the supposed ecological advantages of selective collection. Consequently, the initiative functions more as a transfer of responsibility than a true advancement in local sustainability or circular economy practices. This case highlights the danger of prioritizing political propaganda over substantive action. Launching educational campaigns without corresponding infrastructure not only frustrates the public but also discredits genuine environmental policies. The situation serves as a stark reminder that sustainable transitions require coherent planning and tangible resources, rather than superficial measures that lack the necessary support for citizens to participate meaningfully.
