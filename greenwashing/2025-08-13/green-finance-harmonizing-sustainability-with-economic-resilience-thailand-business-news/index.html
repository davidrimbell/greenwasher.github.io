Green Finance: Harmonizing Sustainability with Economic Resilience - Thailand Business News

Green Finance: Harmonizing Sustainability with Economic Resilience - Thailand Business News

The article highlights that while green bonds lower borrowing costs and support sustainable growth in ASEAN+3, they carry inherent risks to financial stability. A key concern is that increased issuance does not guarantee reduced carbon emissions, exposing the sector to potential misuse. This discrepancy underscores the critical threat of greenwashing, where investments may appear environmentally friendly without delivering substantive benefits. Such practices risk eroding investor confidence and undermining the credibility of green finance initiatives if not properly addressed through rigorous verification and transparent reporting standards. Consequently, robust regulatory oversight, strict green taxonomies, and central bank policies are essential to mitigate these risks. By ensuring adherence to genuine sustainable practices, regulators can safeguard asset values and maintain trust, thereby aligning financial stability with actual climate goals and preventing deceptive environmental claims.
