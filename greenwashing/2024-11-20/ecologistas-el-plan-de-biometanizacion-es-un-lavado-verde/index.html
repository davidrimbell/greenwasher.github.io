Ecologistas: "El Plan de Biometanización es un lavado verde"

Ecologistas en Acción denounces the regional biometanization plan as a greenwashing exercise rather than genuine decarbonization. They argue that it is tailored to serve agricultural and energy lobbies, lacking sufficient sustainability guarantees. Critics highlight the absence of limits on the number of plants, which harms small farms and ignores the poor energy recovery ratio. They also demand greater distances from urban areas to prevent the importation of waste from distant locations, fearing negative local impacts. This case is crucial for understanding greenwashing, as it illustrates how ostensibly "green" policies can prioritize corporate interests over environmental integrity. The initiative risks exploiting ecological concepts to legitimize industrial expansion, demonstrating the gap between marketing claims and actual ecological benefits.
