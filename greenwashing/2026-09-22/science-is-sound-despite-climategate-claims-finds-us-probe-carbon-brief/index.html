Science is sound despite Climategate claims, finds US probe - Carbon Brief

The Climategate investigation definitively found no evidence of data manipulation or ethical breaches by climate scientists, validating the integrity of crucial IPCC research. This conclusion directly counters attempts by political skeptics to delegitimize climate science and cut related funding, exposing their efforts as baseless attacks rather than scientific critiques. This case remains highly relevant to greenwashing discussions as it illustrates how manufactured controversies can be weaponized to discredit factual environmental science. By amplifying unverified claims of misconduct, actors attempt to create public doubt, a tactic often mirrored by corporations denying environmental harm to protect their reputations and profits. Ultimately, the thorough exonerations demonstrate that robust institutional oversight can effectively neutralize misinformation campaigns. Recognizing these patterns helps consumers and policymakers distinguish between genuine scientific debate and coordinated disinformation efforts designed to obscure reality and delay necessary environmental action.
