Greenwashing
ES EN

The article highlights the emergence of "greenlash," a significant political and social backlash against climate policies, which has effectively removed climate change from the top tier of political priorities in major economies. This phenomenon illustrates how corporations and politicians can utilize public resistance to stringent environmental regulations as an excuse for inaction, allowing them to maintain business-as-usual practices under the guise of respecting consumer sentiment. This dynamic is a core mechanism of greenwashing, where the narrative of public opposition is manufactured or exaggerated to deflect accountability for environmental harm. Simultaneously, there is a stark contrast between official policy rhetoric and actual market trends, where fossil fuel interests often dominate despite growing renewable energy investments. The convergence of AI-driven energy demand and political support for natural gas reveals a disconnect between stated climate goals and the infrastructure being built, further enabling greenwashing through ambiguous strategies like "all of the above" energy policies. These tactics allow entities to claim progress while continuing to expand fossil fuel dependencies, masking their true environmental impact. Understanding greenlash is crucial for exposing greenwashing because it reveals how misinformation is weaponized to stall genuine sustainability efforts. By framing necessary emissions reductions as economically burdensome or politically radioactive, opponents of climate action create a pretext for maintaining polluting systems. Recognizing this backlash as a tool for delay, rather than a genuine democratic mandate, helps individuals see through the corporate and political narratives that prioritize profit over planetary health.
