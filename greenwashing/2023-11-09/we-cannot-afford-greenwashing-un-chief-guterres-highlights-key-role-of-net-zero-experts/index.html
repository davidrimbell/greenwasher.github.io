'We cannot afford greenwashing': UN Chief Guterres highlights key role of Net-Zero experts

'We cannot afford greenwashing': UN Chief Guterres highlights key role of Net-Zero experts

The UN Secretary-General established a Net-Zero Expert Group to combat the prevalent greenwashing phenomenon, addressing the severe deficit of credibility and confusion surrounding corporate climate pledges. By targeting non-State entities like businesses and investors, the initiative seeks to establish rigorous, transparent standards that prevent fossil fuel interests from exploiting geopolitical crises to maintain high carbon futures. Central to this effort is the demand for ambitious, verifiable commitments that align with environmental integrity and transparency. The group aims to replace vague promises with actionable, accountable measures, ensuring that net-zero targets are not merely marketing tools but genuine pathways to decarbonization. This focus on verification and robust reporting is essential to distinguish sincere climate action from deceptive practices. Ultimately, these recommendations intend to translate high-level standards into concrete international and national regulations. By creating a unified framework for assessing and implementing net-zero goals, the UN hopes to eliminate fake movers and accelerate global emissions reductions. This structural clarity is vital for maintaining the urgency required to keep the 1.5-degree climate target alive and ensuring genuine progress over performative activism.
