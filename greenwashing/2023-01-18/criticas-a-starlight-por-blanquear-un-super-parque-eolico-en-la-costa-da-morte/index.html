Críticas a Starlight por «blanquear» un «súper parque» eólico en la Costa da Morte

Críticas a Starlight por «blanquear» un «súper parque» eólico en la Costa da Morte

Arco Iris denounces Starlight’s certification of a large-scale wind farm as sustainable, arguing that it causes severe light pollution, which disrupts astronomical observations and harms biodiversity. The environmental group considers this approval a cynical greenwashing tactic aimed at obscuring the ecological and visual damage caused by the industrial complex. This case highlights how certifications can mislead the public by labeling destructive energy projects as eco-friendly, thereby concealing their true negative impact on nature and the night sky.
