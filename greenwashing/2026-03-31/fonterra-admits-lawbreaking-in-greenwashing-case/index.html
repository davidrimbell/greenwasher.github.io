Fonterra Admits Lawbreaking in Greenwashing Case

Fonterra Admits Lawbreaking in Greenwashing Case

New Zealand dairy giant Fonterra admitted its Anchor butter packaging violated fair trade laws, acknowledging that claims of being “100% New Zealand grass-fed” misled consumers. The reality involves the use of imported palm kernel expeller, a feed linked to rainforest destruction in Southeast Asia, highlighting the deceptive nature of the product’s environmental branding. This case exemplifies greenwashing, where companies present an eco-friendly image while engaging in practices that harm the environment. Fonterra’s admission underscores how misleading marketing can obscure the true environmental costs of production, particularly when supply chains involve controversial materials like palm-based feed associated with deforestation. The lawsuit, brought by Greenpeace Aotearoa, marks a significant victory against corporate greenwashing. It demonstrates growing public and legal scrutiny of such deceptive practices, signaling that companies can no longer rely on vague environmental claims to sell products without facing accountability.
