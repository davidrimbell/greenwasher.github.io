Greenwashing
ES EN

Major fossil fuel companies heavily lobby for EU hydrogen expansion, promoting it as a climate solution despite producing mostly carbon-intensive grey hydrogen. They manipulate sustainability metrics to classify blue hydrogen as “clean,” even though its carbon capture technology is ineffective and increases overall emissions. This narrative constitutes a coordinated greenwashing scam that misleads policymakers and the public. By exploiting regulatory loopholes, these corporations justify continued reliance on fossil fuels under the guise of environmental progress, masking the true environmental cost. Understanding this dynamic is crucial for exposing greenwashing, as it reveals how industry lobbying distorts sustainability standards. It highlights the danger of accepting industry-defined “clean” labels that ignore actual emissions, urging scrutiny of corporate claims against scientific reality rather than regulatory definitions.
