Current voluntary carbon credit pricing 'misleading', says BP exec « Carbon Pulse

Current voluntary carbon credit pricing 'misleading', says BP exec « Carbon Pulse

BP’s executive claims voluntary carbon credit pricing is misleading. A few major tech buyers pay premiums for small volumes, while the rest of the market struggles with low prices. This disparity distorts the perceived value of nature-based solutions. Such price fragmentation fuels greenwashing concerns by obscuring the true quality of credits. It allows companies to purchase expensive, low-impact offsets for PR benefits rather than supporting widespread, high-integrity removal. This dynamic undermines market integrity, suggesting that high prices do not necessarily correlate with genuine environmental benefit. Consequently, it challenges the credibility of voluntary carbon markets and highlights the need for better pricing transparency to prevent deceptive claims.
