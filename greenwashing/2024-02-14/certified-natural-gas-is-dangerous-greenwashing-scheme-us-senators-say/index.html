"Certified Natural Gas Is ‘Dangerous Greenwashing Scheme’, US Senators Say"

Progressive senators urge federal regulators to label certified natural gas a dangerous greenwashing scheme. They argue these programs enable the fossil fuel industry to justify continued methane expansion under false climate-friendly pretenses. This directly undermines urgent transitions toward renewable energy sources. The legislation calls for stricter enforcement of updated Green Guides to prevent deceptive marketing. By targeting these certification claims, lawmakers aim to stop the industry from obscuring methane’s severe short-term warming impact. This is relevant to greenwashing because it exposes how "low-emission" labels mask environmental harm. It highlights the critical need for regulatory scrutiny to distinguish genuine sustainability from industry spin, protecting consumers from misleading ecological claims.
