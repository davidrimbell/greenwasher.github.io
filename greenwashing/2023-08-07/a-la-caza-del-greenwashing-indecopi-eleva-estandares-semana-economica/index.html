A la caza del greenwashing: Indecopi eleva estándares | Semana Económica

A la caza del greenwashing: Indecopi eleva estándares | Semana Económica

The article outlines the regulatory framework designed to curb misleading environmental claims, highlighting a shift from voluntary guidelines to mandatory legal standards for sustainability disclosures. By establishing stricter definitions for terms such as “eco-friendly” or “carbon neutral,” the legislation aims to eliminate the ambiguity that companies often exploit to exaggerate their environmental benefits. This tightening of regulations is crucial for combating greenwashing, as it empowers consumers and authorities to hold brands accountable, ensuring that sustainability marketing reflects genuine operational practices rather than superficial branding.
