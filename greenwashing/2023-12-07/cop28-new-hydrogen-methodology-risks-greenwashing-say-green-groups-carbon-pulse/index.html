COP28: New hydrogen methodology risks greenwashing, say green groups « Carbon Pulse

A new hydrogen methodology introduced at COP28 raises significant concerns among NGOs that it could enable greenwashing by the fossil fuel industry. This development is crucial to understanding how vague standards might inadvertently legitimize misleading environmental claims. The potential for deception highlights the urgency of rigorous governance in carbon markets. Without strict oversight, such methodologies risk undermining genuine climate action by allowing polluters to exploit loopholes for public relations benefits. Ultimately, this issue underscores the need for transparency and accountability in defining clean energy. It serves as a warning that poorly designed frameworks can facilitate greenwashing, detracting from the urgent goal of reducing global emissions effectively.
