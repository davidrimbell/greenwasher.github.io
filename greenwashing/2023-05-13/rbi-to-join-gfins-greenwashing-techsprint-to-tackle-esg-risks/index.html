RBI to join GFIN’s Greenwashing TechSprint to tackle ESG risks

The Reserve Bank of India joins global regulators in the GFIN Greenwashing TechSprint to develop tools mitigating greenwashing risks. This initiative unites diverse stakeholders to address critical challenges in sustainable finance through collaborative innovation. By fostering international cooperation, the program aims to create effective solutions for the financial services sector. The project highlights the growing regulatory focus on preventing misleading environmental claims within ESG frameworks. This effort is relevant to greenwashing as it directly targets the lack of transparency and accountability in sustainable finance. It demonstrates a proactive, multi-jurisdictional approach to establishing stricter standards and reducing deceptive marketing practices in the green economy.
