PE: Sí a la sostenibilidad y durabilidad y NO al ecopostureo

The European Parliament has approved a landmark directive designed to empower consumers with greater decision-making power during the ecological transition. This legislative measure prioritizes sustainability and durability, explicitly rejecting superficial marketing tactics. By requiring products to be genuinely long-lasting and environmentally responsible, the initiative aims to align corporate practices with real ecological needs rather than fleeting trends. At the core of this effort is a strict prohibition on misleading environmental claims. General assertions such as “eco-friendly” or “carbon neutral” are banned unless supported by specific, verifiable evidence, effectively targeting greenwashing strategies that rely on vague terminology. The proposal also tackles manipulative practices, such as limiting product functionality through incompatible accessories or falsely advertising longevity, ensuring that marketing accurately reflects actual product performance and transparency. Furthermore, the directive combats planned obsolescence by enforcing repairability standards and clearer warranty labeling. This approach incentivizes manufacturers to design durable goods while providing consumers with honest information about repair options and legal guarantees. This legislation is relevant to greenwashing as it establishes rigorous, enforceable criteria for environmental marketing, shifting the burden of proof from consumers to corporations and dismantling the deceptive narratives that often mask unsustainable business models.
