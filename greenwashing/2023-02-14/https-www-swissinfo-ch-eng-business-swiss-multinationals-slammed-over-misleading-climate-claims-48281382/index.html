Greenwashing
ES EN

The article highlights how major corporations, including Swiss giants Nestlé and Holcim, engage in greenwashing by relying on future carbon offsetting rather than immediate emission cuts. This strategy is fundamentally flawed, as the volume of necessary compensation would require multiple Earths, exposing the mathematical impossibility of their claims. These misleading net-zero pledges distract from the urgent need for actual, economy-wide reductions by 2030. By promising to balance future emissions through mechanisms like tree planting, companies create an illusion of sustainability while failing to align with scientific requirements for climate stability. This case is relevant to greenwashing because it reveals a systemic industry-wide failure to prioritize genuine decarbonization. It demonstrates how vague targets and inadequate transparency allow firms to market environmentally friendly images without delivering the substantive changes required to combat climate change effectively.
