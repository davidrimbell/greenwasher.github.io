Vaisala Seeks to Remove Greenwashing from Carbon Capture with a New Measurement Solution

Vaisala Seeks to Remove Greenwashing from Carbon Capture with a New Measurement Solution

The article introduces Vaisala’s new MGP241 instrument, designed to provide precise, real-time measurement of carbon dioxide and humidity for Carbon Capture, Utilization, and Storage (CCUS) projects. By enabling constant and accurate data collection, this technology aims to eliminate guesswork and "sugarcoating" of results, ensuring that claimed decarbonization efforts are grounded in verified facts rather than opaque estimates. This focus on transparency is critical because CCUS is vital for hard-to-abate industries like cement and energy, which face intense regulatory and public pressure to reduce emissions. However, many current projects are behind schedule or failing to deliver promised results. Accurate measurement builds trust among stakeholders and investors, serving as the foundation for the investment case and proving genuine commitment to sustainability amidst widespread skepticism about the technology’s efficacy. The relevance to greenwashing lies in the instrument’s ability to prevent the exaggeration of environmental benefits. Without reliable data, companies risk misleading the public and regulators about their climate impact. By making measurement transparent, efficient, and significantly cheaper, this tool ensures that carbon reduction claims are substantiated, thereby combating the deceptive practices often associated with greenwashing and fostering accountability in the transition to a low-carbon economy.
