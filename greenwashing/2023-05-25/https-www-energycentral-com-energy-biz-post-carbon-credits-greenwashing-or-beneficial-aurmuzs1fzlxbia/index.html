Greenwashing
ES EN

Carbon credits simultaneously enable genuine environmental investment and serve as greenwashing tools for companies avoiding their own emission reductions. This duality highlights how the practice can mask inaction, allowing corporations to prioritize public relations over substantive climate strategy.
