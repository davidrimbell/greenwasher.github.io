Amend Companies Act to bolster ESG initiatives, set up oversight body: House panel tells MCA

This article is critical to understanding greenwashing because it highlights how vague corporate responsibilities allow companies to hide misleading environmental claims. The committee insists that ESG objectives must become a non-negotiable legal duty for directors, shifting the focus from superficial disclosure to genuine accountability. A dedicated oversight body with forensic expertise is proposed to combat fraudulent ESG claims. By enforcing strict penalties and providing sector-specific guidelines, regulators can dismantle the greenwashing tactics that currently exploit weak enforcement mechanisms. Furthermore, the report demands transparency in corporate social responsibility spending. By requiring evidence-based impact reports rather than just expenditure data, the framework aims to prevent companies from using CSR as a facade for ethical misconduct, ensuring that sustainability efforts are verifiable rather than performative.
