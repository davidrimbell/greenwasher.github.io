Greenwashing
ES EN

Global markets are currently fractured by competing forces, where surging AI-driven technology stocks clash with heightened macroeconomic anxiety. Investors are navigating the tension between robust tech momentum and persistent risks stemming from elevated interest rates, volatile oil prices, and deep geopolitical instability. Geopolitical tensions, particularly between the U.S. and Iran, have introduced severe uncertainty into energy markets. Threats of escalation and potential disruptions to critical shipping lanes are driving up fuel costs and inflation fears, while political maneuvering further complicates the economic outlook for global trade and energy security. This article highlights greenwashing risks by exposing how critical infrastructure constraints, such as nuclear power shortages for data centers, intersect with political rhetoric and energy volatility. It illustrates how narratives around technological progress and national security often obscure the tangible environmental impacts and supply chain fragilities underlying the current energy transition.
