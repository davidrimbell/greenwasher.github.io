Low-carbon transitional sectors prone to increased greenwashing risks, research finds - edie

Low-carbon transitional sectors prone to increased greenwashing risks, research finds - edie

The number of companies exposed to greenwashing risks is rising significantly, with a dramatic surge in incidents over the past five years. This trend highlights a critical failure in corporate environmental claims, where transparency and accuracy are increasingly lacking across global markets. Shifts in sector exposure reveal that scrutiny is moving from traditional polluters toward industries transitioning to the low-carbon economy. Notably, biodiversity concerns have overtaken climate issues as the primary environmental risk, signaling a complex evolution in how sustainability failures are categorized and perceived by stakeholders. This report is vital to understanding greenwashing because it underscores the urgency for financial institutions to demand high-quality, auditable data. As investors grapple with declining rating quality and widespread consumer skepticism, the ability to distinguish credible transition efforts from deceptive marketing becomes essential for maintaining trust and ensuring capital supports genuine sustainability.
