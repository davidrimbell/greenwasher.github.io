Greenwashing
ES EN

Japan’s heavy reliance on incinerating plastic waste contradicts its carbon neutrality goals, as thermal recycling significantly increases greenhouse gas emissions. This highlights how superficial waste management strategies can mask substantial contributions to global warming, a core aspect of greenwashing where environmental claims outpace actual ecological benefits. Switching to recycled or bioplastics offers a viable alternative, balancing economic competitiveness with reduced carbon footprints. By evaluating these materials through comprehensive environmental and quality metrics, stakeholders can identify genuinely sustainable supply chain shifts rather than relying on misleading disposal narratives. This study underscores the urgent need for transparent, science-based plastic policies. It reveals that without shifting away from carbon-intensive disposal methods, current waste strategies serve as greenwashing, failing to address the sector’s significant role in climate change despite corporate sustainability pledges.
