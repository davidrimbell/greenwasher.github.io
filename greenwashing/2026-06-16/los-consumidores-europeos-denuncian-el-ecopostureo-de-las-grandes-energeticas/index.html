Los consumidores europeos denuncian el ecopostureo de las grandes energéticas

Researchers and consumer associations have filed complaints against various energy companies for making misleading environmental claims that deceive the public. The analysis reveals that these firms use generic messaging and sustainability symbols, alongside advertising natural gas as a clean fuel, to mask their ongoing reliance on fossil fuels. Such practices distort the true perception of the transition toward renewable energy. Criticism also focuses on the questionable use of carbon offsets and long-term climate neutrality pledges. These strategies create a false impression of immediate progress, while in reality, oil and gas extraction and marketing activities continue. European authorities are already restricting such messaging, as it does not eliminate direct emissions and can undermine genuine decarbonization efforts. This issue is crucial to understanding greenwashing, as it erodes consumer trust in the energy transition. With a significant majority of citizens keen on reducing their environmental footprint, the lack of clarity prevents them from choosing truly sustainable providers. Combating these practices not only protects consumers but is also essential for accelerating a transparent and credible energy transition.
