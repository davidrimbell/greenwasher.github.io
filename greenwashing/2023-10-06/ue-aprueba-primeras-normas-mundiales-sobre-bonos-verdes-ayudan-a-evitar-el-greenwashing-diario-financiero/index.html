UE aprueba "primeras normas mundiales" sobre bonos verdes: ayudan a evitar el greenwashing | Diario Financiero

The European Parliament has established new voluntary standards for “European green bonds” to combat greenwashing by ensuring that financial labels accurately reflect genuine sustainability. This regulation requires issuers to disclose how the funds are used and to verify that at least 85% of the proceeds support activities aligned with the EU’s sustainable taxonomy. By mandating transparency and external verification, the rules aim to prevent misleading climate claims while providing investors with reliable tools to identify truly eco-friendly investments. This clarity safeguards market integrity and encourages capital flows toward legitimate environmental projects. This development is crucial for raising awareness about greenwashing, as it introduces a global first in regulating green finance labels. It demonstrates how legislative oversight can enforce accountability, thereby reducing deceptive marketing practices and fostering trust in the transition toward a low-carbon economy.
