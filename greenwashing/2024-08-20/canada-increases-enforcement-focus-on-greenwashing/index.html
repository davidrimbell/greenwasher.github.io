Canada Increases Enforcement Focus on Greenwashing

Canada updated its Competition Act to explicitly prohibit greenwashing by mandating robust testing and internationally recognized substantiation for environmental claims. This shift establishes a clear legal framework for enforcement, moving beyond vague guidelines to concrete regulatory expectations for businesses making ecological assertions. The amendments also facilitate private enforcement through individual litigation, significantly increasing the risk for companies engaging in deceptive marketing practices. By empowering private actors, the law creates additional pressure on corporations to ensure their sustainability narratives are factual and verifiable before public release. This development aligns Canada with global efforts to combat greenwashing, signaling sustained regulatory scrutiny. It underscores the growing importance of authentic environmental communication and warns businesses that unsubstantiated green claims now carry serious legal and financial consequences.
