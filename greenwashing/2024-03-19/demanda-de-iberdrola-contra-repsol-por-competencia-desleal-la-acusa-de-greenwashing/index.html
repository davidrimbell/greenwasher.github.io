Demanda de Iberdrola contra Repsol por competencia desleal: La acusa de 'greenwashing'

Iberdrola has filed a lawsuit against Repsol for unfair competition and misleading advertising, alleging that Repsol engages in greenwashing. The electricity company claims that Repsol promotes sustainability while its products contradict this image, thereby violating consumer protection laws. Repsol denies these accusations, arguing that its strategy offers unique value and that Iberdrola feels threatened by entering a competitive market. This legal battle highlights how companies navigate the tension between traditional operations and eco-friendly marketing claims. This case is crucial for understanding greenwashing because it demonstrates how competitors can legally challenge vague or deceptive sustainability narratives. It underscores the growing scrutiny of corporate environmental messaging and the importance of transparency in building genuine consumer trust.
