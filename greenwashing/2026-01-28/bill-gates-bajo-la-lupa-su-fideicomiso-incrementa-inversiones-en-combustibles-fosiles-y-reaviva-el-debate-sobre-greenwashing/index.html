Bill Gates bajo la lupa: su fideicomiso incrementa inversiones en combustibles fósiles y reaviva el debate sobre greenwashing

The article reveals that Bill Gates’ trust has significantly increased its investments in fossil fuel companies, contradicting his public claims of complete divestment. Despite declaring in 2019 that he had eliminated his direct holdings in the oil and gas sectors, financial data show a substantial rise in exposure to companies such as Chevron, BP, and Shell, reaching record levels in recent years. This discrepancy between environmental rhetoric and investment practice raises doubts about the true consistency of his climate commitments. This situation illustrates the concept of “philanthropic greenwashing,” where the reputation gained through funding climate initiatives obscures the negative impact of maintaining capital in polluting industries. While the foundation supports resilience programs for vulnerable communities, it simultaneously earns returns from companies that exacerbate the climate crisis. This strategy dilutes structural responsibilities, shifting the focus away from the root causes of the problem toward palliative solutions that do not eliminate dependence on fossil fuels. The relevance of this case to greenwashing lies in how elite philanthropy can legitimize the status quo. By normalizing the accumulation of symbolic green capital while maintaining exposure to massive emissions, it perpetuates a model in which environmental harms are socialized and profits are concentrated. This is particularly unjust for communities in the Global South, which disproportionately suffer the effects of global warming without benefiting from fossil fuel returns, thereby questioning the actual ethics and effectiveness of current climate strategies.
