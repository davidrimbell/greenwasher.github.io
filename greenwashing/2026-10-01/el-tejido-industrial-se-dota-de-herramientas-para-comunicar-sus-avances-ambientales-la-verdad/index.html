El tejido industrial se dota de herramientas para comunicar sus avances ambientales | La Verdad

El tejido industrial se dota de herramientas para comunicar sus avances ambientales | La Verdad

The Isaac Peral Foundation has launched a pioneering guide designed to combat greenwashing, a deceptive practice that undermines corporate environmental credibility. Its primary objective is to help companies identify and avoid "eco-posturing," promoting rigorous communication based on verifiable data. This approach aims to protect consumers from vague or exaggerated claims, ensuring that environmental messages are transparent, specific, and backed by concrete evidence. It is crucial to recognize that greenwashing has moved beyond reputational concerns to become a legal risk with direct legal consequences. Emerging regulations, such as the European Directive on Consumer Empowerment, strictly regulate pre- and post-sale communications, penalizing unfair commercial practices. Against this legal backdrop, the article highlights that regulatory pressure can lead to both greater rigidity and "environmental silence," where companies fear communicating their genuine progress due to concerns about making mistakes or facing sanctions, thereby hindering authentic ecological transition. The significance of this text lies in its proposal to transform regulation into an opportunity to professionalize sustainability. By emphasizing that action must precede communication, the guide urges companies to act first, measure afterward, and communicate only what can be demonstrated. This ethical stance not only preserves consumer trust, built over years, but also serves as a practical tool for aligning marketing with environmental reality, avoiding the widespread distrust generated by years of misleading advertising practices.
