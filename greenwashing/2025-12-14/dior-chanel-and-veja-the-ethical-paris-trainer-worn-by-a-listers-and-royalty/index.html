Dior, Chanel and … Veja? The ethical Paris trainer worn by A-listers and royalty

Dior, Chanel and … Veja? The ethical Paris trainer worn by A-listers and royalty

Veja has established itself as a prominent sustainable footwear brand by leveraging transparency and ethical production to appeal to consumers who wish to signal their values through fashion. Unlike many competitors, the company avoids traditional marketing and celebrity endorsements, relying instead on its reputation for fair trade practices and material traceability to build trust and drive global sales among fashion-conscious demographics. Despite the broader industry’s decline in sneaker demand and the prevalence of greenwashing, Veja maintains its position by prioritizing durability and repairability over disposability. The brand critiques the widespread misuse of terms like "recycling," highlighting the technical impossibility of recycling complex shoe compositions. By offering cobbling services to extend product lifespans, Veja challenges the fast fashion model and exposes the superficiality of vague environmental claims prevalent in the market. This article is highly relevant to greenwashing because it illustrates the distinction between authentic sustainability and marketing gimmicks. Veja’s approach demonstrates that true environmental responsibility requires radical transparency in supply chains and a commitment to longevity, rather than relying on misleading buzzwords. By contrasting its verifiable practices with the industry’s tendency to co-opt sustainability language for commercial gain, the piece underscores the difficulty consumers face in identifying genuine ethical brands amidst pervasive greenwashing.
