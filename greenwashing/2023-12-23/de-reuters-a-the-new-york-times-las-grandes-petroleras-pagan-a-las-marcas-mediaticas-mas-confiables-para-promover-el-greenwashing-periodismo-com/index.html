De Reuters a The New York Times, las grandes petroleras pagan a las "marcas mediáticas más confiables" para promover el 'Greenwashing' - Periodismo . com

The report reveals that major media outlets, perceived as reliable, facilitate greenwashing by accepting funding from the fossil fuel industry. This collaboration blurs the lines between independent journalism and sponsored advertising, allowing oil companies to promote self-congratulatory narratives and present ineffective solutions to the climate crisis. This practice distorts public perception and delays the necessary climate action. By confusing editorial content with covert advertising, media outlets undermine public trust and help companies sow doubt about climate science. Journalists sometimes actively participate in producing this content or moderate industry events, compromising their supposed impartiality and turning the press into a vehicle for strategic disinformation. The article’s significance lies in exposing how greenwashing has evolved beyond direct corporate marketing to infiltrate the informational infrastructure. By demonstrating that the most respected news sources monetize the legitimacy of polluting companies, it becomes evident that the fight against climate change also requires reform in the financial and editorial integrity of the media to prevent them from becoming covert allies of climate obstacles.
