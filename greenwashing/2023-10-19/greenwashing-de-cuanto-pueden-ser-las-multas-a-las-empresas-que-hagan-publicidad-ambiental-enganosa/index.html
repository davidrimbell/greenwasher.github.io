Greenwashing: ¿De cuánto pueden ser las multas a las empresas que hagan publicidad ambiental engañosa?

INDECOPI has released a guide on environmental advertising to combat greenwashing, which occurs when companies falsely claim their products are eco-friendly. This initiative addresses the critical issue of deceptive marketing, where vague or unsubstantiated environmental claims mislead consumers about a product's true ecological impact. The agency highlights that a significant portion of such claims globally are often misleading, emphasizing the need for transparency in sustainable commerce. The guide clarifies that greenwashing is punishable under Peruvian law, subjecting companies to severe sanctions and corrective measures. By establishing clear guidelines, the agency aims to reduce transaction costs for businesses while ensuring they possess verifiable proof for their environmental assertions. This framework shifts ambiguous marketing practices into a regulated environment where factual accuracy is mandatory, thereby protecting fair competition and consumer trust. This update is highly relevant to understanding greenwashing as it demonstrates the growing regulatory scrutiny faced by corporations making sustainability claims. It signals a shift from voluntary compliance to enforceable legal standards, showing that vague assertions can lead to substantial financial penalties. The initiative underscores the importance of evidence-based environmental marketing, warning companies that without rigorous verification, their "green" image may result in serious legal and reputational consequences.
