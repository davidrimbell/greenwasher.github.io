Carbon Dioxide Does Not Cause Warming – No Gas Causes Warming - LewRockwell

This article fundamentally rejects the scientific consensus on anthropogenic climate change, asserting that carbon dioxide does not trap heat or cause global warming. The author argues that academic models relying on theoretical gas laws are flawed and disconnected from empirical reality. By contrasting university-based theories with private-sector engineering practices, the text claims that real-world physics proves gases cool rapidly and do not retain thermal energy, thereby invalidating the greenhouse effect theory entirely. The relevance to greenwashing lies in the exposure of potential ideological fraud within environmental policy advocacy. If the core scientific premise is indeed false, as the author contends, then net-zero policies and carbon reduction mandates are based on deception rather than fact. This perspective suggests that corporations and governments may be promoting environmental initiatives under a false pretext, potentially to secure funding or exert control, which constitutes a severe form of greenwashing where sustainability claims are built on fabricated scientific narratives. Consequently, the article implies that current climate regulations cause unnecessary economic harm while addressing a non-existent problem. It urges a shift toward empirical testing over peer-reviewed theory, suggesting that the widespread acceptance of climate change serves as a mechanism for imposing destructive laws rather than protecting the environment. Ultimately, it frames the climate movement as a misguided effort driven by financial incentives and academic dogma, warning readers to scrutinize the foundational validity of all environmental claims.
