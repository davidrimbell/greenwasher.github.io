Nace TrueGreen, una IA capaz de optimizar el diagnóstico de “greenwashing” en un 90%

The article underscores the urgent need for brands to ensure that their sustainability communications are authentic and verifiable in order to avoid greenwashing—a challenge exacerbated by stricter European regulations such as the Green Claims Directive. This regulatory shift aims to protect consumers and ensure fair competition by eliminating misleading environmental claims, thereby establishing a context in which transparency is no longer optional but a legal requirement. To address this issue, a new AI-driven tool has been introduced to analyze digital assets and sustainability messages in real time against a comprehensive set of legal criteria. By automating the diagnostic process, the technology significantly reduces the time and resources required for compliance checks, enabling companies to quickly identify risks and implement corrective actions—such as modifying content or obtaining relevant certifications—before facing penalties. This development is highly relevant to the issue of greenwashing, as it represents a technological evolution from mere self-regulation to data-driven compliance. It highlights that preventing deceptive practices now requires robust, objective verification mechanisms to safeguard corporate reputation and build genuine trust with consumers who demand accuracy in environmental claims.
