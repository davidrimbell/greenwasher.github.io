Greenwashing
ES EN

ESG: ser o aparentar - Inese

European authorities highlight greenwashing as a damaging practice in the financial sector, warning that it misleads consumers and investors by concealing products that are misaligned with their sustainability preferences. This deception harms not only buyers but also insurance providers, whose reputations suffer significant damage when discrepancies between claims and reality emerge. The issue is particularly critical because firms risk appearing compliant while lacking substantive alignment, either through intentional deception or unintentional oversight. The growing consumer demand for genuine ESG integration makes this transparency essential, forcing companies to move beyond superficial marketing toward authentic operational changes. This article underscores the urgent need for professional training to navigate complex regulations and meet stakeholder expectations. By emphasizing substance over appearance, institutions can mitigate reputational risks and truly integrate sustainability into their core business models, ensuring that green claims accurately reflect their environmental and social impact.
