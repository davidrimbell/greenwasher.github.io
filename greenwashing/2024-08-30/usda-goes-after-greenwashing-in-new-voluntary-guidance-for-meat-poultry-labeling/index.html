USDA goes after ‘greenwashing’ in new voluntary guidance for meat, poultry labeling

USDA goes after ‘greenwashing’ in new voluntary guidance for meat, poultry labeling

The USDA demands stronger evidence for environmental and animal welfare claims on meat and poultry labels to ensure consumer trust and fair market competition. This move aims to prevent misleading marketing that could allow products to benefit from unjustified premium pricing. Such scrutiny is vital for combating greenwashing, as vague claims often obscure the true environmental impact of agricultural products. Without rigorous verification, companies can exploit eco-friendly narratives to gain market advantages while misleading the public. However, these regulations face backlash from environmental groups who argue that certain labels, like "climate-friendly beef," fundamentally contradict scientific realities regarding cattle emissions. This tension highlights the difficulty in defining genuine sustainability within industries heavily associated with significant greenhouse gas output.
