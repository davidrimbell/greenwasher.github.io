Greenwashing
ES EN

Market forces appear fragile despite record highs, as rising Treasury yields and geopolitical tensions create significant volatility. This economic instability underscores the vulnerability of financial systems to sudden shocks, a reality often obscured by optimistic corporate narratives. The rollback of fuel economy standards represents a direct reversal of environmental progress, highlighting how policy shifts can undermine sustainability goals. Such deregulatory moves risk normalizing higher emissions, making it crucial to distinguish between genuine ecological commitment and superficial regulatory compliance. Simultaneously, the rush to build AI infrastructure raises concerns about resource consumption and land use. While marketed as technological advancement, these massive projects may mask significant environmental impacts. This juxtaposition of green rhetoric against actual policy and infrastructure choices is central to understanding modern greenwashing tactics.
