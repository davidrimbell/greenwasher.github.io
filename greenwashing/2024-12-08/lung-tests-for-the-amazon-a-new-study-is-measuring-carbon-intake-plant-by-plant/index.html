Scientists are now moving beyond satellite imagery to conduct precise, on-ground measurements to determine the exact state of this ecological tipping point. The findings are particularly relevant to greenwashing because they directly impact the integrity of carbon credit markets. This revelation challenges the narratives often used by corporations and governments to mask their emissions, exposing potential inaccuracies in sustainability claims that rely on outdated or exaggerated models of forest health. Ultimately, accurate data is essential for updating climate prediction models and ensuring transparency in environmental accountability.
