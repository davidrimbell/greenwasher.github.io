La Generación Z castiga el 'greenwashing' y exige a las marcas coherencia

The article highlights that Generation Z has created a new reputational battlefield for brands by demanding transparency and proof regarding environmental claims. Unlike previous demographics, these young consumers actively evaluate corporate behavior and distinguish between genuine commitment and mere marketing, rejecting inconsistent or empty messages. This zero-tolerance attitude is directly relevant to greenwashing, as the gap between rhetoric and practice leads to severe penalties for companies. Research indicates that the industry is moving beyond vague sustainability narratives, since Generation Z demands verifiable actions and concrete results. Consequently, brands attempting to disguise poor environmental practices face significant reputational risks, as this cohort builds its loyalty based on authentic ecological impact rather than superficial advertising. Geographical and gender differences further shape these perceptions, with European consumers showing higher skepticism compared to those in Latin America. Ultimately, the study underscores that while tolerance for greenwashing is at an absolute minimum, there is strong recognition for authentic effort. This dynamic forces corporations to align their internal operations with their public communications to maintain trust and reputation among increasingly critical stakeholders.
