UN chief seeks urgent action against global warming, urges countries to

UN chief seeks urgent action against global warming, urges countries to

UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres explicitly condemned greenwashing alongside climate mis- and disinformation, urging global leaders to combat these deceptive practices during urgent climate action talks. By grouping greenwashing with harassment and falsehoods, the address highlights how corporate or political obfuscation undermines legitimate environmental efforts. This stance underscores that misleading claims about sustainability are not merely marketing tactics but active barriers to implementing the science-based solutions required to mitigate existential planetary risks. The report emphasizes that combating such deception is vital for ensuring the integrity of early-warning systems and climate finance. As governments struggle to deliver on funding promises and adaptation goals, false narratives can distract from the need for robust infrastructure and policy changes. Protecting the flow of information is therefore essential for maintaining public trust and securing the resources necessary to protect vulnerable communities from increasingly severe weather events. This context is highly relevant to greenwashing because it positions deceptive environmental marketing as a direct threat to global safety and climate justice. When entities obscure the true scale of warming or the efficacy of renewable energy, they delay critical interventions. Recognizing greenwashing as a dangerous form of disinformation reinforces the need for strict transparency, ensuring that climate actions are driven by factual science rather than profitable but misleading narratives.
