Greenwashing
ES EN

Increasing scrutiny over false environmental claims drives textile companies toward greenhushing. By hiding genuine sustainability achievements, brands avoid criticism regarding their progress, creating a defensive posture that stifles transparency and open dialogue about environmental efforts. This phenomenon is exacerbated by severe legal consequences, as illustrated by recent lawsuits against a major fashion brand for misleading recycling claims. These cases demonstrate that the fear of being caught in greenwashing accusations pushes corporations to under-report positive impacts rather than risk public backlash or litigation. This trend is highly relevant to greenwashing because it obscures the truth about corporate environmental responsibility. When companies silence their successes to avoid scrutiny, it becomes difficult for stakeholders to distinguish between genuine progress and actual deception, ultimately hindering the industry’s overall sustainability advancement.
