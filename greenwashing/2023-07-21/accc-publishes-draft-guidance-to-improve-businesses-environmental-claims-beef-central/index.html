ACCC publishes draft guidance to improve businesses' environmental claims - Beef Central

The ACCC has released draft guidance to combat greenwashing, addressing a recent sweep that revealed over half of surveyed businesses were making potentially misleading environmental claims. This initiative aims to protect consumer trust and ensure market fairness, as deceptive marketing allows dishonest firms to charge premium prices while unfairly disadvantaging companies investing in genuine sustainable practices. Central to the new framework are eight practical principles encouraging transparency and evidence-based marketing. Businesses are urged to ensure all claims are accurate, clear, and substantiable, aligning their operations with Australian Consumer Law obligations. The guidance emphasizes that if a company cannot verify an environmental benefit, it should refrain from making the claim entirely to avoid misleading the public. This update is crucial for understanding greenwashing because it shifts the regulatory focus from merely identifying violations to providing clear, preventive standards for compliance. By soliciting feedback from both businesses and consumers, the ACCC aims to foster an environment where legitimate sustainability efforts are distinguished from exaggerated marketing, thereby reducing ambiguity and enhancing accountability in the marketplace.
