New carbon accounting rules for cos target 'greenwashing' - ET EnergyWorld

New global reporting standards aim to eliminate corporate greenwashing by establishing reliable, uniform metrics for greenhouse gas emissions. Previously, inconsistent data and a lack of common guidelines allowed firms to exaggerate their environmental credentials, misleading stakeholders. The International Sustainability Standards Board introduces these rules to create a shared language for disclosing climate risks. By ensuring transparent and accurate sustainability information, the standards seek to rebuild trust in financial markets and support more informed investment decisions. This initiative is crucial for combating greenwashing because it removes the ambiguity companies previously exploited. Mandatory or voluntary adoption of these rigorous norms ensures that climate claims are substantiated by comparable, high-quality data rather than vague marketing.
