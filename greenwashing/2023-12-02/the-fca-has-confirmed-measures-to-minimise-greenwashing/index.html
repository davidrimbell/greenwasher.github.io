The FCA has confirmed measures to minimise greenwashing

The UK financial regulator is implementing strict new rules to combat greenwashing, aiming to restore consumer trust in sustainable investing. By prohibiting misleading environmental claims and enforcing standardized product labels, the FCA ensures that sustainability descriptors accurately reflect a fund’s actual impact. This regulatory shift addresses the widespread confusion caused by inconsistent terminology like “green” or “ESG.” The measures guarantee that marketing materials are fair and transparent, preventing firms from exaggerating their positive contributions to society or the environment to attract capital. These changes are vital for greenwashing prevention, as they shift responsibility from voluntary disclosure to enforced accountability. By aligning market practices with genuine sustainability goals, the regime protects investors from deception while strengthening the integrity of the broader sustainable finance ecosystem.
