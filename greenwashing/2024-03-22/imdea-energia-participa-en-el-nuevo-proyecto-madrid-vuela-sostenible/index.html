Imdea Energía participa en el nuevo proyecto Madrid Vuela Sostenible

Repsol leads a public-private consortium to advance Sustainable Aviation Fuels (SAF), aiming to decarbonize air travel through innovative production methods. By leveraging scientific research and industrial partnerships, the alliance seeks to transform various waste streams into high-quality biofuels, highlighting a strategic shift toward circular economy solutions in the mobility sector. The initiative underscores the industry’s reliance on next-generation biofuels to claim environmental credentials without fundamentally altering travel habits. This approach allows major energy companies to promote green alternatives while continuing to operate fossil fuel infrastructure, raising questions about whether these efforts constitute a genuine ecological transition or merely extend the lifespan of carbon-intensive models through technological tweaks. This case is highly relevant to greenwashing because it illustrates how corporations can market specific sustainable products to overshadow their broader environmental impacts. By emphasizing isolated innovations like SAF, companies can project an image of comprehensive climate responsibility, potentially misleading stakeholders about the true scale of their carbon footprint and the systemic changes required for real sustainability.
