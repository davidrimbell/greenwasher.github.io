EU lawmakers vote to ban 'greenwashing' and penalise companies who make fake claims

EU lawmakers vote to ban 'greenwashing' and penalise companies who make fake claims

The European Parliament advances the Green Claims Directive to eliminate greenwashing by mandating scientific verification and pre-approval for all environmental marketing claims. This regulatory shift aims to protect consumers and ensure transparency by holding companies accountable for misleading sustainability assertions. Enforcement includes severe penalties for non-compliance, such as fines and procurement bans, compelling businesses to adopt genuine sustainable practices rather than relying on deceptive advertising tactics. The measure directly addresses the widespread prevalence of unsubstantiated environmental claims across industries. This legislation is highly relevant to greenwashing efforts as it establishes a strict legal framework requiring third-party verification. By forcing companies to substantiate their eco-friendly statements, the directive removes the ambiguity that often enables misleading environmental marketing.
