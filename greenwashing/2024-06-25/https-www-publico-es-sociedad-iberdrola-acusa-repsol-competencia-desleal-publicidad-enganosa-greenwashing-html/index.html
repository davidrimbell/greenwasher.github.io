Greenwashing
EN ES

Iberdrola has filed a lawsuit for unfair competition against Repsol, accusing its rival of greenwashing by promoting an image of sustainability that contradicts its current reliance on fossil fuels. The utility company considers Repsol’s advertising campaigns misleading, as they prioritize the use of traditional fuels while portraying the company as an ecological leader—a practice Iberdrola labels “eco-posturing” that distorts the market and confuses consumers. The conflict highlights the competitive tension between different energy transition models: Repsol defends its multi-energy strategy, whereas Iberdrola advocates for electrification as the sole solution. Iberdrola is seeking an injunction to halt these practices and the destruction of advertising materials, arguing that such actions erode trust in genuine sustainability initiatives and undermine the efforts of companies genuinely committed to decarbonization. This dispute is relevant to the study of greenwashing because it exemplifies how companies use legal accusations to delegitimize competitors’ environmental narratives. It serves as a tangible case of the tensions arising from the Directive on empowering consumers for the green transition, demonstrating the need to regulate business practices that mislead the public about a company’s actual environmental commitment.
