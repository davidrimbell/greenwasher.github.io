Consumo se suma a autoridades de 35 países para luchar contra el 'ecopostureo' de las aerolíneas - Nacional - murcia.com

Consumo se suma a autoridades de 35 países para luchar contra el 'ecopostureo' de las aerolíneas - Nacional - murcia.com

Governments are taking decisive action against the pervasive practice of greenwashing, or eco-posturing, in the aviation industry. By issuing a joint letter through an international consumer protection network, authorities aim to combat misleading communications that project an environmentally friendly image not supported by reality. This collaborative effort underscores the urgent need to ensure that corporate statements regarding environmental impact are truthful, clear, and precise. The core directive requires airlines to substantiate all environmental claims with robust scientific evidence. Companies are advised to avoid vague terminology such as "sustainable" or "ecological," which often mislead consumers, and to refrain from exaggerating minor changes as significant benefits. Furthermore, communications must clearly disclose the limitations and uncertainties of carbon offsetting systems, rather than highlighting uncertain future aspirations or vague commitments that do not reflect current actions. This initiative is highly relevant to greenwashing because it addresses the gap between marketing rhetoric and actual environmental performance. It highlights how the aviation sector, often criticized for high emissions, risks manipulating consumer perceptions through ambiguous language regarding sustainable fuels and net-zero goals. By enforcing stricter transparency, regulators seek to protect consumers from deception and ensure that economic decisions are based on accurate data rather than fabricated ecological virtues.
