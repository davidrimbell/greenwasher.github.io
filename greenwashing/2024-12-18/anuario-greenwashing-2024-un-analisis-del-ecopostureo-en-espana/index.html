Anuario Greenwashing 2024: un análisis del ecopostureo en España

Anuario Greenwashing 2024: un análisis del ecopostureo en España

The second edition of the Greenwashing Yearbook identifies a significant decline in greenwashing, marking a pivotal shift in corporate environmental communication. This trend suggests that the era of blatant "ecoposturing" is waning as regulatory pressure and public skepticism increase. Consequently, the landscape is evolving from overt deception to more subtle forms of misinformation, necessitating a deeper understanding of how companies are adapting their strategies to maintain legitimacy without substantial ecological improvements. A key development is the rise of "greenhushing," where firms now understate their sustainability efforts to avoid scrutiny and legal repercussions. Simultaneously, there has been a surge in formal legal complaints against major corporations, particularly in the energy and food sectors. This judicial push indicates that traditional self-regulation is no longer sufficient, and consumers are increasingly empowered to hold entities accountable through legal channels rather than just consumer boycotts. This publication is relevant to greenwashing because it highlights the transition from awareness to enforcement, demonstrating that sustainable communication is becoming a legally risky endeavor. By analyzing both the decline in overt greenwashing and the emergence of new deceptive tactics like greenhushing, the work provides a critical roadmap for distinguishing between genuine corporate responsibility and strategic PR maneuvers. It underscores that while progress has been made, the fight for true sustainability requires continuous vigilance against evolving corporate narratives.
