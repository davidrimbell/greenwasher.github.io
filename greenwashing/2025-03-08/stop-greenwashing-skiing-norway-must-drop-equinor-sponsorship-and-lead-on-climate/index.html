Stop Greenwashing Skiing: Norway Must Drop Equinor Sponsorship and Lead on Climate

Stop Greenwashing Skiing: Norway Must Drop Equinor Sponsorship and Lead on Climate

The article argues that Norway is engaging in blatant greenwashing by allowing Equinor, a major fossil fuel producer, to sponsor the 2025 World Ski Championships while simultaneously expanding domestic oil and gas drilling. This contradiction highlights how the industry uses high-profile cultural events to improve its public image, effectively hijacking the narrative of sustainability to delay necessary climate action. It is a superficial display of eco-consciousness that masks the reality of continued environmental destruction. This sponsorship strategy is particularly insidious because it leverages the visible threat of climate change against winter sports to further distract from the root cause. While athletes and locals warn that rising temperatures are shrinking snow seasons, the fossil fuel industry continues to profit from the crisis. By presenting themselves as partners in climate solutions while doubling down on extraction, companies like Equinor create a false sense of progress, undermining genuine efforts to transition to renewable energy sources. Ultimately, the piece calls for a rejection of this hypocrisy, urging a commitment to a Fossil Fuel Non-Proliferation Treaty. It emphasizes that true climate leadership requires halting the expansion of fossil fuels rather than funding it through sovereign wealth. The author concludes that individuals and athletes must actively demand the removal of fossil fuel sponsors to ensure that global events do not serve as platforms for industry propaganda.
