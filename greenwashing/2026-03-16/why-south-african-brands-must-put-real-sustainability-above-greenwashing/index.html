Why South African brands must put real sustainability above greenwashing

The beauty industry faces a critical challenge in combating greenwashing, where brands misrepresent their environmental impact. As regulatory scrutiny tightens in Europe, South African companies must prioritize transparency and accountability over misleading marketing claims. True sustainability requires aligning language with actual production realities, avoiding deceptive labels like "eco-friendly" when harmful materials remain in use. Honesty is essential, as no brand can claim absolute perfection or zero environmental footprint. Instead of striving for unattainable ideals, businesses should focus on measurable reductions in their ecological impact and openly communicate their limitations. This approach shifts the narrative from bold, often false promises to genuine efforts to minimize harm based on a company’s specific capabilities and resources. Furthermore, brands bear the responsibility to educate consumers, enabling them to make informed choices amidst pervasive marketing. The future of sustainable beauty depends on regenerative practices and clear communication about what is and isn’t being done. Ultimately, transparency about ongoing progress rather than static perfection is the only way to build trust and genuinely address the greenwashing crisis.
