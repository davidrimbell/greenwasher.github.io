'Vague' net zero rules threaten climate targets, scientists warn - Times of India

Scientists warn that counting forest carbon absorption in net zero plans creates vague rules, potentially accelerating global warming beyond expectations. This oversight allows nations to appear compliant while missing crucial climate targets, highlighting a critical loophole in current environmental accounting practices. Such ambiguity exemplifies greenwashing by masking true emissions and undermining the integrity of global climate commitments through deceptive reporting methods.
