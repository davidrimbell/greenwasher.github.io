New Report Provides An In-Depth Look at Western Fossil Fuel Investments in Azerbaijan -

New Report Provides An In-Depth Look at Western Fossil Fuel Investments in Azerbaijan -

The report reveals how Western financial institutions and corporations fund Azerbaijan’s fossil fuel expansion, directly supporting an authoritarian regime. Despite public commitments to sustainability and human rights, these investors neglect rigorous social and environmental standards in practice. This hypocrisy allows lucrative energy projects to proceed while ignoring severe rights abuses and environmental degradation. At COP29, Azerbaijan leverages the global climate summit primarily as an investment platform rather than a venue for genuine emission reductions. The government plans to increase oil and gas production, contradicting global climate goals. Meanwhile, heavy lobbying by fossil fuel interests overshadows the silence of independent civil society members who face detention or house arrest for opposing these projects. This case is crucial for understanding greenwashing, as it demonstrates how clean energy branding masks continued support for polluting industries. It exposes the disconnect between corporate sustainability rhetoric and actual operations in repressive regimes. The findings urge investors and policymakers to enforce stricter accountability, highlighting how financial complicity undermines both human rights and legitimate climate action.
