'Vague' net zero targets could push global warming past 2°C: experts

Relying on natural carbon sinks to offset fossil fuel use creates vague net zero policies, risking temperatures exceeding safe limits. This approach falsely suggests progress while allowing continued emissions, undermining genuine climate action. Experts warn that such strategies mislead the public into believing the world is on track for 1.5°C warming. In reality, this flawed logic may push global temperatures beyond 2°C, exacerbating the climate crisis through deceptive accounting methods. This highlights a critical greenwashing tactic: prioritizing appearance over effective action. Without geological storage, reliance on natural sinks constitutes greenwashing, as it masks ongoing pollution rather than eliminating it. Clear regulations are essential to prevent such deceptive practices from hindering real climate solutions.
