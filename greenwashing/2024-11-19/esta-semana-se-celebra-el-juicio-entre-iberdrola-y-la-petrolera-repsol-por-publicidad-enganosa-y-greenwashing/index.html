Esta semana se celebra el Juicio entre Iberdrola y la petrolera Repsol por publicidad engañosa y 'greenwashing'

Esta semana se celebra el Juicio entre Iberdrola y la petrolera Repsol por publicidad engañosa y 'greenwashing'

The lawsuit between Iberdrola and Repsol illustrates the growing regulatory tension in the Spanish energy sector. Iberdrola accuses Repsol of greenwashing, claiming that its marketing promises sustainability while continuing to promote fossil fuels, which would constitute misleading advertising. The significance of this case lies in how companies use green narratives to cover up controversial activities. It represents a legal precedent where accusations of environmental deception challenge traditional corporate strategies, forcing greater transparency about the true environmental impact. Ultimately, the conflict signals the end of relative impunity in corporate communications. As the market increasingly values the energy transition, legal disputes over the accuracy of eco-labels become crucial for establishing clear standards and protecting consumers from green misinformation.
