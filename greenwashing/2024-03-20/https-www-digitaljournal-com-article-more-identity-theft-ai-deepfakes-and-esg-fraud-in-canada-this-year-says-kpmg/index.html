This content is relevant to greenwashing because it frames ESG misrepresentation not just as a marketing failure, but as active fraud. By classifying false sustainability claims alongside cyber threats, the article underscores that greenwashing can involve deliberate deception and financial misconduct. Consequently, it warns that organizations must implement stringent anti-fraud measures to ensure authentic ESG compliance and maintain integrity with partners and investors.
