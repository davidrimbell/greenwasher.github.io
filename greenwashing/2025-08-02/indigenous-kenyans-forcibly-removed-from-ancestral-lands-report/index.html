Indigenous Kenyans Forcibly Removed from Ancestral Lands - Report

Conservation groups in Kenya face allegations that wildlife and carbon offset projects justify the violent eviction of Indigenous communities. These entities exploit environmental narratives to seize ancestral lands, masking human rights abuses under the guise of ecological stewardship. This case highlights how greenwashing enables displacement, revealing the dangerous intersection between corporate sustainability claims and the systemic dispossession of marginalized populations.
