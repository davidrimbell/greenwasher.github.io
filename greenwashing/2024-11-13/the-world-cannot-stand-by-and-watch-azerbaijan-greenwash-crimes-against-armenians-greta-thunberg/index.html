“The World Cannot Stand By and Watch Azerbaijan Greenwash Crimes Against Armenians”: Greta Thunberg

Greta Thunberg highlights how Azerbaijan exploits the upcoming climate conference in Baku to greenwash its human rights abuses and alleged ethnic cleansing of Armenians. This conflation of environmental diplomacy with authoritarian crimes exemplifies how regimes use global sustainability forums to legitimize oppression and distract from their domestic and regional aggression. She condemns the hypocrisy of nations, particularly the EU, that import Azerbaijani fossil fuels while claiming to support human rights. These energy ties effectively fund and normalize Azerbaijan’s actions, undermining the moral authority of climate policies that fail to address the political context of fuel sources. This article is relevant to greenwashing because it exposes the danger of decoupling climate action from human rights. It illustrates how states can use ecological narratives to sanitize their image, masking severe ethical violations behind the veneer of international cooperation and energy trade.
