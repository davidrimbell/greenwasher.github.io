New lawsuit claims Apple Watches aren’t carbon neutral - MacTech.com

New lawsuit claims Apple Watches aren’t carbon neutral - MacTech.com

Consumers have sued Apple, alleging its marketing of specific Apple Watches as "carbon neutral" is deceptive. The plaintiffs argue that relying on questionable carbon offset projects rather than achieving genuine reductions misleads buyers about the devices' true environmental impact. This case highlights a core greenwashing tactic: using carbon credits to mask lingering emissions instead of eliminating them. By certifying products based on these offsets, companies can claim neutrality while continuing activities that contribute to climate change, creating a false sense of sustainability for environmentally conscious shoppers. The lawsuit is relevant to greenwashing because it exposes how third-party certifications can be manipulated to validate misleading environmental claims. It underscores the need for transparency in sustainability reporting and challenges the validity of offset-heavy strategies that prioritize marketing over actual ecological responsibility.
