Greenwashing
EN ES

A lawsuit alleges Apple exaggerates its Apple Watch’s environmental friendliness by relying on carbon offset projects that offer no genuine additional benefits. Plaintiffs argue that the cited forest initiatives in Kenya and China are either legally protected or already forested, rendering the claimed emissions reductions redundant and misleading. This case highlights how corporations can exploit the growing demand for sustainability by using ineffective offset schemes rather than implementing real reductions. Such practices allow companies to market themselves as carbon-neutral while avoiding the significant costs associated with genuinely decarbonizing their supply chains and manufacturing processes. The lawsuit serves as a critical warning about greenwashing, demonstrating that carbon neutrality claims based on questionable offsets may be false. It underscores the necessity for rigorous verification of environmental projects to ensure they deliver actual ecological benefits, rather than merely serving as marketing tools to capitalize on consumer eco-consciousness.
