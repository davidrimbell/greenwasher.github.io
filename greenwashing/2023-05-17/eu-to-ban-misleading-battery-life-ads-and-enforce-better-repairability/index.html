EU to Ban Misleading Battery Life Ads and Enforce Better Repairability

EU to Ban Misleading Battery Life Ads and Enforce Better Repairability

The European Parliament proposes new legislation to combat deceptive marketing by prohibiting unsubstantiated environmental claims like "climate neutral." This directly tackles greenwashing by ensuring only certified, evidence-based ecological labels are permitted, preventing manufacturers from profiting from false sustainability narratives. The directive also targets planned obsolescence by mandating transparent repair cost information and interoperability requirements. By exposing practices designed to shorten product lifespans, the law forces companies to prioritize durability, shifting the market reward toward genuinely sustainable goods rather than those engineered to fail. This shift is crucial for greenwashing mitigation as it empowers consumers with verified data. By eliminating the jungle of misleading labels and forcing transparency on repairability, the legislation ensures purchasing decisions reflect actual product longevity and environmental impact, rather than fabricated marketing hype.
