Greenwashing
ES EN

Markets show divergence between tech optimism and broader economic instability. Rising inflation and political tensions create volatility, while corporate results highlight specific sector strengths and vulnerabilities amidst a complex regulatory landscape. This financial volatility matters to greenwashing because it drives demand for attractive ESG narratives. Companies often exploit such uncertainty by emphasizing minor green credentials to draw capital away from skeptical investors. Consequently, superficial sustainability claims become more potent as a marketing tool. This dynamic allows firms to obscure actual environmental performance, leveraging market anxiety to maintain investment inflows without committing to substantive ecological reforms.
