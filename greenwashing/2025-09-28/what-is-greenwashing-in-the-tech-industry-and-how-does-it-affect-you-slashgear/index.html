What Is Greenwashing In The Tech Industry And How Does It Affect You? - SlashGear

Microsoft claims massive carbon removal efforts, yet its annual emissions slightly exceed this total. This disparity highlights how corporate sustainability announcements can mask the negligible impact of actual operations on climate change. The company’s reliance on energy-intensive data centers reveals a contradiction between public green messaging and rising resource consumption. Critics argue this strategy prioritizes reputation over meaningful environmental responsibility, stressing local supplies. This case illustrates classic greenwashing, where symbolic actions distract from substantial ecological harm. It underscores the need to scrutinize total operational impacts rather than relying solely on selective environmental achievements.
