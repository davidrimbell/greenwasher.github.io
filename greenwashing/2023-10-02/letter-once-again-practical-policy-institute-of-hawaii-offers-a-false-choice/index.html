Letter: Once again, Practical Policy Institute of Hawaii offers a false choice

Letter: Once again, Practical Policy Institute of Hawaii offers a false choice

The article refutes false narratives suggesting renewable energy adoption is impractical, confirming scientific consensus that human activity drives climate change. It highlights how denying this reality obstructs necessary transitions to sustainable infrastructure, particularly for vulnerable regions like Hawaii. Rejecting the false dichotomy between climate action and preparedness, the text argues that developing independent renewable energy is the most logical strategy to secure energy independence against potential geopolitical disruptions. This piece is relevant to greenwashing as it exposes tactics used by vested interests to discredit established climate science. By framing necessary sustainability measures as impractical, such arguments serve to delay regulatory action and protect fossil fuel interests, a core mechanism of deceptive greenwashing campaigns.
