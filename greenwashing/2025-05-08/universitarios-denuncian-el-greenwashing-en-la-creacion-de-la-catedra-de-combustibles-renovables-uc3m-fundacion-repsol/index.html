Universitarios denuncian el ‘greenwashing’ en la creación de la ‘Cátedra de Combustibles Renovables UC3M–Fundación Repsol’

Universitarios denuncian el ‘greenwashing’ en la creación de la ‘Cátedra de Combustibles Renovables UC3M–Fundación Repsol’

Protesters argue that UC3M’s new chair with Repsol exemplifies corporate greenwashing, allowing a major polluter to exploit public academia for reputational laundering without consequence. This partnership prioritizes corporate interests over societal needs, effectively privatizing research agendas and undermining the integrity of public education resources. The demonstration highlights a broader concern regarding the increasing influence of fossil fuel companies in universities. Activists view this as part of a systematic strategy to legitimize destructive industries within academic institutions, thereby shaping research outcomes to benefit private entities rather than addressing urgent ecological crises or student concerns. This case is relevant to greenwashing as it illustrates how corporations use funding to mask their environmental impact and manipulate public perception. By embedding themselves in educational settings, companies like Repsol create an illusion of sustainability and responsibility, deceiving the public while continuing practices that contribute to global warming and ecological destruction.
