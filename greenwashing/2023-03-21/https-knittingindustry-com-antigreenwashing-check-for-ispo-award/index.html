Greenwashing
ES EN

The ISPO Award has institutionalized a rigorous anti-greenwashing evaluation, leveraging expert scrutiny to verify sustainability claims before final jury deliberation. This shift addresses the rising complexity of eco-claims by ensuring that awards reflect genuine environmental efforts rather than superficial marketing tactics. By integrating this specialized check into the permanent process, the organization aims to elevate the integrity of its accolades. Applicants now undergo a preliminary assessment by a recognized industry expert who examines manufacturing processes, circularity, and material sourcing. If ambiguities arise, brands receive early feedback, allowing them to clarify details and strengthen their submissions. This proactive step ensures that the jury makes decisions based on fact-based, comprehensive data rather than potentially misleading or vague corporate messaging. This initiative is crucial for combating greenwashing as it provides a trusted seal of quality for consumers and industry stakeholders. It empowers buyers to distinguish authentic sustainable products from deceptive advertising, fostering greater transparency in the sports and outdoor sector. Ultimately, this mechanism reinforces consumer trust and sets a higher standard for accountability in global sustainability reporting.
