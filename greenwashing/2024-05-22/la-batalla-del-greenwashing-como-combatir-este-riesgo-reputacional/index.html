La batalla del greenwashing: cómo combatir este riesgo reputacional

The article emphasizes that recent and forthcoming European Union directives are transforming greenwashing from a mere reputational risk into a serious regulatory issue, one that carries substantial financial penalties. This legal shift aims to empower consumers and combat unfair competition by enforcing stricter standards for environmental claims, compelling companies to move beyond vague marketing toward verifiable, sustainable business models. A critical implication is the deep-seated distrust consumers have toward corporate sustainability promises, fueled by previously widespread misleading and unfounded environmental assertions. Consequently, transparency and honest communication are no longer optional but essential prerequisites for credibility. The legislation serves as a catalyst for internal organizational alignment, forcing businesses to ensure that their actual performance matches their public messaging in order to restore consumer confidence. Ultimately, this regulatory framework is viewed as a strategic opportunity rather than merely a compliance burden. By eliminating greenwashing practices and integrating genuine sustainability into core operations, companies can turn ethical responsibility into a competitive advantage. The focus is shifting from symbolic gestures to tangible impact, ensuring that marketing and communication accurately reflect real environmental and social contributions to support responsible consumption.
