The ecological party Verdes EQUO highlights that "green posturing" and greenwashing have become detrimental to environmental progress. They argue this deceptive practice is more harmful than outright denialism because it masks policies that actively damage ecosystems, such as airport expansions or water exploitation, while falsely projecting an ecological image. This hypocrisy creates a false sense of security, allowing destructive projects to proceed under the guise of sustainability. Greenwashing is particularly relevant because it blocks urgent, necessary actions by prioritizing political optics over genuine environmental responsibility. The article illustrates how actors simultaneously defend supposed eco-friendly stances while supporting initiatives that contradict climate goals. This discrepancy undermines trust and prevents the implementation of decisive measures required to address the climate emergency, revealing a disconnect between public relations and actual policy impacts on nature and society. Ultimately, the text warns that reducing ecology to superficial campaigns fails to address the transversal nature of environmental issues. True sustainability must permeate all sectors, from energy to transport, rather than serving as a marketing tool. The relevance here lies in exposing how strategic communication manipulates public perception, delaying systemic change. By distinguishing between actual ecological commitment and mere image management, the article underscores the critical need for coherence between environmental rhetoric and tangible actions to effectively combat the climate crisis.
