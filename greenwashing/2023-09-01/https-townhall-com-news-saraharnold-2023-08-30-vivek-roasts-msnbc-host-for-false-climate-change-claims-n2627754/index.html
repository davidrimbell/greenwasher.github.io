Greenwashing
ES EN

Ramaswamy dismisses climate change as a hoax, arguing that fossil fuel access has drastically reduced weather-related deaths and promotes plant growth through carbon dioxide. He challenges opposing views as anecdotal, insisting that empirical data proves climate concerns are exaggerated or false. This narrative is highly relevant to greenwashing because it spreads scientifically dubious claims to undermine urgent environmental action. By framing climate science as a "hoax," it creates a pretext for maintaining fossil fuel dependence while appearing to engage in rational debate. Such rhetoric helps industries obscure their environmental impact and resist regulatory pressures under the guise of factual accuracy.
