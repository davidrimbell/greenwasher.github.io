Science to the Rescue: AI to fight greenwashing
Science Foundation Ireland launched the Future Innovator Prize to drive global change through ambitious, challenge-based funding. This initiative highlights how structured competition can direct scientific resources toward critical societal problems, fostering innovation that transcends local boundaries. One key focus is developing AI methods to detect greenwashing and accurately measure progress toward United Nations Sustainable Development Goals. By creating machine learning systems to sift through disclosures, stakeholders can identify misleading claims and take action against deceptive environmental practices. This project is relevant to greenwashing because it offers a technological solution to expose corporate greenwashing, thereby improving transparency and accountability in sustainability reporting.
