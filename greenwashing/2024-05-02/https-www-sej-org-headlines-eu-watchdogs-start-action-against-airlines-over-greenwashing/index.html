Greenwashing

European authorities are targeting airlines for vague carbon offset claims, demanding scientific substantiation. This enforcement highlights how misleading environmental promises deceive consumers. By mandating transparency, regulators combat greenwashing, ensuring accurate information protects public trust and prevents deceptive marketing in the travel sector.
