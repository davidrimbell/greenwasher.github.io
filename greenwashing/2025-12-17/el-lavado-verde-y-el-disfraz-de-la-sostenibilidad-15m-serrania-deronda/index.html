El lavado verde y el disfraz de la «sostenibilidad»##15M Serranía deRonda

El lavado verde y el disfraz de la «sostenibilidad»##15M Serranía deRonda

The article argues that current narratives of "green growth" serve as a deceptive mask to preserve unsustainable capitalist structures and the privileges of the ultra-wealthy. This superficial transition relies solely on technological fixes while ignoring the need for fundamental changes in production and consumption patterns, ultimately exacerbating ecological crises and social inequalities. By maintaining the dogma of perpetual economic growth, the system continues to exploit both human and non-human life, perpetuating war and genocide under the guise of prosperity. A major concern is the incompatibility between endless economic expansion and ecological limits, which the text identifies as the root cause of overlapping social and environmental disasters. The author highlights that while corporate wealth has surged, essential planetary boundaries have been breached, threatening the well-being of the majority. This disconnect reveals how the current model prioritizes accumulation over life, failing to deliver genuine prosperity to most people and endangering future generations. This analysis is crucial for understanding greenwashing because it exposes how corporations and governments use the language of sustainability to legitimize business-as-usual practices. The text advocates for a post-growth model centered on collective well-being within planetary boundaries, proposing alternatives like universal basic income and agroecology. By questioning what society truly values and demanding democratic deliberation, it challenges the "green" facade that hides systemic destruction and calls for a genuine dismantling of exploitative economic structures.
