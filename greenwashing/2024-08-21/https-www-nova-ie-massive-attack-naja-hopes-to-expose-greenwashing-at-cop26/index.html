Greenwashing
ES EN

This initiative exposes the pervasive nature of corporate greenwashing by revealing how major polluters manipulate public perception through social media. By tracking and visualizing climate disinformation, the campaign highlights the disconnect between companies’ environmental claims and their actual impact. This transparency forces a confrontation with the reality that much of the "eco-friendly" messaging is a strategic distortion designed to obscure significant ongoing damage, challenging the legitimacy of corporate sustainability narratives. The project specifically targets the role of social media platforms in amplifying this misleading content for profit. It argues that tech giants actively retain control over policies that normalize climate disinformation, drawing a parallel to how they manage health misinformation. The central implication is that these platforms are not neutral conduits but active participants in the "greenwash industrial complex," prioritizing engagement and revenue over truth. This holds digital executives accountable for failing to apply the same rigorous content moderation standards to climate issues as they do to public health crises. This work is crucial to the greenwashing discourse because it moves beyond criticizing individual brands to expose the systemic infrastructure enabling deceptive marketing. By providing real-time data on how advertising campaigns influence public opinion, it empowers consumers and activists to recognize manipulation tactics. The collaboration between artists and researchers underscores the cultural sector’s responsibility to refuse complicity, making this intervention a vital tool for dismantling the trust erosion caused by coordinated corporate disinformation efforts.
