Could Trump Accidentally End Greenwashing?

Critics warn that funding unscalable technologies like electric vehicles while ignoring leaner options constitutes greenwashing, hindering genuine climate progress. True sustainability requires moving beyond incremental technological fixes to rethink entire systems, such as transportation, for substantial gains. The article highlights how focusing on superficial solutions distracts from smarter, data-backed alternatives needed for real impact.
