Greenwashing
EN ES

Privacy policies often mask data exploitation as standard service operation, exemplifying greenwashing by framing invasive tracking as benign analytics. This obscures the true environmental and social costs of digital surveillance. Recognizing such deceptive transparency helps consumers identify and resist manipulative corporate narratives that prioritize profit over genuine sustainability.
