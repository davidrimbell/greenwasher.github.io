Lawsuit says ‘certified climate neutral’ snacks are not climate neutral

Lawsuit says ‘certified climate neutral’ snacks are not climate neutral

Clif Zbars are labeled "certified climate neutral," yet Mondelēz faces accusations of misleading consumers about the product's true carbon footprint. This lawsuit highlights how vague sustainability claims can mask substantial emissions, deceiving shoppers who believe they are buying eco-friendly options. Such practices exemplify greenwashing by using ambiguous terminology to project environmental responsibility without guaranteeing actual climate neutrality.
