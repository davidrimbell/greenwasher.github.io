Google’s carbon capture bullshit proves big tech is speed-running the greenwashing gauntlet

Google’s carbon capture bullshit proves big tech is speed-running the greenwashing gauntlet

The article argues that carbon capture and storage (CCS) is not a viable climate solution but a persistent fantasy used by the fossil fuel industry to prolong the use of high-emitting technologies. Despite decades of catastrophic underperformance against every projection from major scientific bodies, CCS continues to receive funding and political support. Its primary function is not to reduce atmospheric CO2, but to enable continued fossil fuel extraction and combustion under the guise of environmental responsibility, effectively serving the industry’s economic interests rather than ecological ones. Big technology companies are now adopting this failed model, utilizing CCS claims to mask their skyrocketing energy consumption driven by AI and data centers. By framing CCS as an "advanced" solution, tech giants create a misleading veneer of sustainability that allows them to build new gas-fired power plants while maintaining the appearance of progress toward net-zero goals. This strategy shifts focus away from the urgent need to eliminate fossil fuel dependence, instead offering a rhetorical promise that distracts from the reality of unabated emissions and the likelihood of project failures. This convergence of tech and fossil fuels exemplifies greenwashing, where corporations use complex, unproven technologies to justify continued pollution. The narrative of technological salvation serves to fend off genuine regulation and public scrutiny, allowing companies to prioritize profit and growth over actual decarbonization. Ultimately, the reliance on CCS represents a desperate attempt to maintain the status quo, blending corporate greenwashing with industrial failure to delay necessary systemic changes in global energy production.
