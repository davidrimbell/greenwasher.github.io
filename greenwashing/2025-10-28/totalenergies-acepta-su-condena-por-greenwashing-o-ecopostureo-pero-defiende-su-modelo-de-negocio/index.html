TotalEnergies acepta su condena por ‘greenwashing o ecopostureo’, pero defiende su modelo de negocio

TotalEnergies acepta su condena por ‘greenwashing o ecopostureo’, pero defiende su modelo de negocio

This case represents a significant legal milestone because it judicially confirms that oil companies can be penalized for greenwashing when their advertising messages contradict their actual practices. The Paris Judicial Court’s ruling highlights that claiming to be a leader in the energy transition while maintaining a strategy predominantly focused on fossil fuels constitutes a misleading commercial practice, establishing an important precedent for corporate accountability. The relevance to the fight against greenwashing lies in how the sustainability narrative of TotalEnergies is dismantled. NGOs argue, and the court upholds, that the discrepancy between massive investments in hydrocarbons and campaigns projecting a commitment to carbon neutrality manipulates consumers. This exposes how the promotion of small investments in renewables can merely serve as a facade to conceal the negative environmental impact of their core business. Finally, the company’s response illustrates the typical tension in such cases: although it accepts the condemnation and will modify its communications to be more factual, it continues to defend its oil and gas production as essential. This outcome underscores the difficulty traditional companies face in aligning their marketing with operational reality, reinforcing the need for environmental claims to be backed by tangible structural changes rather than just public relations strategies.
