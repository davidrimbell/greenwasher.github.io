Greenwashing
EN ES

The article highlights two recent lawsuits exposing NW Natural for allegedly misleading the public about its environmental impact. The company is accused of portraying natural gas as a clean, climate-friendly alternative while simultaneously contributing significantly to greenhouse gas emissions and health hazards. These legal actions suggest that the utility has systematically obscured the true carbon costs of its services to maintain market dominance, raising serious concerns about corporate accountability in the energy sector. A core issue involves the company’s use of vague sustainability claims to manipulate consumer perception. One lawsuit targets a program purportedly offsetting carbon emissions, revealing that funds were directed toward industrial dairy farms with questionable environmental benefits. Additionally, the utility has distributed misinformation about climate policies to discourage regulatory action. These practices demonstrate how corporations can exploit the complexity of carbon accounting to create a false sense of eco-friendly operations, effectively hiding the reality of their fossil fuel dependency from customers. This case is critical to understanding greenwashing because it illustrates how entrenched utility companies manipulate information to resist the transition to renewable energy. By framing fossil fuels as necessary and harmless, NW Natural attempts to maintain public trust and infrastructure investment despite growing evidence of climate and health harms. The legal challenges and calls for local organizations to sever ties underscore the urgent need for transparency, showing that greenwashing is not just about marketing but about actively obstructing public awareness and policy progress toward electrification.
