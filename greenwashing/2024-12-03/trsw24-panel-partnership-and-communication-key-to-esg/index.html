TRSW24 panel: Partnership and communication key to ESG

TRSW24 panel: Partnership and communication key to ESG

The panel highlights that both greenwashing and greenhushing are detrimental to genuine sustainability progress. Greenhushing, where companies remain silent about their efforts to avoid scrutiny, obscures environmental impacts and erodes public trust. This lack of transparency prevents stakeholders from making informed decisions, ultimately hindering collective industry advancement and discouraging active consumer support for authentic green initiatives. Regulatory frameworks, particularly from the EU, are tightening to ensure environmental claims are scientifically substantiated and based on full life-cycle assessments. These regulations aim to eliminate misleading statements and distinguish genuine benefits from standard legal requirements. Consequently, companies face increased liability and must rigorously verify their marketing, requiring deep collaboration between legal, compliance, and marketing teams to navigate complex legislative landscapes accurately. Consumer demand, especially from younger demographics, is driving a shift toward transparency and authentic value alignment. Travelers now prioritize brands that demonstrate clear environmental and social impacts, viewing sustainability as a competitive advantage rather than a mere compliance exercise. This trend underscores the necessity for open communication across value chains, as no single entity can achieve significant sustainable transformation without partnership and shared accountability.
