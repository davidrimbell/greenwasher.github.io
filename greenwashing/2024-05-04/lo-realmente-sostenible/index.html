Lo realmente sostenible

The article highlights the energy transition as a vital opportunity to reduce emissions, enhance security, and boost economic development through clean technologies. However, it warns that not everything marketed as renewable is truly sustainable, emphasizing the need to distinguish between genuine sustainability and misleading marketing that conceals negative environmental or social impacts. This distinction is crucial because the widespread adoption of greenwashing practices erodes consumer trust and undermines the efforts of companies genuinely committed to environmental protection. The text underscores that greenwashing is not merely an advertising issue, but a disrespect toward consumers that prevents informed decision-making and compromises the integrity of authentic environmental initiatives. The article’s relevance to the study of greenwashing lies in its connection to new European regulations, such as the Due Diligence Directive and rules against unfair commercial practices. These measures aim to eliminate the sale of falsely “green” products, demonstrating how corporate deception distorts the market and harms both society and the global credibility of climate change mitigation efforts.
