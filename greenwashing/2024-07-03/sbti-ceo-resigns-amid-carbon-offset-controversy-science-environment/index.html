SBTi CEO Resigns Amid Carbon Offset Controversy | Science-Environment

The CEO of the Science Based Targets Initiative has resigned amid intense controversy over the organization’s decision to permit companies to offset supply chain emissions using carbon credits. This pivot sparked significant backlash from staff and climate experts who argued that relying on offsets undermines the urgent need for direct emission reductions, creating a dangerous precedent for corporate accountability. This dispute highlights a critical greenwashing risk: allowing offsets may incentivize businesses to maintain high emission levels by purchasing credits rather than achieving genuine decarbonization. Critics warn that such policies dilute the integrity of scientific climate targets, potentially enabling companies to greenwash their environmental impact while failing to address the root causes of their carbon footprint. The incident underscores the fragility of standards that attempt to balance market mechanisms with strict climate action. As the organization seeks new leadership, this case serves as a vital warning about the reputational and ethical dangers of designing frameworks that prioritize financial flexibility over absolute emission cuts, thereby misleading stakeholders about true corporate sustainability.
