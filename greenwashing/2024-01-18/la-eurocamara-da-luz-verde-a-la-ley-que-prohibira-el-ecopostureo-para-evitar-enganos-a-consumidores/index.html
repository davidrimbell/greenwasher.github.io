La Eurocámara da luz verde a la ley que prohibirá el 'ecopostureo' para evitar engaños a consumidores

The EU bans misleading green marketing and eco-posturing, prohibiting vague environmental claims that lack verified proof. This targets deceptive corporate practices in which brands exaggerate their sustainability efforts to appear greener than they actually are. The regulation also forbids unfair comparisons and false durability claims, ensuring that labeling is transparent and based on certified standards. These measures prevent companies from concealing poor environmental performance behind superficial or manipulated marketing narratives. This regulation is crucial for combating greenwashing by holding firms accountable for specific, verifiable environmental impacts. It protects consumers from manipulation and mandates genuine sustainability transparency across the market.
