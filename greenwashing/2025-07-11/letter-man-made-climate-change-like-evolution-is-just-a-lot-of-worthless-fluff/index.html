Letter: Man-made climate change, like evolution, is just a lot of worthless fluff

Letter: Man-made climate change, like evolution, is just a lot of worthless fluff

The letter argues that reducing fossil fuel usage fails to lower atmospheric CO2, dismissing climate science as propaganda. It claims net-zero initiatives are scams exploiting taxpayers and ignores energy security risks from foreign dependencies. This perspective is relevant to greenwashing because it promotes climate denialism, a tactic often used by industries to deflect responsibility for their environmental impact. By labeling scientific consensus and sustainability efforts as fraudulent, the author attempts to delegitimize genuine eco-friendly policies and maintain the status quo. Such rhetoric obscures the reality of corporate and systemic contributions to global warming. It shifts focus away from actual emission sources, allowing bad actors to exploit public doubt. Understanding this manipulation helps distinguish between true sustainability efforts and deceptive narratives designed to protect harmful business practices.
