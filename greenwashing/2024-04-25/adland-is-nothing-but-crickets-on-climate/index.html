Adland Is Nothing but Crickets on Climate

Rising regulations and activist pressure are causing brands to hide their sustainability efforts, a phenomenon known as greenhushing. This strategic silence serves as a filter, separating authentic environmental commitments from superficial or misleading advertising claims. Consequently, the current period allows for a more rigorous assessment of genuine corporate responsibility versus greenwashing.
