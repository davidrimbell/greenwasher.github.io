Competition Bureau to speed development of guidance on new greenwashing rules

The Competition Bureau is accelerating guidance on new anti-greenwashing laws requiring companies to substantiate environmental claims. This move addresses significant industry uncertainty, exemplified by the Pathways Alliance halting its environmental communications due to regulatory ambiguity. These updates are crucial for clarifying legal standards and preventing misleading eco-promotions.
