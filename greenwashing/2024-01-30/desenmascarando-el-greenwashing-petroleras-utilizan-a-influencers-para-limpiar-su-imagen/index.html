Desenmascarando el Greenwashing: ¿Petroleras utilizan a influencers para limpiar su imagen?

Major oil companies have shifted their climate denial strategy toward sophisticated greenwashing tactics, leveraging influencers and digital marketing to divert attention from their environmental impacts. This new approach aims to project a superficially eco-friendly image, much like their historical campaigns that placed the blame on consumers for carbon footprints, while downplaying their actual investments in renewable energy. The significance of this phenomenon lies in the disconnect between advertising narratives and corporate reality. By promoting sports activities or nature-related content on social media platforms, these companies create a "smokescreen" that distracts the public from the urgent need to reduce emissions. This not only deceives society but also perpetuates dependence on fossil fuels under a facade of sustainability, effectively slowing down the global energy transition. Combating greenwashing requires a collaborative and proactive response from civil society, governments, and regulatory agencies. It is crucial to establish clear accountability standards for advertising agencies and monitor their ties to the oil industry. Without strict oversight and collective awareness, disinformation tactics will continue to hinder progress toward a transparent and sustainable future, keeping corporations free from legitimate responsibilities.
