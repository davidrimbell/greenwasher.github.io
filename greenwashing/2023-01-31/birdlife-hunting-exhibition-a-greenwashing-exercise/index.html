BirdLife: Hunting exhibition a ‘greenwashing exercise’

BirdLife Malta condemns a state-financed hunting exhibition in Gozo schools as a greenwashing tactic, arguing it masks the immoral reality of hunting. The NGO views this initiative as an unethical attempt to legitimize killing wildlife by linking it to conservation activities like planting trees. The exhibition has sparked significant backlash from conservationists who reject the notion that hunting serves ecological purposes in developed Europe. By framing hunting as environmentally beneficial, organizers seek to improve their public image while misleading students about the true impacts of the practice. This case is relevant to greenwashing because it illustrates how organizations use educational settings to sanitize harmful activities. It highlights the strategic manipulation of environmental narratives to gain social acceptance, urging educators to recognize and resist such deceptive marketing efforts disguised as public service.
