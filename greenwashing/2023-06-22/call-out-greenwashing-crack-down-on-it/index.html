Call out greenwashing, crack down on it

Delta faces litigation over carbon neutral claims, reflecting growing regulatory scrutiny against misleading environmental marketing assertions in the aviation sector. Regulators actively challenge unsubstantiated green labels, as seen when authorities forced Ryanair to halt its low emission advertising due to insufficient evidence. This trend highlights increased accountability for companies using vague sustainability narratives to mask their actual environmental impact and operational reality.
