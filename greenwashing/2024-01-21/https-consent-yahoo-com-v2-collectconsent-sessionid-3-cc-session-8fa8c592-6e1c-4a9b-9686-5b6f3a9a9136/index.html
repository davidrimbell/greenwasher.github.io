Greenwashing
ES EN

This cookie consent banner lacks specific sustainability claims, yet often features eco-friendly aesthetics or green imagery to subtly associate the brand with environmental responsibility. Such visual cues can mislead consumers into believing a company holds strong environmental credentials, despite the text focusing solely on data tracking and privacy settings. This highlights greenwashing by demonstrating how brands exploit green aesthetics to enhance their image, distracting users from the reality of extensive personal data collection.
