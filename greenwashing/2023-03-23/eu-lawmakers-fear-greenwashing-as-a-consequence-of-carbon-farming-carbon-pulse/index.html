EU lawmakers fear greenwashing as a consequence of carbon farming « Carbon Pulse

European lawmakers voiced serious concerns about greenwashing risks associated with carbon farming during a recent expert hearing. This highlights the critical need for rigorous standards to prevent misleading environmental claims. Such scrutiny ensures that nature-based solutions deliver genuine ecological benefits rather than serving as mere marketing tools for dubious carbon credits.
