Cena de BioCultura Madrid 2025: Decálogo contra el Greenwashing y Futuro del Sector Ecológico

Cena de BioCultura Madrid 2025: Decálogo contra el Greenwashing y Futuro del Sector Ecológico

The article highlights the launch of a "Decalogue against Greenwashing" at the BioCultura Madrid 2025 gala, marking a pivotal moment for the professionalization of the Spanish organic sector. Amid a leadership transition in the organizing entity, industry leaders united to combat fraudulent marketing and confusing eco-labels. This initiative aims to protect the integrity of certified organic agriculture by clearly distinguishing genuine ecological practices from superficial sustainability claims that mislead consumers. Central to this effort is the call for robust regulatory frameworks and public policy support to ensure the sector's growth and viability. Stakeholders demanded specific incentives, such as reduced VAT for organic products, public procurement targets for canteens, and fiscal benefits that favor true sustainable producers. The group argued that administrative action must prioritize the polluters’ responsibility for environmental damage while making legitimate organic food more affordable and accessible to the general public. This event is highly relevant to greenwashing as it represents a concerted industry defense against the dilution of ecological standards. By presenting a unified front, the sector seeks to eliminate the ambiguity surrounding terms like "sustainable" or "regenerative," which are often exploited for marketing purposes. The decalogue serves as a strategic tool to educate consumers, demand stricter regulation of unverified claims, and preserve the credibility of the organic movement against the rise of eco-posturing.
