Greenwashing
ES EN

A recent report reveals that major global banks are greenwashing their environmental impact by funding fossil fuel projects that devastate the Amazon rainforest. Despite claiming adherence to strict ethical guidelines, their policies fail to protect most of the biome due to significant geographical and technical limitations in monitoring. This hypocrisy is particularly damaging as these institutions provide over half of all loans to offending oil and gas companies. Their inadequate oversight allows financing to continue for activities that destabilize the climate and violate Indigenous land rights, directly contradicting their public sustainability commitments. This case highlights a critical greenwashing trend where financial entities exploit vague ESG standards to mask destructive practices. It underscores the urgent need for transparent, enforceable environmental regulations to hold lenders accountable for their role in ecological degradation and human rights abuses.
