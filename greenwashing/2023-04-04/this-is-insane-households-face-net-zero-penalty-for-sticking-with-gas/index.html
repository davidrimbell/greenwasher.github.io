This is insane: Households face net zero penalty for sticking with gas

This is insane: Households face net zero penalty for sticking with gas

The government plans to raise gas costs and lower electricity prices to accelerate the shift away from fossil fuels. This economic pressure aims to speed up the transition to green energy, directly impacting household budgets. The policy is relevant to greenwashing as it demonstrates concrete financial disincentives for carbon-intensive energy, proving a genuine commitment to net-zero goals rather than mere marketing.
