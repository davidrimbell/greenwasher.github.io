Scientists Reveal the Scale of Meat and Dairy Industry Greenwashing

Many meat and dairy firms prioritize carbon offsetting over genuine decarbonization, relying on minimal-scale pilots rather than systemic change. This superficial approach masks the lack of substantial emission reductions, offering only nominal environmental improvements. Such practices exemplify greenwashing by creating an illusion of sustainability while avoiding the significant operational shifts necessary for meaningful climate impact.
