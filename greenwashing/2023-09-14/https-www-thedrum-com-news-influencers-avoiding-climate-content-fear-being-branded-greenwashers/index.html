Greenwashing
ES EN

The article highlights a significant gap between high consumer trust in influencers for sustainability advice and the pervasive risk of greenwashing in this sector. It reveals that while creators are increasingly producing eco-conscious content, many hesitate due to insufficient education and fear of backlash. To combat misinformation and ensure alignment with current climate science, Unilever has established a Creator Council. This initiative partners with major global organizations to develop guidelines that help influencers navigate complex regulatory landscapes and maintain authentic, accurate messaging. This development is crucial for understanding greenwashing, as it underscores the industry’s recognition of its vulnerability to deceptive practices. By creating structured oversight, the fashion attempts to transform influencers from potential vehicles of environmental misinformation into verified advocates for genuine sustainable behavior.
