Apple’s ‘carbon neutral’ claims come under scrutiny as EU seeks to clamp down on ‘greenwashing’

Apple’s ‘carbon neutral’ claims come under scrutiny as EU seeks to clamp down on ‘greenwashing’

Apple’s claim of carbon-neutral watches relies heavily on purchasing offsets, a practice now facing scrutiny for potentially misleading consumers. The EU is banning such neutrality claims by 2026, arguing that offset-based assertions are scientifically inaccurate and lack genuine environmental benefit. This conflict highlights greenwashing risks, where companies use financial instruments like carbon credits to fabricate eco-friendly images rather than reducing actual emissions.
