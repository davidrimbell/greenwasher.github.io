Greens call for investigation into council's slow net zero progress

The Highland Council’s failure to meet its initial 2025 net-zero target, which was later diluted to 2045, highlights a critical gap between political pledges and actual environmental stewardship. This delay raises serious concerns about the credibility of local government commitments in the face of accelerating climate crises. Community pressure and political candidates are now demanding independent scrutiny from Audit Scotland to verify if the council is taking sufficient action. The lack of tangible progress despite declared emergencies suggests potential systemic negligence or administrative incompetence in implementing necessary decarbonization strategies. This case is relevant to greenwashing as it illustrates how entities may make ambitious sustainability claims without executing corresponding actions. By shifting targets rather than achieving results, organizations risk misleading the public, undermining trust, and delaying critical climate mitigation efforts through superficial or unfulfilled declarations.
