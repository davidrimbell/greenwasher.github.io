Greenwashing
EN ES

New guidelines mandate that environmental claims require credible evidence and clear qualifiers. This prevents ambiguous terms like “sustainable” from masking misleading practices, ensuring transparency and scientific backing for all assertions. These regulations aim to protect consumers from deceptive marketing while encouraging genuine corporate responsibility. By harmonizing manufacturer efforts with consumer interest, the rules foster integrity without stifling legitimate environmental initiatives in the growing market for eco-friendly products. This initiative is vital for combating greenwashing by establishing strict disclosure standards. It shifts the burden of proof onto companies, ensuring that environmental labels reflect actual performance rather than vague marketing tactics, thereby restoring trust in commercial environmental claims.
