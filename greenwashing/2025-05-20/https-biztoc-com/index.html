Greenwashing
EN ES

FedEx’s $300 million electric truck order signals a major corporate step toward fleet electrification. However, such high-profile sustainability pledges often mask the full environmental impact of manufacturing and energy sourcing. This highlights the greenwashing risk where marketing benefits overshadow actual carbon footprint reductions. Meanwhile, TSMC and Caterpillar’s massive US investments focus on capacity and infrastructure rather than ecological responsibility. These deals emphasize economic growth and supply chain resilience, potentially sidelining critical green criteria. Such narratives can distract investors from evaluating whether these projects genuinely support the energy transition or simply exploit green trends for prestige. This article is relevant to greenwashing because it showcases how corporations leverage eco-friendly announcements to enhance their public image. By highlighting electric vehicles and large-scale investments, firms may create an illusion of sustainability without committing to comprehensive environmental accountability. Investors must look beyond these surface-level claims to assess true ecological impact.
