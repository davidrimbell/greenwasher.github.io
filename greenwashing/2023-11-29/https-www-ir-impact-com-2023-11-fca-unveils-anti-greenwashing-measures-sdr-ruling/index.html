Greenwashing
ES EN

The Financial Conduct Authority has implemented strict sustainability disclosure rules to combat misleading claims, ensuring investment products are labeled and marketed with transparency. This initiative mandates that all authorized firms present fair, clear, and accurate information, directly targeting the deceptive practices often associated with greenwashing. By enforcing rigorous standards for naming and product labels, the regulator aims to protect consumers and investors from exaggerated environmental benefits. This creates a trustworthy market environment where capital allocation decisions are based on genuine sustainability goals rather than vague or false advertising. These measures are crucial for maintaining integrity in the growing sustainable finance sector. By prioritizing consumer protection and preventing greenwashing, the UK seeks to preserve its leadership position, fostering trust that encourages genuine investment in truly sustainable assets.
