Opportunity Green take on cruise companies for greenwashing over use of LNG
This article highlights a critical form of greenwashing in the cruise industry, where major companies promote liquified natural gas (LNG) as an environmentally responsible fuel choice. The core issue lies in the deceptive marketing of a fossil fuel as a climate solution, despite scientific evidence indicating that methane leaks during extraction and combustion negate any carbon dioxide reductions. By emphasizing only partial benefits while omitting the high climate-warming impact of methane, these companies create a misleading narrative of sustainability that contradicts international climate goals. The report identifies specific communication strategies that likely violate UK advertising regulations, such as labeling LNG as a step toward net-zero emissions or describing it as a cleaner, eco-friendly alternative. These claims are characterized as hyperbole that serves commercial interests rather than environmental protection. The legal director of Opportunity Green argues that such marketing is pure greenwashing, designed to protect corporate balance sheets rather than address the climate emergency, thereby exploiting the public’s desire for sustainable tourism options. This case is highly relevant to greenwashing because it demonstrates how vague or selectively positive environmental claims can deceive consumers and mask continued reliance on harmful fossil fuels. The organization’s decision to pursue legal action underscores the growing scrutiny of corporate environmental messaging. It signals a shift from mere criticism to regulatory enforcement, urging the industry to abandon misleading LNG propaganda in favor of credible, science-based zero-emission solutions that genuinely align with the Paris Agreement’s temperature targets.
