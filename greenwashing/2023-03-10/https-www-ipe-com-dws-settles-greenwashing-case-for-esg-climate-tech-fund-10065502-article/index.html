Greenwashing
ES EN

German authorities have successfully challenged asset managers DWS and Commerz Real, highlighting critical gaps in sustainability disclosures. DWS agreed to stop marketing its Climate Tech fund as excluding controversial sectors, preventing a court trial and signaling stricter regulatory scrutiny of ESG claims. Simultaneously, a Stuttgart court ruled that Commerz Real failed to adequately explain the methodology behind its klimaVest fund’s environmental impact calculations. The decision emphasizes that financial institutions must transparently define their positive environmental targets in clear proximity to product information to comply with EU regulations. These cases underscore the growing legal risks associated with vague or insufficient greenwashing. They establish a precedent that mere investment in green sectors is insufficient without rigorous, transparent proof of environmental impact, forcing firms to align marketing narratives with substantive, verifiable data.
