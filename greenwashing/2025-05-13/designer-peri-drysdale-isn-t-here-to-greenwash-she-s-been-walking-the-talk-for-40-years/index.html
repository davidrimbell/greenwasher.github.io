Designer Peri Drysdale isn't here to greenwash, she's been walking the talk for 40 years

The article positions Untouched World as a definitive counter-example to greenwashing, contrasting its decades-long, holistic sustainability practices against the superficial marketing tactics prevalent in the fashion industry. Founder Peri Drysdale criticizes the widespread miscommunication and false claims made by larger brands that prioritize newsworthy sustainability narratives over genuine environmental impact. This distinction is crucial because it highlights how true sustainability requires tangible actions, such as designing for recyclability, rather than vague or misleading assertions that confuse consumers. A primary illustration of this commitment is the brand’s rigorous approach to material innovation and waste management. Unlike competitors that create unrecyclable fiber blends for marketing purposes, Untouched World actively diverts nearly all textile waste from landfills and invests in circular solutions like recycled yarns. By focusing on durability and end-of-life recyclability, the brand demonstrates that environmental responsibility involves practical problem-solving and transparency, directly addressing the pitfalls of greenwashing where products are labeled "eco-friendly" despite lacking structural sustainability. Furthermore, the narrative emphasizes the importance of educating the next generation of designers to combat industry-wide deception. Through initiatives like the Fashion Reimagined programme, the brand mentors students to prioritize quality and ethical manufacturing over fleeting trends. This educational role is relevant to the greenwashing discourse because it fosters a future leadership capable of recognizing and rejecting superficial sustainability claims, thereby promoting a fashion culture rooted in integrity, long-term thinking, and authentic environmental stewardship.
