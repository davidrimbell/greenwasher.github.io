Greenwashing
ES EN

KLM agreed to withdraw its “Fly Responsibly” campaign but made no commitments regarding future marketing practices, raising concerns about continued environmental misinformation. Despite this concession, the airline continues to offer carbon offsetting, which critics argue misleadingly suggests customers can neutralize their flight’s impact through minor contributions like reforestation or biofuel purchases. Experts emphasize that removing specific slogans does not resolve the underlying issue of greenwashing. Critics argue that as long as the airline pursues unsustainable growth, any claims of sustainability are inherently deceptive, undermining genuine climate efforts by creating a false impression of environmental responsibility. This case highlights the aviation industry’s broader struggle with contradictory messaging, where promotion of climate action coexists with policies that exacerbate emissions. The ruling serves as a critical precedent for holding corporations accountable for misleading green claims, reinforcing the need to address the structural disconnect between marketing narratives and actual ecological impact.
