Greenwashing
ES EN

Markets display uneven strength driven by AI optimism, contrasting with weaknesses in traditional sectors and franchise struggles. This volatility highlights how selective growth narratives can mask broader economic fragility, a dynamic often exploited in greenwashing to suggest universal sustainability progress. Simultaneously, a regulatory vacuum in crypto allows firms to expand without clear oversight. The rush to fill this void with vague rules creates an environment where companies can claim compliance or innovation without tangible accountability, mirroring the superficial commitments seen in environmental claims. Ultimately, the lack of decisive regulatory action empowers entities to define their own standards. This ambiguity enables organizations to market vague benefits as substantive achievements, perpetuating greenwashing by shifting responsibility away from concrete, verifiable impacts.
