Autocontrol desestima la reclamación de Iberdrola contra Repsol por lavado de imagen verde

Autocontrol dismissed Iberdrola’s complaint against Repsol, concluding that the average consumer would not misinterpret the advertisements as a certification of total renewability. This ruling suggests that certain self-regulatory limits may permit ambiguous claims that exaggerate corporate sustainability without facing immediate sanctions. The dispute illustrates the tension between environmental marketing and unfair competition, highlighting how companies can exploit gaps in advertising clarity. The resolution reflects the difficulty in defining what constitutes “greenwashing” within contexts of industrial self-regulation, where the subjective interpretation of consumers determines the legitimacy of campaigns. This case is relevant to greenwashing because it exposes the vulnerability of environmental defense mechanisms in the face of industry practices. It shows that even allegations of deception can be dismissed, allowing questionable practices to persist while companies seek prior validation to shield themselves from criticism.
