Greenwashing
EN ES

The article illustrates how climate misinformation functions as a form of greenwashing by weaponizing political rhetoric to undermine scientific consensus. By misrepresenting nuanced policy critiques as wholesale attacks on environmental strategies, media outlets create a false narrative that public support for net-zero goals is collapsing. This distortion serves to delay urgent action by manufacturing the impression that established climate measures are ineffective or unpopular, thereby protecting fossil fuel interests under the guise of fiscal responsibility. Furthermore, the deliberate dismantling of climate research institutions and the flood of AI-generated content erode the credibility of legitimate science. This strategic devaluation of expertise mimics greenwashing tactics by creating doubt where none exists, suggesting that the climate crisis is less certain or actionable than proven by data. The resulting confusion allows policymakers and corporations to evade accountability, presenting inaction as a reasoned alternative to evidence-based mitigation rather than acknowledging the overwhelming scientific reality. Ultimately, this environment of confusion and disinformation directly facilitates greenwashing by decoupling corporate or governmental narratives from factual climate progress. When the integrity of the scientific community is systematically weakened through funding cuts and misinformation campaigns, it becomes easier for entities to make unsubstantiated environmental claims without facing rigorous scrutiny. This undermines global efforts to enforce transparency, allowing false sustainability credentials to persist unchecked while the actual crisis intensifies.
