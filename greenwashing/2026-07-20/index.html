EU Green Rules Spark Silence: Why Tourism Hides Eco Efforts

Stories selected and summarised by AI, not reviewed by a person. Every source is linked.

New rules in Europe are changing how tourism companies act. This has created a strange trend called “greenhushing.” Now, businesses are afraid to talk about being green. They worry that if they claim to be sustainable, people will accuse them of lying. This fear is called greenwashing. So, companies keep quiet about their real efforts to help the environment.

This silence makes it hard for you to make good choices. When you are looking for a trip, you need to know if a company is truly eco-friendly. But now, they are not telling you.

There is a good side to this. The new rules force companies to do real work. They cannot just use pretty words. The European Commission’s Green Claims Directive stops companies from using vague terms like “eco-friendly” without proof. This shift encourages firms to focus on cutting carbon emissions. It is not about branding anymore. It is about action. For example, the EU banned false environmental claims in 2024. This forces brands to check their supply chains carefully. Now, the products you consider are backed by real data, not just feelings.

However, there are bad sides for consumers. The fear of getting in trouble makes many organizations stay silent. A study by the University of Cambridge in 2023 found that 60% of companies reduced their public sustainability communications after stricter laws were proposed. This lack of information makes it hard for you to compare options. You cannot tell if a hotel is saving energy if the company hides its data. Without clear numbers, you are guessing about the true impact of your choices.

This secrecy also stops you from supporting good innovation. When a brand uses renewable energy, it often stays quiet to avoid scrutiny. As a result, you miss the chance to support leaders in the field. The Greenwashing Digest shows how this trend hurts competition. Without visible proof, you cannot tell the difference between a company that is improving and one that is just following the rules. This uncertainty slows your decision-making. It also lowers your trust in the market.

You need clear data to make smart choices. The current silence takes away the evidence you need to match your purchases with your values. Companies must balance following the rules with talking to you. This will help you navigate the sustainable market. Only through open talk can you find brands that are truly making a difference.
