Earth Day Greenwashing Must Not Detract From Message: Protest Founder

Earth Day Greenwashing Must Not Detract From Message: Protest Founder

Denis Hayes, a founder of Earth Day, condemns fossil fuel companies for exploiting the event’s legacy to secure favorable public relations. This strategic appropriation of environmental symbolism represents classic greenwashing, distracting from the urgent reality of the climate crisis. Such corporate messaging directly contradicts the movement’s original aims by masking destructive practices behind superficial eco-friendly branding. This disconnect highlights how industry actors manipulate public perception to appear responsible while continuing harmful extraction activities. This article is vital to understanding greenwashing because it illustrates the tension between genuine activism and corporate opportunism. It serves as a cautionary example of how powerful entities co-opt sustainability narratives to deflect accountability rather than drive meaningful ecological change.
