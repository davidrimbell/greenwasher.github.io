PFS launches course on Sustainability Disclosure Requirements and Anti-Greenwashing - IFA Magazine

The Personal Finance Society launched an e-learning course to help financial advisers comply with new UK regulations on sustainable investments. This training focuses on meeting the Financial Conduct Authority’s anti-greenwashing rules and understanding new investment labels, ensuring firms can offer compliant products. By educating professionals on identifying greenwashing examples and adhering to Sustainability Disclosure Requirements, the course aims to enhance market integrity. It ensures advisers can accurately label products and prevent misleading claims that undermine genuine sustainable finance efforts. This initiative is crucial for combating greenwashing because it bridges the gap between regulatory expectations and practical application. It empowers advisers to make informed decisions, thereby protecting clients from deceptive marketing and promoting transparency in the growing sustainable investment sector.
