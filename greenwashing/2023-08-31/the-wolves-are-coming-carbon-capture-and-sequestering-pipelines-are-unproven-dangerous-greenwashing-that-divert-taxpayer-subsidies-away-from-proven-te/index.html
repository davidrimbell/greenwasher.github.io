The Wolves Are Coming!: Carbon Capture and Sequestering Pipelines Are Unproven Dangerous Greenwashing That Divert Taxpayer Subsidies Away from Proven Technologies

The article argues that carbon capture and sequestration, often promoted as a vital climate solution, is largely a greenwashing tactic that diverts essential resources from proven renewable technologies. By highlighting the technology’s poor historical success rate and lack of permanent storage guarantees, the author exposes how corporate interests use these projects to maintain fossil fuel dependency rather than addressing the root causes of emissions. This misleading narrative masks the significant environmental and safety hazards associated with the infrastructure required to transport captured carbon. Furthermore, the text details the severe physical risks posed by high-pressure CO2 pipelines, which can rupture and cause catastrophic, lethal plumes of asphyxiating gas, as evidenced by past incidents. The author emphasizes that these projects prioritize corporate profit and federal tax credits over local safety, often utilizing eminent domain to bypass property owner consent. This dynamic creates a scenario where landowners bear disproportionate risks, including potential harm to their health, crops, and water sources, while receiving minimal financial compensation compared to sustainable energy alternatives. This case is relevant to greenwashing because it illustrates how carbon capture initiatives are marketed as immediate, effective climate actions to secure public support and funding, despite being economically and technically flawed. The aggressive expansion of these pipelines, driven by government incentives, exemplifies how industries rebrand problematic infrastructure as "green" to justify its construction. Recognizing this disparity between the marketed benefits and the actual environmental and community costs is crucial for identifying and combating the deceptive practices inherent in current climate policy frameworks.
