Greenwashing
ES EN

British Airways is among firms criticized for buying ineffective carbon credits, highlighting a broader industry trend of misleading environmental claims. The Advertising Standards Authority is actively cracking down on unqualified “net zero” assertions, signaling stricter regulatory scrutiny. Recent scientific evidence confirms that many offset programs, particularly forest protection projects, fail to reduce real emissions. This data validates long-standing suspicions that such practices constitute greenwashing rather than genuine sustainability efforts. This situation is crucial to understanding greenwashing as it demonstrates how corporations exploit vague carbon offset mechanisms to appear eco-friendly without making actual operational changes. The convergence of regulatory action and academic proof exposes the gap between marketing narratives and environmental reality.
