This case highlights why sports sponsorship is a critical vector for greenwashing, allowing polluters to gain social license to operate and delay regulatory action. By portraying themselves as partners in a sustainable future, these firms create a false sense of progress, undermining genuine climate efforts. Understanding this dynamic is essential for recognizing how corporate influence can manipulate public perception, ensuring that society remains vigilant against marketing strategies designed to obscure environmental harm rather than eliminate it.
