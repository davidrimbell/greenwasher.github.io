Protesters at COP 29 accuse Azerbaijan of greenwashing genocide as host of COP29, call for release of Armenian prisoners

The article argues that participating in Azerbaijan’s upcoming COP29 allows the host nation to greenwash its severe human rights abuses. By ignoring the illegal detention of 23 political prisoners, including Ruben Vardanyan, the international community implicitly validates the regime’s actions. This distraction shifts focus away from the government’s systematic crackdown on dissent and potential genocide against Armenians. Critics assert that declaring the climate summit a symbol of peace is hypocritical while citizens remain imprisoned on bogus charges. The UN’s own warnings about genocide risks highlight the severity of the situation, suggesting that environmental conferences are being exploited to sanitize the state’s reputation. Relevance to greenwashing lies in how environmental platforms can be used to obscure criminal behavior. By hosting COP29, Azerbaijan attempts to rebrand itself as a peaceful leader, thereby masking its ongoing oppression. This underscores the danger of separating climate action from human rights, allowing authoritarian regimes to use green initiatives as a shield for atrocities.
