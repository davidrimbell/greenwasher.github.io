Declaran para este viernes la segunda preemergencia ambiental del año en la RM: Habrá restricción vehicular adicional

Santiago is experiencing severe pollution due to stagnant air, prompting a pre-emergency declaration that restricts traffic and the burning of wood. This situation underscores the gap between environmental urgency and public health risks. The measures highlight the real-world impact of inadequate urban air quality management. Authorities are enforcing strict protocols to mitigate hazardous emissions, prioritizing immediate health protection over long-term sustainability goals. This crisis reveals the limitations of temporary solutions in areas plagued by chronic pollution. It serves as a stark warning against greenwashing, demonstrating that superficial eco-labels cannot substitute for the necessary structural changes in energy and transportation systems.
