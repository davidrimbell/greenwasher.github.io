Greenwashing
ES EN

Lululemon faces scrutiny for allegedly misleading consumers about its environmental benefits while relying on polluting materials and processes. This case highlights how corporations may exaggerate ecological contributions to mask significant environmental damage. It underscores the need to distinguish genuine sustainability efforts from deceptive marketing claims in the fashion industry.
