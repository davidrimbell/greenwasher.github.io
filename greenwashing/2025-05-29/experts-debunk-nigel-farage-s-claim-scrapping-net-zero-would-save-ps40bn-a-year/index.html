Experts debunk Nigel Farage's claim scrapping net zero would save £40bn a year

Nigel Farage claimed that scrapping climate policies would fund social benefits, a premise heavily disputed by experts. Former Treasury officials and researchers argue he drastically inflates the actual costs of net zero initiatives, which remain far lower than his assertions. This discrepancy highlights a core greenwashing tactic: exaggerating the economic burden of sustainability to justify regressive policy shifts. By framing environmental action as prohibitively expensive, politicians can mislead the public into supporting the rollback of essential climate measures under the guise of fiscal responsibility. The article is relevant to greenwashing because it demonstrates how distorted data is weaponized to undermine climate commitments. It reveals the strategy of manufacturing a false economic crisis around green spending, thereby masking the political motivation to abandon environmental protections while presenting such actions as necessary financial relief for citizens.
