Greenwashing
EN ES

Greenwashing fundamentally undermines brand trust and discourages sustainable purchasing behavior by triggering consumer skepticism when environmental claims appear exaggerated or fraudulent. This deception leads to significant drops in positive brand sentiment, proving that deceptive marketing strategies ultimately damage the very reputation brands seek to enhance. Consumers with higher environmental knowledge are particularly prone to critically evaluating eco-claims and reacting more strongly to perceived dishonesty. By applying psychological frameworks to analyze consumer reactions, researchers have identified that situational involvement drives these responses, highlighting the critical need for brands to substantiate their green assertions with verifiable evidence rather than relying on vague messaging. This research is vital to understanding greenwashing as it exposes the tangible risks of transparently false advertising, moving beyond theoretical concerns to demonstrate real-world reputational damage. It urges brands and regulators to prioritize authenticity and honesty, serving as a warning that without rigorous transparency, companies face severe backlash from increasingly savvy and environmentally conscious audiences.
