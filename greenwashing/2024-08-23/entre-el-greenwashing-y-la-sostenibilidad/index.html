Entre el greenwashing y la sostenibilidad

The growing trend of conscious consumption has driven corporations to adopt sustainability labels and ESG standards, even as the climate crisis persists. This divergence between green marketing and operational reality has elevated greenwashing to a critical threat, where companies simulate eco-friendly practices through superficial gestures that lack real impact, deceiving consumers and evading accountability. The relevance of greenwashing lies in the fact that new international regulations demand tangible transparency, putting an end to the ambiguity of misleading labels. This phenomenon undermines consumer trust and exposes companies to the loss of markets and financing. The fundamental difference does not lie in declared intentions, but in the absence of concrete actions that support environmental claims, which constitutes the essence of green deception. In conclusion, authentic sustainability is an urgent necessity to ensure business viability and national development. Proactively adapting to these requirements, beyond mere marketing, allows companies to transform a business opportunity into a genuine commitment, avoiding market penalties and securing a viable future in the face of stringent global demands.
