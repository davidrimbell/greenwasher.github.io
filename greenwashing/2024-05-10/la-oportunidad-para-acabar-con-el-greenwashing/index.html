La oportunidad para acabar con el ‘greenwashing’

La oportunidad para acabar con el ‘greenwashing’

The article argues that greenwashing, although deceptive, demonstrates that sustainability has become a valuable market asset. However, when this demand translates into empty promises lacking genuine commitment, it distorts competition and erodes consumer trust. This phenomenon not only hinders progress toward global environmental goals but also creates an environment of uncertainty where deception is disguised as ecological responsibility, harming both the market and society. The relevance of this text for understanding greenwashing lies in how new European regulation transforms the perception of the problem. The European study reveals that half of environmental claims are vague or unfounded, justifying the need for strict laws. The regulation not only seeks to penalize deception with severe sanctions but also empowers consumers by demanding clarity and truthfulness, marking a turning point where legislation closes the door to environmental misinformation and compels companies to operate with genuine transparency. Finally, it is proposed that the fight against greenwashing should be viewed as a strategic opportunity for companies, beyond mere legal compliance. The central recommendation is to align the entire corporate strategy with honest communication about environmental impact, discarding superficial marketing practices. By prioritizing integrity, brands not only avoid greenwashing but also enhance their competitiveness and genuinely connect with consumers who reward authentic commitment, thereby consolidating an era of verifiable corporate responsibility.
