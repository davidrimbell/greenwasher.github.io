EU Commission's substantiating green claims proposal lacks focus warns SAC

The Sustainable Apparel Coalition criticizes the EU Commission’s draft proposals for failing to address greenwashing effectively, arguing that the directive lacks scientific rigor and standardized frameworks. This omission creates legal uncertainty and risks allowing inconsistent methodologies, which could ultimately undermine genuine sustainability efforts rather than advancing them. The coalition urges policymakers to adopt the Product Environmental Footprint, a holistic and scientifically grounded method, to ensure clarity for consumers and fair competition for businesses. By relying on established, evolving scientific metrics, the EU can prevent greenwashing and help authentic eco-friendly products gain recognition without falling victim to vague or misleading claims. This article is crucial for understanding greenwashing because it highlights the tension between legislative ambition and practical implementation. It demonstrates how vague regulations can inadvertently enable deceptive marketing, emphasizing that robust, science-based standards are essential to protect consumers and ensure that environmental claims reflect true sustainability impacts.
