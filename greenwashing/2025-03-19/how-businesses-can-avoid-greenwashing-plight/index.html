The core message is that eco-friendly marketing is not merely a branding strategy but a legally regulated practice with significant consequences for non-compliance. It explains that while specific anti-greenwashing legislation may be absent in some regions, existing competition, consumer protection, and advertising laws provide robust frameworks for prosecuting deceptive practices. These regulations mandate accuracy in product descriptions and prohibit false information, meaning that overstating environmental benefits constitutes a violation of consumer rights. Consequently, companies must ensure all sustainability assertions are backed by credible evidence, as vague or unsupported terms like "carbon-neutral" are increasingly scrutinized under broad legal definitions of deception. Relevance to greenwashing lies in the urgent call for transparency and evidence-based marketing to distinguish genuine sustainability efforts from deceptive advertising.
