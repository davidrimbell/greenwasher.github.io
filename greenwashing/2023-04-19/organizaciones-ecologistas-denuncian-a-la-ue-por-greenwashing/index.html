Organizaciones ecologistas denuncian a la UE por 'greenwashing'

Environmental groups argue that the European Commission engages in greenwashing by classifying certain gas and nuclear investments as sustainable. This misleading categorization creates a false perception of environmental friendliness, potentially benefiting industries while obscuring their significant ecological impact and contradicting climate goals. The core issue lies in validating fossil fuels and nuclear power under green finance rules, despite their high carbon emissions and reliance on controversial technologies. By equating these polluting sources with renewables, the Commission fails to accurately reflect environmental reality, thereby misleading stakeholders about the true sustainability of these energy transitions. This case is crucial for the greenwashing discourse as it challenges official institutional narratives. It highlights how regulatory frameworks can be manipulated to legitimize harmful practices through strategic labeling, urging greater scrutiny of corporate and governmental claims to ensure transparency and genuine ecological accountability in the fight against climate change.
