Más de 800 casos de publicidad ambiental engañosa fueron difundidos en el 2023

The article highlights that Indecopi investigated over 800 cases of misleading environmental advertising in Peru last year, signaling a significant rise in greenwashing within the corporate sector. This surge reveals that deceptive sustainability claims are increasingly overshadowing genuine eco-friendly practices, challenging the integrity of market offerings and exposing consumers to falsehoods about product impact. To combat this, consumers must remain critical and rely on recognized third-party certifications and tangible evidence of sustainability rather than accepting vague corporate promises. Simultaneously, businesses face severe reputational risks if they engage in these deceptive practices. True sustainability requires clear, measurable goals, internal policy enforcement, and full transparency, all of which are now achievable through modern technology. The article is relevant to greenwashing as it underscores the urgent need for verified, traceable claims to protect both consumer trust and the environment, moving the market toward authentic rather than superficial environmental responsibility.
