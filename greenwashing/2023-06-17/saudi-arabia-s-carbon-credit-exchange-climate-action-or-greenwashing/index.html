Saudi Arabia's carbon credit exchange: climate action or greenwashing?

Saudi Arabia's carbon credit exchange: climate action or greenwashing?

Saudi Arabia launched a major carbon credit auction, sparking debates over whether this demonstrates genuine climate action or serves as a public relations stunt for a major oil producer. Critics question the authenticity of the initiative, viewing it as greenwashing by an energy giant, while officials frame it as a responsible step toward their 2060 net-zero goals. This highlights the tension between corporate sustainability claims and the actions of fossil fuel-dependent nations, raising doubts about the sincerity of such environmental investments.
