ICC welcomes European Commission’s Green Claims Directive - ICC - International Chamber of Commerce

ICC welcomes European Commission’s Green Claims Directive - ICC - International Chamber of Commerce

The International Chamber of Commerce emphasizes that environmental marketing must adhere to strict standards of truthfulness and scientific substantiation. This guidance is critical for combating greenwashing, as it demands that aspirational claims, such as future net-zero goals, are backed by concrete methodological proof. Without such rigorous evidence, companies risk misleading consumers about their actual environmental impact. To address this, the ICC has updated its global principles and practical frameworks to help marketers craft honest communications. These resources provide clear checklists and commentary to identify valid environmental claims, ensuring that advertising aligns with responsible practices. This structured approach helps businesses distinguish between genuine sustainability efforts and deceptive marketing tactics that exploit public concern. Ultimately, these standards serve as a vital tool for regulators and policymakers aiming to enforce transparency. By promoting harmonized global rules, the ICC seeks to protect consumers from misleading information while encouraging businesses to take genuine climate action. This initiative highlights the urgent need for accountability in green marketing to prevent the erosion of trust in environmental claims.
