Call for the EU to reject carbon offsets following scandal of largest voluntary carbon offset certifier - Global Forest Coalition

Google Analytics tracks anonymous site data to help owners understand visitor behavior and improve their digital presence. This data collection reveals the prevalence of analytics tools, which can be used to mask the actual environmental impact of corporate operations. Understanding these digital footprints is relevant to greenwashing, as companies may use sophisticated data to appear more transparent than they are.
