PM Carney scraps Canada’s anti-‘greenwashing’ rules

PM Carney scraps Canada’s anti-‘greenwashing’ rules

Mark Carney’s government is dismantling previous anti-greenwashing regulations to prioritize oil and gas competitiveness. By reinstating tax incentives for liquefied natural gas and carbon capture, the administration frames fossil fuels as environmentally superior. This strategic pivot signals a deliberate shift away from stringent environmental accountability. The reforms suggest that economic interests now outweigh rigorous standards for verifying genuine climate action, weakening safeguards against misleading environmental claims. Consequently, this policy retreat undermines trust in corporate and governmental sustainability pledges. It exemplifies greenwashing by using selective incentives to portray fossil fuel expansion as legitimate climate progress, eroding the integrity of national environmental commitments.
