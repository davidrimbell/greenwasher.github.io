Greenwashing
ES EN

The article exposes the inherent hypocrisy of the fossil fuel sector, where companies that perpetuate the climate crisis attempt to rebrand themselves as sustainability leaders. This contradiction, akin to a tobacco company advertising against cancer, reveals a systematic image-washing strategy based on misleading language and the occupation of institutional spaces, while their emissions and extractive activities continue to rise. The relevance of this text lies in its identification of greenwashing as a critical barrier to effective climate action. Experts and organizations emphasize that relying on corporate voluntary commitments is as risky as "leaving the flock to the wolf," since these practices conceal the destructive nature of their core business operations, affecting not only the energy sector but also the banking and insurance industries that finance such activities. The main conclusion is that self-regulation is insufficient and that strict legislation is required, such as the pioneering initiative in the United Kingdom and the legislative advances in the European Union, to ban misleading advertisements. This regulatory step is necessary and must be extended across the entire financing chain, establishing clear limits to combat dishonest marketing and ensure that the fight against climate change is not sabotaged by corporate interests.
