Greenwashing
EN ES

The article argues that the fossil fuel industry aggressively greenwashes natural gas by mislabeling it as a clean or renewable energy source to maintain market dominance and profits. This deceptive marketing ignores the full lifecycle emissions, which often exceed those of coal, and exploits the misleadingly benign term "natural gas" despite its composition of potent methane. By presenting this dirty fuel as an environmentally friendly option, companies aim to delay the transition to truly sustainable energy systems while continuing to exploit resources. This narrative extends beyond advertising into policy manipulation, where governments and regulatory bodies classify natural gas as green energy to justify subsidies and tax credits. Such classifications contradict scientific data and international climate agreements, effectively locking societies into long-term fossil fuel dependency. The industry’s emphasis on "renewable natural gas" further obscures the reality that methane, regardless of its source, remains a significant driver of global warming and air pollution, rendering these labels largely meaningless for genuine climate action. The persistence of natural gas usage poses severe health and environmental risks, including methane leaks from infrastructure, respiratory issues linked to gas stoves, and contamination from fracking processes. The article concludes that these corporate and political maneuvers are deliberate attempts to preserve business models reliant on fossil fuels, rather than authentic efforts toward decarbonization. True climate progress requires rejecting these greenwashing tactics in favor of a rapid shift to renewable electricity sources like wind and solar.
