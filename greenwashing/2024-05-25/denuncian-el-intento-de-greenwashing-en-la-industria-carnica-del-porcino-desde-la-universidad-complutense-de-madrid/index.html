Denuncian el intento de greenwashing en la industria cárnica del porcino desde la Universidad Complutense de Madrid

Denuncian el intento de greenwashing en la industria cárnica del porcino desde la Universidad Complutense de Madrid

The Stop Industrial Livestock Coordination denounces that the pork industry employs “greenwashing” strategies to distort its environmental impact, as exemplified by a course sponsored by the Complutense University of Madrid that seeks to manage the sector’s reputation under the banner of sustainability. Critical organizations argue that this initiative aims to present a positive narrative of “triple sustainability” and animal welfare, thereby concealing the reality of an industrial model responsible for severe ecological damage. The significance of this case for greenwashing lies in the discrepancy between promotional messaging and scientific data on pollution. Despite claiming leadership in biosecurity, intensive pig production in Spain significantly contributes to greenhouse gas emissions and massively contaminates surface and groundwater with nitrates, violating European regulations and causing public health problems. The article concludes that these advertising practices manipulate society to justify an unsustainable food system, similar to recent sanctions against other multinational meat companies. It calls for the withdrawal of the university training program for legitimizing this misleading discourse and highlights the urgent need for a radical transformation toward healthier, environmentally friendly food systems, free from the influence of fossil fuels.
