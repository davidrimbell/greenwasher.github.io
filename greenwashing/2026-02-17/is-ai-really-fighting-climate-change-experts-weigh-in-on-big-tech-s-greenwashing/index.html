Is AI really fighting climate change? Experts weigh in on big tech's ‘greenwashing’

Tech giants are misleading the public by claiming artificial intelligence is a critical solution to climate change, despite lacking verifiable evidence for substantial emission reductions. This narrative ignores the reality that generative AI’s massive energy and water demands create a growing carbon footprint, effectively undermining its purported environmental benefits. The industry is employing classic greenwashing tactics by conflating the efficiency of traditional machine learning with the high-impact nature of modern large language models. By attributing past success with older technologies to current AI expansion, companies justify their resource-intensive operations while obscuring the fact that AI’s net impact on global heating is likely negative rather than positive. This article is crucial for understanding greenwashing because it exposes how powerful entities manipulate environmental narratives to mask harmful practices. It highlights the danger of accepting corporate sustainability claims without rigorous scrutiny, demonstrating how "green" branding can be used to distract from the ecological damage caused by rapid technological scaling and infrastructure expansion.
