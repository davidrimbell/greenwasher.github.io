Greenwashing
ES EN

Companies collect vast user data, using cookies and geolocation for targeted advertising and behavioral tracking. This practice fuels greenwashing by enabling firms to project eco-friendly images while masking unsustainable operations through data-driven marketing. Relevance lies in how companies use privacy-invading tracking to greenwash their brand. By leveraging precise user data for personalized ads, they create an illusion of transparency while obscuring the environmental harm behind their products.
