The article is relevant to greenwashing because it illustrates how environmental and ethical claims can be obscured by broader financial narratives. With companies like Volkswagen recalling millions of vehicles due to safety defects, there is a risk that corporations might use unrelated positive news, such as tariff refunds or new drug approvals, to distract from underlying operational failures. This environment allows firms to potentially mask unsustainable or hazardous practices behind superficial sustainability or innovation branding, making it harder for consumers and investors to distinguish genuine ethical conduct from calculated public relations efforts that divert attention from significant product flaws or security breaches.
