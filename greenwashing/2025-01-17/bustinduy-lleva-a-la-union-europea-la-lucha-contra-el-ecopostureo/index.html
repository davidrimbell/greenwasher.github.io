Bustinduy lleva a la Unión Europea la lucha contra el "ecopostureo"

Bustinduy lleva a la Unión Europea la lucha contra el "ecopostureo"

The publication of the “Sustainable Communication Guide” marks a turning point in Europe’s fight against greenwashing, as it establishes, for the first time, clear criteria for identifying and sanctioning unverified environmental claims. This pioneering tool demonstrates that promoting products as eco-friendly without scientific evidence constitutes an unfair and misleading practice, depriving consumers of the ability to assess the true sustainability of offerings. By linking these practices to violations of consumer protection law, the guide legitimizes the need for rigorous transparency that safeguards people’s rights against so-called “eco-posturing.” The significance of this document lies in its preventive and corrective approach, addressing the crisis of trust generated by the proliferation of vague or false environmental claims in the market. With a large proportion of environmental claims lacking verifiable basis, the guide aims to empower consumers by demanding truthful and accessible information, aligned with the European Union’s new regulatory frameworks. This not only seeks to correct market distortions but also to foster an ecological transition based on trust, ensuring that companies’ environmental commitments are tangible and demonstrable through solid data. Finally, the initiative underscores the urgency of transnational collaboration to enforce these standards, as evidenced by joint actions against sectors such as aviation for misleading advertising. The threat of severe penalties and continuous oversight serves as a powerful deterrent, encouraging corporations to abandon greenwashing in favor of genuine strategies. This measure is crucial for preserving the integrity of the sustainable market, ensuring that the right to responsible consumption is not undermined by corporate misinformation, and consolidating an environment where sustainability is a proven fact rather than merely a marketing claim.
