Miliband’s net zero fantasies are falling apart – and only he can’t see it

The article argues that government-driven net-zero policies are causing deliberate economic harm by destroying profitable industries like oil and gas and automotive manufacturing, resulting in massive job losses and reduced tax revenues. It contends that officials prioritize ideological goals over practical economic stability, leading to higher energy costs and the collapse of new green initiatives, thereby refuting the narrative that such transitions create sufficient new employment opportunities. This disconnect is relevant to greenwashing because it exposes the discrepancy between the promoted benefits of green policies and their tangible negative impacts. The piece suggests that political and corporate advocates engage in deceptive practices by claiming economic advancement and cleaner living standards while ignoring or hiding the real-world consequences, such as job insecurity and financial burden on consumers. Ultimately, the author claims that these policies rely on flawed data and grandiose promises that do not align with reality. By dismissing critics as "culture warriors" rather than addressing legitimate concerns about energy reliability and cost, proponents of net-zero are accused of misleading the public about the true viability and economic soundness of their agenda.
