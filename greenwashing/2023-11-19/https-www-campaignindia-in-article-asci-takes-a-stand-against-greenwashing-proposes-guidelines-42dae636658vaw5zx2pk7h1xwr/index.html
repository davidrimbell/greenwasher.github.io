Greenwashing
ES EN

These draft guidelines aim to curb greenwashing by prohibiting brands from misleading consumers through selective claims, such as highlighting the absence of ingredients not typically found in competing products. This ensures that marketing does not create a false impression of superior environmental benefits where none exist. Furthermore, the regulations demand that all environmental certifications originate from recognized authorities and clearly define evaluated attributes. Claims regarding biodegradability or recyclability must be supported by reliable scientific evidence, preventing the use of vague or aspiratory statements without actionable plans to substantiate them. Relevant to greenwashing, these standards enforce transparency and authenticity in advertising. By mandating clear, evidence-backed disclosures, the guidelines protect consumers from deceptive practices, ensuring that "green" labels accurately reflect a product’s true environmental impact rather than serving as mere marketing tactics.
