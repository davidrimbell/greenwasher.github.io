Greenwashing
ES EN

Italy’s antitrust agency is investigating Shein for potentially misleading consumers with vague environmental claims regarding its sustainable collections. The probe highlights discrepancies between marketing messages about decarbonization and rising actual emissions, questioning the validity of using recycled materials or claiming garment recyclability without proof. This action directly addresses greenwashing by challenging how fast fashion brands market their eco-friendly status. The investigation underscores the risk of companies using generic terms to create a false image of sustainability while lacking transparency about their environmental impact or product lifecycle limitations. The case is highly relevant as it tests new EU anti-greenwashing regulations that ban unsubstantiated environmental claims. It demonstrates how authorities are moving beyond competition issues to protect consumers from deceptive practices, signaling stricter enforcement against vague "green" labeling in the apparel industry.
