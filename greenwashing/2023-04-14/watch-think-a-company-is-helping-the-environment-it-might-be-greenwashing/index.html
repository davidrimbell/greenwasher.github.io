WATCH — Think a company is helping the environment? It might be greenwashing

Companies frequently use terms like “eco-friendly” to mislead consumers, a practice known as greenwashing. This deception aims to capitalize on environmental concerns to drive sales, obscuring the true nature of their products or practices. Understanding this manipulation is crucial for identifying deceptive marketing. By recognizing these tactics, consumers can make more informed decisions rather than falling for superficial claims that prioritize profit over genuine sustainability. This topic highlights the importance of critical scrutiny in modern advertising. It serves as a vital reminder that labels do not always reflect reality, empowering individuals to demand transparency and authentic environmental responsibility from corporations.
