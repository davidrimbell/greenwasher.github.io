Turismo y greenwashing en campañas promocionales - Nexotur.com

The article highlights how the tourism industry has increasingly used sustainability as a marketing tool, inadvertently fueling the rise of greenwashing. By making exaggerated, ambiguous, or false environmental claims, companies create a misleading narrative that prioritizes commercial appeal over genuine ecological responsibility. This practice distorts consumer perception and erodes trust in the sector, turning sustainability from a serious operational commitment into mere promotional rhetoric. Greenwashing significantly undermines fair competition by penalizing companies that invest in real environmental improvements while rewarding those that excel only in storytelling. When claims lack verifiable backing, consumers cannot objectively compare offerings, leading to market confusion. This dynamic damages the reputation of truly sustainable businesses and weakens the overall credibility of the green transition, as the focus shifts from measurable impact to attractive but unsubstantiated messaging. In response, European regulations are tightening to enforce transparency and require scientific proof for all environmental assertions. The future of tourism will depend on external certifications and internal operational coherence rather than marketing alone. Authenticity and verified data are becoming essential competitive assets, ensuring that sustainability remains a driver of genuine progress rather than a vehicle for deception.
