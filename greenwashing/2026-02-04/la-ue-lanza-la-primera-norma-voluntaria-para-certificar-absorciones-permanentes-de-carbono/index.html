La UE lanza la primera norma voluntaria para certificar absorciones permanentes de carbono

The European Commission has established a new voluntary certification framework to regulate carbon removal technologies, aiming to accelerate the emerging climate tech market while supporting the EU’s 2050 climate neutrality goals. This initiative seeks to transform technological promises into measurable, comparable climate assets through clear methodologies for direct air capture, biogenic emission capture, and biochar absorption. By implementing these standards, the Commission aims to provide clear rules for companies and investors, thereby fostering responsible climate action. The framework intends to set a global reference, encouraging other regions to adopt similar rigorous guidelines for carbon accounting and sustainable development practices. This development is highly relevant to greenwashing because it directly combats vague environmental claims. By establishing verifiable standards and robust governance, the EU reduces the risk of "green" slogans masking lack of action. Consequently, the scheme promotes transparency and accountability, ensuring that carbon removal efforts are authentic and scientifically sound rather than mere marketing tactics.
