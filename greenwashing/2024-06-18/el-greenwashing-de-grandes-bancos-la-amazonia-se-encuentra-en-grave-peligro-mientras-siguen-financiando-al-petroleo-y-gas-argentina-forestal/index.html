El greenwashing de grandes bancos: la Amazonia se encuentra en grave peligro mientras siguen financiando al petróleo y gas - Argentina Forestal

A recent report reveals that the major banks financing oil and gas extraction in the Amazon possess environmental policies with serious gaps, allowing their investments to continue destroying the ecosystem. Despite their public commitments to social responsibility, most of these financial institutions do not apply their own risk management frameworks to most of the region, resulting in inadequate compliance with safeguards for indigenous peoples and biodiversity. This situation illustrates a clear case of greenwashing, where institutions use complex financing mechanisms, such as syndicated bonds, to avoid due diligence and conceal the destructive impact of their capital. By structuring transactions in ways that bypass environmental filters, these banks mask their contribution to environmental degradation, denying the reality that they are financing activities that violate fundamental rights and accelerate the loss of the tropical rainforest. The relevance of this text to the study of greenwashing lies in demonstrating how empty promises and misleading policies serve to perpetuate ecological and social harm. The report concludes that the only effective solution is the complete geographic exclusion of fossil fuel financing in the Amazon, demanding that banks cease being accomplices to the climate crisis and take real steps toward sustainability, ending financial practices that are supposedly regulated but in practice ignore them.
