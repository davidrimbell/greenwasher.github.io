Los países pactan una posición común para acabar con el 'ecopostureo' - EFEAgro

Los países pactan una posición común para acabar con el 'ecopostureo' - EFEAgro

The European Union has established a unified strategy to combat greenwashing by regulating explicit environmental claims. This directive aims to eliminate deceptive marketing practices, commonly referred to as "greenwashing" or eco-posturing, by ensuring that advertising regarding a product's environmental benefits is truthful and substantiated. Companies must now base their claims on clear criteria and recent scientific evidence, with most declarations subject to independent pre-verification. By mandating transparency and standardized proof, the legislation prevents businesses from using vague labels to mislead consumers, thereby forcing a shift toward genuine sustainability communication rather than superficial branding. This development is crucial for addressing greenwashing because it introduces legal accountability for environmental messaging. It empowers consumers to make informed choices based on verified data rather than manipulative aesthetics, directly tackling the misinformation that allows corporations to exaggerate their ecological efforts and exploit growing public demand for sustainable products.
