NAB prods farmers to use blockchain to prove green compliance

National Australia Bank adopts blockchain to verify rural sustainability data for green loans. This transparency directly mitigates greenwashing risks by ensuring accurate ESG reporting. Consequently, borrowers face credible scrutiny for claiming environmental benefits, enhancing trust in green finance.
