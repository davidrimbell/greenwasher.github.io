Galán advierte sobre el "greenwashing" y "los que dicen que hacen, pero no hacen" para retrasar la descarbonización

Ignacio Sánchez Galán, president of Iberdrola, criticizes the fact that greenwashing has replaced climate denialism as the primary tool for delaying decarbonization. This strategy allows certain actors to feign green actions without implementing real changes, solely with the aim of slowing down the energy transition. Galán points out that, although there is political and social consensus in favor of change, these delayers seek to hinder progress under the guise of sustainability, which contradicts the current environmental urgency. The Q-Zero Alliance emerges as a practical response to accelerate the decarbonization of thermal demand in industry and buildings, sectors that represent a crucial part of national energy consumption. By bringing together companies from various sectors and the government, the initiative aims to overcome existing barriers and take advantage of Spain’s abundant renewable energy. The goal is to transform dependence on fossil fuels into an economic opportunity, reducing imports and leveraging domestic natural resources such as sunlight and wind. This movement is relevant to the debate on greenwashing because it demonstrates how large corporations are moving from empty rhetoric to concrete collaboration. By focusing on real and measurable solutions for emission reductions, the alliance attempts to distinguish genuine action from misleading marketing. The initiative reflects a trend where social pressure and climate policies demand transparency and tangible results, preventing companies from using sustainability as a mere facade to maintain the status quo.
