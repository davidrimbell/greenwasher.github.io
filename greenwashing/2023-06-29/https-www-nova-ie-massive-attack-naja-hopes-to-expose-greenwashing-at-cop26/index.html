Greenwashing
ES EN

The campaign led by Massive Attack’s Rob Del Naja exposes the pervasive nature of corporate greenwashing by mapping the vast scale of climate disinformation spread through major social media platforms. By leveraging AI-driven data visualization, the project highlights how top polluting corporations manipulate public perception, creating a misleading impression of environmental responsibility. This initiative aims to reveal the "greenwash industrial complex," demonstrating how these entities profit from platforms that prioritize engagement over truth, thereby eroding collective trust in factual reality during a critical historical moment. A central focus is the accountability of social media giants, particularly Facebook, which the campaign identifies as the primary distributor of this harmful content. The artists argue that platform leaders possess the power to mitigate these harms but choose not to, driven by profit motives rather than ethical responsibility. By comparing the handling of pandemic misinformation to climate disinformation, the campaign challenges tech CEOs to apply similar safeguards to climate issues, emphasizing that the technology exists to protect users if the will to implement it were present. This intervention is highly relevant to understanding greenwashing because it moves beyond simple accusations of false advertising to expose the systemic infrastructure enabling it. It illustrates that greenwashing is not just a marketing tactic but a coordinated effort amplified by digital platforms to normalize corporate pollution. By making these manipulation tactics visible and relational for the public, the project underscores the urgent need for transparency and corporate accountability, showing how cultural sectors can return the favor by helping remove unethical sponsors from public view.
