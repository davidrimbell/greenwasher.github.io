Greenwashing
ES EN

Energy consumers have borne substantial hidden green tax costs over the past decade, exacerbated by proposed government additions. This financial burden disproportionately affects households during the cost of living crisis, prompting calls to suspend levies. These charges artificially inflate energy prices, revealing how environmental policies impact daily consumer budgets rather than solely benefiting the environment. The significant cumulative costs highlight the direct economic pressure placed on families. This is relevant to greenwashing because it exposes the disconnect between environmental rhetoric and actual household expenses. It suggests that green initiatives may serve political narratives while imposing severe financial penalties on the public, questioning the true nature of these eco-friendly claims.
