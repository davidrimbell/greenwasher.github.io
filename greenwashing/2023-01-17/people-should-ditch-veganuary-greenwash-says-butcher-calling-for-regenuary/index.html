'People should ditch Veganuary greenwash', says butcher calling for Regenuary

The article argues that Veganuary promotes a reductive narrative which inadvertently facilitates greenwashing by implying that all plant-based diets are inherently environmentally superior. Glen Burrows contends that this oversimplification ignores the ecological damage caused by importing produce like avocados, which often involves destructive farming practices and significant carbon footprints. By framing veganism as a default ethical choice, the movement obscures the complex realities of global supply chains, allowing consumers to feel environmentally responsible while supporting industries that may cause severe harm elsewhere. Regenuary was established to counter this lack of nuance by advocating for regenerative, locally sourced food regardless of dietary preference. The campaign highlights that true sustainability requires looking beyond ingredient type to the entire lifecycle of food, including transport, land use, and social impact. This approach challenges the "black and white" assumption that plant-based automatically equals low impact, urging consumers to recognize that cheap, imported alternatives can be more damaging than responsibly raised local meat. This perspective is crucial to understanding greenwashing because it exposes how broad sustainability labels can mask specific environmental abuses. By shifting the focus from dietary ideology to ethical sourcing and transparency, the article reveals how simple switches in consumer behavior can inadvertently support destructive industries. It underscores the importance of scrutinizing supply chains rather than relying on generic labels, thereby challenging companies that use vague ethical claims to掩盖 their actual environmental footprint.
