Cooking the Books, Cooking the Planet: David MacLean Is on a Mission to End Greenwashing Through His P3I Platform

Cooking the Books, Cooking the Planet: David MacLean Is on a Mission to End Greenwashing Through His P3I Platform

The Planet People Profit Impact (P3I) platform challenges the industry’s traditional "lowest-first-cost" mentality by quantifying the hidden environmental and social costs of sustainable building practices. By monetizing these often-overlooked burdens, such as health issues and lost productivity, the tool aims to shift decision-making from short-term financial savings to long-term holistic value. This approach seeks to make the true costs and benefits of development transparent to investors, architects, and community planners, revealing who specifically benefits or suffers from current building standards. The article highlights the inadequacy of existing green certifications like LEED, which it describes primarily as marketing tools for high-end projects rather than universal standards for accountability. The P3I platform positions itself as a corrective mechanism by framing sustainability as an economic advantage rather than merely an ethical obligation. By providing data that bridges the gap between expected and actual outcomes, it empowers stakeholders to understand the financial realities of poor design, such as reduced property values and increased liability, thus moving beyond superficial branding to substantive performance metrics. This initiative is relevant to the discourse on greenwashing because it directly addresses the opacity that allows superficial sustainability claims to mask negative impacts. By forcing the "hidden books" of environmental and social damage onto the balance sheet, the platform reduces the opportunity for stakeholders to ignore externalities while still labeling a project as "green." It promotes transparency and equal weighting of non-financial metrics, serving as a critical tool to distinguish genuine, measurable impact from mere marketing rhetoric in the built environment sector.
