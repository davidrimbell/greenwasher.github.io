RBI steps in to prevent 'greenwashing' - ET RealEstate

The Reserve Bank of India introduced regulations for green deposits to channel funds into sustainable projects while actively combating greenwashing. By mandating board-approved policies and independent annual verification, the regulator ensures that marketed environmental benefits are genuine and rigorously monitored. This framework requires lenders to strictly allocate proceeds toward verified activities like carbon reduction and ecosystem protection. It establishes a clear accountability structure where internal checks complement external audits, preventing institutions from falsely claiming ecological contributions without substantive operational changes. These measures are critical to greenwashing discourse because they create enforceable standards for financial claims. By shifting from voluntary promises to regulated verification, the RBI protects investors and markets from deceptive practices, ensuring that "green" labels reflect authentic environmental impact rather than mere marketing narratives.
