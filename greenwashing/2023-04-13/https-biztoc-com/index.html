Greenwashing
ES EN

The article highlights the tension between robust tech sector growth, driven by AI momentum, and broader economic pressures like inflation and geopolitical risks. This dichotomy illustrates how market narratives can mask underlying vulnerabilities, a key dynamic in greenwashing where impressive surface-level metrics obscure structural or environmental realities. Political interventions, such as potential fuel export bans and aggressive regulatory actions, suggest that high consumer costs and supply constraints are becoming unignorable. These measures indicate that short-term political fixes may inadvertently exacerbate long-term supply issues, revealing a gap between stated policy goals and actual market outcomes. The fierce competition among tech giants for dominance in AI and digital platforms further complicates the landscape. As companies vie for influence, their public stances on innovation and responsibility often serve to attract investors, potentially diverting attention from the true environmental or ethical impacts of their rapid expansion strategies.
