INT: California sues ExxonMobil over deceptive plastic recycling claims

INT: California sues ExxonMobil over deceptive plastic recycling claims

ExxonMobil faces litigation for decades of deception, falsely promoting widespread plastic recyclability despite technical and economic impossibilities. This misleading marketing drove excessive single-use consumption, exacerbating the global pollution crisis. The suit highlights how corporate greenwashing distorts consumer behavior, leading to increased waste rather than sustainable recycling. It underscores the danger of misleading environmental claims that shift responsibility onto consumers while denying material realities. This case is vital to understanding greenwashing, as it exposes deliberate misinformation campaigns. It demonstrates how corporations exploit recycling myths to maintain profitability and avoid accountability for plastic production, harming public trust and the environment.
