New corporate coalition strives for 24/7 carbon-free power - edie

The launch of the 24/7 Carbon-Free Coalition marks a significant shift in corporate climate strategy by moving beyond annual energy matching to hourly verification. By requiring companies to align their electricity consumption with carbon-free sources at all times, this initiative directly addresses the transparency and credibility gaps often exploited in greenwashing. This approach prevents organizations from relying on generic green certificates to mask their actual carbon footprint, ensuring that corporate claims reflect genuine, localized decarbonization efforts rather than superficial accounting tricks. Beyond immediate procurement changes, the coalition aims to drive systemic infrastructure and regulatory advancements through collective advocacy. The participating firms plan to share learnings and push for technologies like improved battery storage and flexible energy solutions, demonstrating that 24/7 clean energy is both viable and beneficial. This collaborative model encourages businesses to engage with local policymakers, thereby accelerating the transition of the broader electricity grid. It signals a move from isolated corporate targets to industry-wide structural change, reducing the risk of fragmented and less impactful climate actions. This development is critically relevant to greenwashing because it establishes a stricter, more verifiable standard for what constitutes "clean" energy. With increasing global pressure and political uncertainty, companies must demonstrate tangible investment in decarbonization rather than relying on outdated or misleading metrics. By raising the bar for hourly consistency and grid benefit, the coalition sets a new benchmark for integrity, making it harder for corporations to exaggerate their environmental progress and ensuring that their sustainability claims are robust and accountable.
