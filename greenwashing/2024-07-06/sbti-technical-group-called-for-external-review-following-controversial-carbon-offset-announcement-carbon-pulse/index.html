SBTi technical group called for external review following controversial carbon offset announcement « Carbon Pulse

Former Science Based Targets initiative leader Luiz Amaral resigned amid mounting internal pressure from technical experts. This departure highlights significant governance struggles within major climate certification bodies. The conflict suggests deep divisions over methodological integrity and target credibility. Such internal turmoil can undermine the reliability of corporate net-zero claims, raising questions about the robustness of currently endorsed standards. This event is relevant to greenwashing as it exposes vulnerabilities in the frameworks companies use to justify environmental promises. When governing bodies face instability, the validity of their certifications may be compromised, potentially allowing superficial or misleading sustainability efforts to go unchecked.
