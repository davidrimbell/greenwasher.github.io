SBTI CEO steps down following Scope 3 controversy - Just Style

The departure of the Science Based Targets initiative’s CEO, amid controversies over its controversial plans to allow carbon credits for supply chain emissions, highlights a critical tension in corporate climate accountability. This leadership transition underscores the persistent risk of greenwashing, where initiatives may prioritize accommodating fossil-heavy supply chains over genuine emission reductions. Criticism from major retailers and environmental groups reveals that such concessions can undermine the integrity of net-zero commitments. Allowing offsets without strict criteria enables companies to claim sustainability progress without substantial operational changes, effectively diluting the standard’s impact and misleading stakeholders. This development is highly relevant to greenwashing debates because it demonstrates how structural weaknesses in certification bodies can facilitate misleading environmental claims. As the organization revises its standards, the outcome will determine whether it maintains rigorous accountability or succumbs to industry pressures, directly influencing trust in corporate climate pledges.
