Más del 50% de las empresas en Europa son acusadas de Greenwashing

Más del 50% de las empresas en Europa son acusadas de Greenwashing

A recent European Commission report reveals that more than half of the environmental claims made on products sold in the EU lack verifiable evidence, fueling consumer distrust and prompting stricter regulations against greenwashing. This situation calls for a shift from vague marketing to substantiated action, as authorities are introducing rigorous verification requirements and penalties for misleading advertising. As a result, many companies are now engaging in "green hushing," refraining from communicating their sustainability efforts for fear of being accused of exaggeration. This trend underscores the urgent need for robust, auditable ESG data. Without credible foundations, businesses risk losing trust and failing to meet the stringent standards expected by regulators and investors at global forums such as COP29. DoGood People addresses this challenge by empowering organizations to implement verifiable ESG strategies. By engaging employees and optimizing key metrics, the tool helps companies build genuine credibility. This approach not only ensures compliance with new laws but also strengthens brand reputation, attracting talent and sustainable investment while actively combating greenwashing through tangible results.
