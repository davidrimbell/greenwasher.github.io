First lawsuit for greenwashing in Spain: Iberdrola accuses Repsol of "misleading advertising" | The Corner

Iberdrola has sued Repsol in Spain’s first greenwashing trial, alleging unfair competition and misleading advertising. The electric giant claims Repsol misleads consumers by promoting sustainability while primarily driving fossil fuel use. This landmark case challenges corporate communications that exaggerate environmental commitments. It sets a critical precedent for holding energy firms accountable for deceptive marketing practices that mask their true carbon footprint. The trial highlights growing scrutiny of green claims in the energy sector. Its outcome will likely influence how companies balance marketing narratives with genuine sustainable practices, reinforcing legal standards against greenwashing.
