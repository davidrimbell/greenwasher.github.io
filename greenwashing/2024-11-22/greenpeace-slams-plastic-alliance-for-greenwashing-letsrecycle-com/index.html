Greenpeace slams plastic alliance for ‘greenwashing’ - letsrecycle.com

Greenpeace slams plastic alliance for ‘greenwashing’ - letsrecycle.com

The Alliance to End Plastic Waste, backed by major fossil fuel producers, falsely claims to reduce plastic pollution while simultaneously manufacturing millions of tonnes of plastic. This discrepancy exposes a sophisticated greenwashing strategy, where the industry promotes inadequate recycling schemes to divert attention from the root cause: unchecked production. This investigation highlights how corporations use public relations campaigns to counteract negative perceptions, effectively masking their role in the growing waste crisis. By focusing on minor cleanup efforts rather than production cuts, these entities deceive the public and policymakers about the true scale of environmental harm caused by single-use plastics. Such deception is particularly dangerous ahead of critical UN treaty negotiations. The article serves as a stark warning that industry lobbyists are attempting to derail binding global rules with false solutions. Recognizing this greenwashing is essential for governments to enforce genuine production limits and hold polluters accountable for plastic pollution.
