Environmental footprint software Pickler receives €500,000 in funding round

Pickler, an independent software startup, has secured funding to help packaging companies accurately calculate and share emissions data. This initiative aims to assist producers, distributors, and retailers in complying with strict EU legislation, such as the Green Claims Directive. By providing transparent, lifecycle-based environmental evidence, the platform enables businesses to distinguish their genuine sustainability efforts from competitors. The software’s predictive algorithms and accessible methodology allow even those with limited technical backgrounds to generate cost-effective Life Cycle Assessments, facilitating better decision-making across the value chain. The article is highly relevant to greenwashing because it directly addresses the industry’s need for credible, verifiable environmental claims. By aligning its methodology with regulatory frameworks and collaborating with academic foundations, Pickler seeks to eliminate ambiguity in marketing messages. This focus on independence and data accuracy ensures that companies can substantiate their eco-friendly assertions, thereby combating misleading practices. Transparent reporting allows consumers and regulators to trust sustainability narratives, reducing the risk of greenwashing accusations and fostering a market where truthful environmental stewardship is rewarded. Beyond compliance, the platform empowers businesses to actively reduce their carbon footprint while maintaining a positive business case. The ability to compare materials and processes supports strategic shifts toward lower-emission designs. As more companies adopt such transparent tools, the industry moves toward collective emission reduction targets. This shift demonstrates that sustainability and profitability are not mutually exclusive, encouraging widespread adoption of honest environmental practices across the global packaging sector.
