Parliament panel pitches for ESG oversight body to combat greenwashing activities

Parliament panel pitches for ESG oversight body to combat greenwashing activities

A Parliamentary committee urges the establishment of a dedicated ESG oversight body to actively combat greenwashing, arguing that current disclosure-based regimes are insufficient to prevent fraudulent climate-friendly claims. This recommendation highlights the critical need for specialized forensic expertise to scrutinize corporate sustainability assertions, moving beyond mere reporting to enforce genuine accountability. The panel insists on statutory amendments to make ESG objectives integral to directors' fiduciary duties, thereby elevating sustainability from optional disclosure to a non-negotiable strategic imperative. By ensuring expeditious penal applications against false claims, the proposed framework aims to deter misleading marketing and secure a clear legal bedrock for corporate responsibility. This report is relevant to greenwashing because it identifies the regulatory gap allowing companies to make unsubstantiated environmental claims without severe consequences. By demanding explicit legislative mandates and robust oversight mechanisms, the article underscores how inadequate governance structures currently enable greenwashing, calling for systemic reforms to ensure transparency and prevent deceptive corporate practices.
