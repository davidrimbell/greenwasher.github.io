El negocio del consumo consciente: el auge de las etiquetas verdes y de los productos sustentables - La Tercera

El negocio del consumo consciente: el auge de las etiquetas verdes y de los productos sustentables - La Tercera

Research confirms that specific and clear sustainability labels lead to a significant increase in sales, encouraging consumers to choose eco-friendly options even without actively seeking them out. This phenomenon demonstrates that transparency in environmental attributes, such as carbon neutrality, is a powerful tool that directly influences purchasing decisions, surpassing the need for aggressive promotions or discounts. In Chile, this behavior is reflected in the explosive growth of sustainable categories on leading platforms, indicating that environmental awareness is no longer a marginal trend but a structural factor in the market. The preference for ethical and environmentally friendly products has permeated various generations, showing that consumers value the composition and origin of goods as key elements of their identity as responsible buyers. This article is relevant to greenwashing because it exposes the power of honest communication versus deception. By showing that precise and verifiable labels are effective, it highlights that success in the current market depends on the integrity of the information presented. This discourages superficial environmental marketing, as increasingly informed consumers distinguish between real substance and vague claims, rewarding brands with tangible actions and penalizing those that attempt to compensate for a lack of commitment with empty slogans.
