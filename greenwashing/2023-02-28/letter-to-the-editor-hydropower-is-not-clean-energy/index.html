Letter to the editor: Hydropower is not clean energy

Letter to the editor: Hydropower is not clean energy

The article argues that Central Maine Power is greenwashing by labeling the New England Clean Energy Connect as clean. This marketing spin deceptively frames hydroelectric power from Quebec as environmentally friendly, masking the true motive of corporate financial gain behind ecological benefits. Hydropower is misrepresented as emissions-free, despite reservoirs emitting significant methane. Recent U.N. reporting standards now require dam operators to disclose these emissions, challenging the validity of hydro as a sustainable energy source. Ignoring this data leads to inaccurate permitting and misguided infrastructure investments. This case is relevant to greenwashing because it illustrates how corporations exploit outdated definitions of "clean" energy to justify environmentally harmful projects. The piece urges the public to scrutinize such claims and oppose the corridor, highlighting the danger of accepting corporate narratives without verifying scientific facts.
