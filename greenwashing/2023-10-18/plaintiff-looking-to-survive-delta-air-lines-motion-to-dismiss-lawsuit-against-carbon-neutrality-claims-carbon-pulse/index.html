Plaintiff looking to "survive" Delta Air Lines' motion to dismiss lawsuit against carbon neutrality claims « Carbon Pulse

Delta faces a federal lawsuit challenging its carbon offsetting practices. The airline’s attempt to dismiss the case was opposed by the plaintiff, keeping the legal action alive. This dispute highlights the growing scrutiny of voluntary carbon markets. Airlines often use offsets to claim carbon neutrality, yet these methods remain legally and ethically contentious. Such litigation underscores the risks of greenwashing in the aviation sector. Regulators and courts are increasingly holding companies accountable for potentially misleading environmental claims.
