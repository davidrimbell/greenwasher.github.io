Sustainable funds fall foul of ‘greenwashing’ rules

Divergent global anti-greenwashing regulations make it nearly impossible for funds to consistently market themselves as sustainable. A study reveals that a negligible percentage of such products actually comply with these varied regulatory requirements. This highlights the severe challenge of achieving consistent sustainability claims amidst fragmented international oversight.
