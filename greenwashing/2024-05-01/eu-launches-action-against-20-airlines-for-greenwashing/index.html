EU launches action against 20 airlines for greenwashing

EU authorities are cracking down on airlines for misleading environmental claims, emphasizing the need for scientifically substantiated evidence regarding carbon offsets and sustainable fuels. This regulatory action aims to protect consumers from deceptive marketing, ensuring transparency in corporate environmental commitments rather than vague assertions. The initiative highlights the growing scrutiny of corporate greenwashing, with recent court rulings setting strict benchmarks for what airlines can legally promise regarding their ecological impact.
