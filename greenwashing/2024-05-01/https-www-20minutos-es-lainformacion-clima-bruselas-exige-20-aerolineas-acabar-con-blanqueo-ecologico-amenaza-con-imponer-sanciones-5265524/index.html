Greenwashing
ES EN

The European Commission and consumer organizations have warned twenty airlines about potentially misleading practices that constitute greenwashing. These companies are being urged to stop making unverified claims about offsetting emissions through additional fees, after it was identified that they lead consumers to believe their payments reliably and effectively mitigate climate impact. The report highlights the lack of scientific rigor in tools such as emissions calculators and claims about sustainable fuels, as well as the absence of transparent comparisons. By presenting these solutions without solid evidence or verifiable commitments toward carbon neutrality, the airlines are violating unfair commercial practices regulations, fostering distrust and exploiting passengers’ environmental awareness. This intervention is crucial in the fight against greenwashing because it sets a precedent for legal oversight of how environmental benefits are communicated. By demanding transparency and a scientific basis, authorities protect consumers from being misled by empty green marketing, forcing the airline industry to replace deceptive advertising with real, auditable initiatives that demonstrate genuine reductions in their pollution footprint.
