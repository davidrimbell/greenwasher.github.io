EU to take action against 20 airlines on greenwashing concerns « Carbon Pulse

The European Commission has launched investigations into twenty airlines for potential greenwashing, specifically targeting their use of voluntary carbon credits. This action highlights the growing scrutiny of aviation’s sustainability claims by consumer authorities across member states. It underscores the regulatory pushback against misleading environmental marketing in the travel sector.
