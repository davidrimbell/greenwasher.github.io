El Inventario Forestal Continuo contribuiría a revalorizar los créditos de carbono del monte

The article examines the emerging carbon credit market in Galicia, highlighting how the proposed FES-CO2 fund seeks to establish fixed pricing for carbon sequestration. A key determinant of the value of these credits is uncertainty; therefore, implementing a Continuous Forest Inventory is essential. By providing precise, up-to-date data on forest composition and carbon storage capacity, this inventory reduces market ambiguity. As a result, it enhances the value of carbon assets for landowners by introducing greater objectivity in calculating the additional carbon captured through specific projects relative to natural baselines. This regulatory development serves as an important case study for understanding greenwashing risks within carbon markets. When mechanisms lack transparency or robust verification standards, there is a risk that credits may be overvalued or based on inaccurate baseline assumptions. The emphasis on accurate measurement and the distinction between natural sequestration and the additional impacts of projects underscores the need for rigorous scientific validation. Without such clarity, entities could exploit vague metrics to market environmental benefits that do not genuinely exist, engaging in deceptive practices that undermine the integrity of climate action initiatives. Ultimately, the text cautions against viewing forests as a sole solution to emission problems, stressing the need for reductions across all polluting sectors, such as transport and energy. This perspective is relevant to discussions on greenwashing because it highlights the risk of "offsetting" becoming a license to pollute elsewhere. True sustainability requires genuine emission reductions in high-impact industries rather than relying exclusively on claims of carbon storage. This holistic approach ensures that environmental marketing reflects real systemic change rather than superficial fixes, thereby maintaining the credibility of corporate and governmental sustainability commitments.
