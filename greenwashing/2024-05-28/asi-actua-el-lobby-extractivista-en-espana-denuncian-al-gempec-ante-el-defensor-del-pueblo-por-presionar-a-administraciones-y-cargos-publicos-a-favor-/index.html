Así actúa el lobby extractivista en España: denuncian al GEMPEC ante el Defensor del Pueblo por presionar a Administraciones y cargos públicos a favor del proyecto de tierras raras de Quantum Minería en Ciudad Real - Tercera Información

The complaint filed by Sí a la Tierra Viva reveals how a pressure group without legal status, linked to mining and energy interests, employs greenwashing strategies to influence public decision-making. This entity operates in the shadows, holding private meetings with local authorities to advance the interests of a mining company, exploiting the lack of transparency to manipulate the administrative process and undermine the rights of affected citizens. The article highlights the use of false arguments and the intentional omission of serious environmental risks, such as radioactivity and water consumption in a dry region, as key tools of greenwashing. By presenting rare earth extraction as a clean and strategic solution, the real negative impacts are concealed, creating a misleading narrative that favors commercial exploitation at the expense of the environment and public health, thereby exposing a disconnect between the projected ecological image and the polluting reality of the project. This situation is relevant for understanding greenwashing because it demonstrates how sustainability and national security rhetoric are used to legitimize harmful industries and pressure government officials. The complaint underscores the urgency of detecting these manipulative tactics that subordinate the public interest to private interests, reminding us that approving projects without social consensus or rigorous analysis compromises institutional integrity and the well-being of local communities.
