EPA deliberately engineering power grid failure with new “Clean Power Plan 2.0” – NaturalNews.com

EPA deliberately engineering power grid failure with new “Clean Power Plan 2.0” – NaturalNews.com

The article argues that the EPA’s Clean Power Plan 2.0 represents a dangerous form of greenwashing by prioritizing aggressive net-zero targets over tangible grid reliability. By mandating unproven emissions technologies, the regulation forces the closure of stable coal and gas plants, effectively engineering a power grid vulnerable to failure. This policy masks its true impact by framing the transition to renewables as an environmental necessity, while ignoring the practical realities of energy infrastructure. Critics highlight that the forced shift toward intermittent solar and wind sources is unsustainable, citing their low reliability percentages and recent industry instability. The article suggests that this "rush-to-green" agenda ignores existing, effective clean technologies like modern coal and carbon capture, which could achieve emission reductions without sacrificing dependability. By dismissing these alternatives, the plan creates a false narrative that renewable energy is the only viable path, despite evidence of rising costs and potential blackouts. This regulatory approach is relevant to greenwashing because it disguises a potentially catastrophic reliability crisis as an environmental triumph. The policy promotes renewable energy under the guise of sustainability, even when those sources prove economically fragile and technically insufficient to meet baseline demand. Ultimately, the article warns that such rigid mandates prioritize ideological goals over public safety, exploiting the language of climate action to implement an unrealistic and economically damaging energy strategy.
