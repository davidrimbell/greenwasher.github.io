'Carbon neutral' fee charged by ride-hailing company risks greenwashing -NGO « Carbon Pulse

'Carbon neutral' fee charged by ride-hailing company risks greenwashing -NGO « Carbon Pulse

A Southeast Asian ride-hailing service faces greenwashing accusations for charging customers to offset travel emissions using questionable voluntary carbon credits. This practice raises serious doubts about the validity of their climate mitigation efforts. The investigation reveals that the underlying credits lack integrity, potentially misleading consumers who believe they are supporting genuine environmental benefits. Consequently, the company risks legal and reputational damage for promoting ineffective solutions as sustainable. This case highlights the critical importance of verifying carbon credit quality. It demonstrates how opaque markets can enable corporations to exploit voluntary schemes for marketing gains without delivering real climate impact.
