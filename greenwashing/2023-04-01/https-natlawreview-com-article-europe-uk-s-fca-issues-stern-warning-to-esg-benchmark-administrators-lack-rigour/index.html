Greenwashing
ES EN

The Financial Conduct Authority criticizes ESG benchmark disclosures for being poor, incomplete, and inaccurate. This regulatory crackdown signals a shift toward stricter enforcement against misleading environmental claims, directly targeting the opacity that enables greenwashing. Administrators are warned that failure to comply will result in formal supervisory tools and enforcement actions. The regulator demands greater transparency and precision in how ESG factors are integrated and calculated within benchmark methodologies. Consequently, investors must reduce reliance on these benchmarks or increase scrutiny until standards improve. This article highlights the growing regulatory pressure to eliminate greenwashing by ensuring ESG ratings accurately reflect underlying data and methodologies.
