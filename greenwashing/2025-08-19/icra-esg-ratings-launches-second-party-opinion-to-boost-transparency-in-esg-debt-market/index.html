ICRA ESG Ratings launches Second Party Opinion to boost transparency in ESG debt market

ICRA ESG Ratings launches Second Party Opinion to boost transparency in ESG debt market

ICRA ESG Ratings has launched a Second Party Opinion service to independently assess ESG debt frameworks, aiming to strengthen market credibility and align instruments with global best practices. This initiative addresses the growing demand for transparent, rigorous evaluations of governance and use of proceeds, helping issuers demonstrate the integrity of their sustainability commitments to investors. The launch responds directly to regulatory mandates requiring independent third-party reviewers for ESG securities, thereby enhancing the verification of project criteria and impact reporting. By ensuring strict adherence to international standards, these reviews are designed to build investor confidence that capital is genuinely directed toward sustainable initiatives, rather than superficial claims. This development is critical to combating greenwashing by introducing objective scrutiny into the rapidly expanding sustainable finance ecosystem. As regulatory requirements evolve and market volume surges, independent opinions provide a necessary barrier against misleading environmental claims, ensuring that ESG labels reflect substantive, verifiable impact rather than marketing rhetoric.
