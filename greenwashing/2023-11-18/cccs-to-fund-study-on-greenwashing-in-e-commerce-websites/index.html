CCCS to fund study on greenwashing in e-commerce websites

CCCS to fund study on greenwashing in e-commerce websites

Research reveals that nearly half of online product environmental claims are vague, misleading consumers about actual ecological benefits. This highlights greenwashing as a critical issue where suppliers deceive buyers by overstating environmental friendliness without substantiating evidence or clear explanations. The study underscores how generic terms and technical jargon obscure true impacts, preventing verification. Such practices manipulate consumer perception, creating an illusion of sustainability that does not reflect reality. This deception erodes trust and allows companies to capitalize on eco-consciousness without genuine commitment. Consequently, regulatory bodies emphasize the need for specific, transparent, and verifiable claims. This article is relevant to greenwashing as it identifies common deceptive marketing tactics and establishes clear guidelines to combat misleading environmental assertions, ensuring accountability in digital marketplaces.
