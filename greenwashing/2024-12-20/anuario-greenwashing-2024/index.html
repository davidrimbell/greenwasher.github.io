Anuario Greenwashing 2024

The article highlights a significant shift in consumer behavior, where environmental sustainability has become a key purchasing factor, prompting companies to heavily market themselves as eco-friendly. However, this trend has exacerbated the prevalence of greenwashing, a deceptive strategy where corporations falsely portray their products and practices as environmentally responsible to capitalize on public concern. Despite media and consumer scrutiny, many of these claims lack genuine support from concrete sustainable actions, revealing a disconnect between marketing narratives and actual corporate responsibility. A major development in 2024 was the transition from mere criticism to formal legal accountability, marking a historic turning point in the fight against deceptive advertising. Several prominent companies, particularly in the energy and food sectors, faced official complaints for misleading campaigns, signaling a stricter regulatory environment. This legal push-back is complemented by emerging phenomena like "greenhushing," where firms understate their environmental efforts to avoid scrutiny, indicating a complex evolution in corporate communication strategies rather than a simple disappearance of the problem. The publication of the Greenwashing Yearbook underscores the urgency of distinguishing between genuine sustainability and superficial marketing. While there is a noted decline in some aspects of greenwashing and an increase in public debate, the article concludes that substantial work remains to ensure corporate practices align with their ecological claims. The real significance lies in the growing demand for transparency, as consumers and regulators increasingly reject symbolic gestures in favor of verifiable, sustainable business models that go beyond mere appearance.
