Greenwashing
ES EN

A consumer advocacy group has formally accused seventeen European airlines of greenwashing, alleging their marketing practices violate EU unfair commercial rules. The complaint argues that carriers are deceptive by suggesting their services are environmentally responsible, thereby misleading the public about the sustainability of air travel. Specifically, the group criticizes extra fees for sustainable aviation fuels and carbon offset programs as misleading since the fuels are not yet market-ready and offset schemes fail to genuinely neutralize emissions. This highlights how companies exploit consumer guilt with vague promises rather than providing tangible, immediate environmental benefits, a hallmark of deceptive green marketing. This development is crucial for understanding greenwashing as it demonstrates regulatory scrutiny of ambiguous climate claims. It underscores the tension between corporate sustainability marketing and actual ecological impact, urging authorities to enforce stricter transparency and prevent companies from profiting from the perception of eco-friendliness without substantive action.
