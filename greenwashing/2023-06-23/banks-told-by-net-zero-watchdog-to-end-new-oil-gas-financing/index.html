Banks told by net-zero watchdog to end new oil, gas financing

The Science Based Targets initiative mandates that financial institutions must immediately cease funding new fossil fuel projects to have their net-zero claims verified. This strict requirement ensures banks do not exacerbate climate issues while claiming sustainability, forcing them to align actual capital allocation with stated environmental goals rather than relying on vague pledges. By establishing rigorous third-party validation standards, this policy addresses the industry’s historical lack of transparency regarding how net-zero targets will be achieved. The move prevents companies from masking continued high-carbon investments, thereby enhancing investor confidence and ensuring that sustainability commitments reflect tangible reductions in emissions across all operational scopes. This development is crucial for combating greenwashing, as it closes loopholes that allowed banks to finance carbon-intensive expansion while marketing themselves as eco-friendly. By enforcing mandatory criteria for fossil financing, the initiative ensures that net-zero labels represent genuine progress, protecting consumers and investors from misleading environmental marketing and promoting accountability in the financial sector.
