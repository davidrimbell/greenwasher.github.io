Varias asociaciones europeas de consumidores acusan de 'lavado verde' a 17 aerolíneas

European consumer organizations, including several Spanish associations, have filed a complaint with the European Commission against 17 airlines for greenwashing practices. These companies use misleading marketing to suggest that travelers can offset or neutralize their CO2 emissions through additional payments, thereby inducing consumers to mistakenly believe that their current flights are sustainable. The analysis points out that these claims are objectively false because the climate benefits of carbon offsetting are uncertain, and sustainable aviation fuels are not yet ready for the market. By implying that air travel can be responsible or eco-friendly through such strategies, the airlines mislead the public, creating confusion and violating the commercial transparency required by EU regulations on unfair business practices. This complaint is crucial in the fight against greenwashing, as it exposes how the aviation industry distorts environmental realities to maintain demand. By urging authorities to take firm action, the goal is to establish a legal precedent that compels honesty about the sector’s unsustainability, protecting consumers from advertising that obscures the urgent need for genuine systemic changes rather than mere symbolic gestures.
