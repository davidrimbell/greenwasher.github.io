Fundación Terram publicó su Balance Ambiental: critican a Gobierno e industrias por su "sello verde"

Fundación Terram’s 2023 Environmental Balance criticizes the Chilean government for prioritizing a “green development” narrative that essentially serves global economies. The organization argues that the administration has shifted toward promoting natural resource extraction under a green label, lacking adequate regulations to protect local ecosystems. This approach favors large-scale investments in lithium and industrial hydrogen, effectively exporting raw materials to the Global North while neglecting the environmental impact on Chilean territories. The report highlights specific instances of corporate greenwashing, most notably mining company Teck. Despite facing serious environmental violations and ongoing sanctions, Teck donated funds to a nature conservation fund. Terram identifies this as a clear strategy to mask destructive operations with charitable contributions, a practice that undermines genuine environmental stewardship and exploits the disparity between corporate public relations and actual ecological damage. This analysis is crucial for understanding greenwashing because it exposes how political rhetoric and corporate philanthropy can be used to legitimize unsustainable practices. By contrasting the government’s promotional “green” agenda with its approval of controversial projects like Los Bronces Integrado and the ongoing issues in the salmon industry, the article reveals a pattern where environmental branding substitutes for substantive regulation, allowing significant ecological harm to continue unchecked.
