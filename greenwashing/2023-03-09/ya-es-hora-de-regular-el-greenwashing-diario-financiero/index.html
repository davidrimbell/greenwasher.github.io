Ya es hora de regular el greenwashing | Diario Financiero

Ya es hora de regular el greenwashing | Diario Financiero

Certifying firms linked to deforestation underscores the urgent need for regulations against greenwashing to safeguard economic integrity and incentivize genuine sustainability. However, penalties must be carefully calibrated, avoiding permanent bans to ensure they remain proportional and legally sound. Crucially, restricting all advertising related to voluntary commitments or reparations risks stifling environmental innovation. Regulations should distinguish between serious offenses and minor infractions, allowing companies to publicize corrective actions without undermining their credibility. This nuance is vital in the discourse on greenwashing, as blanket prohibitions may inadvertently discourage the very sustainable practices that policymakers aim to promote. Balanced regulation ensures accountability while fostering continuous environmental improvement, rather than uniformly punishing all non-compliance.
