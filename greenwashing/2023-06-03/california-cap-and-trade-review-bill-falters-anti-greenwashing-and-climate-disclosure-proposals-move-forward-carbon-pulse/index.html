California cap-and-trade review bill falters, anti-greenwashing and climate disclosure proposals move forward « Carbon Pulse

California advances voluntary carbon offset and climate disclosure legislation while pausing its cap-and-trade review. These regulatory shifts signal tightening oversight of carbon markets, directly impacting how companies validate their environmental claims. This development is crucial for understanding greenwashing, as stricter disclosure rules increase transparency. It reduces the risk of misleading sustainability narratives by enforcing more rigorous verification standards for carbon credits and corporate climate reporting.
