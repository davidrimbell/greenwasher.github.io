Los Supervisores europeos analiza cuál es el impacto del Greenwashing en el sector

European authorities have published joint reports defining greenwashing as the discrepancy between sustainability claims and the actual profile of a financial entity or product. This practice, whether intentional or not, misleads consumers and investors, eroding trust in the market and jeopardizing the integrity of sustainable finance. The insurance and pensions sector is significantly impacted, as unfounded claims distort purchasing decisions and damage the reputation of providers. This widespread distrust not only affects companies but also hinders the financing needed for the transition to a truly sustainable economy, creating a negative cycle for all market participants. Regulatory oversight is evolving to address these practices, although supervisors face initial challenges such as a lack of resources and specialized methodologies. While the current framework provides a foundation, gaps have been identified that require improvements. The relevance of this article lies in highlighting how regulation seeks to protect investors from greenwashing, ensuring that the “green” label genuinely reflects sustainability and is not used as a misleading marketing tool.
