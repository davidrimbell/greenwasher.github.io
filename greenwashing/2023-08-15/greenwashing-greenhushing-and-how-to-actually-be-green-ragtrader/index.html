Greenwashing, greenhushing and how to actually be green - Ragtrader

Greenwashing, greenhushing and how to actually be green - Ragtrader

The article argues that the era of vague greenwashing slogans is ending, replaced by a demand for authentic accountability. Consumers and regulators are increasingly scrutinizing corporate claims, forcing fashion brands to move beyond superficial marketing. This shift highlights that credibility is now built on substantive action and transparency rather than empty promises, signaling a necessary evolution in the economic system toward genuine sustainability. Certified B Corporations exemplify this transition by treating sustainability as an ongoing journey rather than a final destination. Unlike traditional certifications that may offer a "finish line," B Corp certification requires rigorous, continuous improvement in social and environmental performance. This framework helps brands navigate the complex realities of their supply chains, recognizing tangible efforts in resource conservation, fair labor, and community impact, thereby providing a robust alternative to greenwashing narratives. Crucially, the piece advises against "greenhushing," or the fear of admitting imperfections, which stifles progress. Brands are encouraged to openly share their challenges and areas for improvement, as transparency fosters trust and collaborative support from stakeholders. By owning their shortcomings alongside their successes, companies demonstrate integrity and purpose, ultimately proving that acknowledging the complexity of impact is the most effective defense against accusations of greenwashing.
