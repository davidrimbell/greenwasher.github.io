SBTi CEO Dr Amaral Resigns Citing Personal Reasons

SBTi CEO Dr Amaral Resigns Citing Personal Reasons

The Science Based Targets initiative faces significant internal turmoil and public scrutiny over proposed changes to its Net-Zero Standard. The leadership resignation occurs amidst heated debates regarding whether to allow companies to rely more heavily on carbon credits for offsetting indirect emissions, challenging the organization's credibility and governance integrity. This controversy is central to greenwashing concerns, as relaxing offset rules could enable corporations to claim climate progress without implementing substantive operational reductions. By potentially increasing the allowance for environmental attribute certificates, the initiative risks legitimizing superficial solutions that mask ongoing environmental harm rather than driving genuine decarbonization efforts across supply chains. The resulting staff unrest and member dissatisfaction highlight the tension between corporate convenience and scientific rigor. As the organization navigates these governance failures and potential policy dilutions, the situation underscores the urgent need for transparent, strict standards to prevent the erosion of trust in corporate climate accountability frameworks globally.
