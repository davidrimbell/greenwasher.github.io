TotalEnergies: Greenwashing en estado puro

The article concludes that TotalEnergies engages in "pure greenwashing" by preserving its core business model while promoting a false narrative of ecological responsibility. Despite rebranding and pledging substantial emission reductions, the company continues to invest heavily in large-scale new fossil fuel projects in Uganda and the United States. This contradiction reveals that the corporation prioritizes the continued extraction and sale of oil and gas over genuine environmental stewardship, using sustainability claims merely to sanitize its public image. Financial incentives remain the primary driver, as renewable energy has not yet proven sufficiently profitable compared to traditional hydrocarbons. The investigation highlights that while the company invests billions in expanding production, its actual contribution to renewable energy is negligible. Industry experts confirm that major oil companies are reluctant to fully transform their business models because fossil fuel extraction remains far more lucrative, indicating that the transition is rhetorical rather than operational. This case is crucial for understanding greenwashing because it exposes the mechanisms companies use to avoid accountability for climate change. By launching high-profile environmental initiatives while simultaneously destroying ecosystems and displacing communities through new fossil fuel projects, TotalEnergies creates a dissonance between its stated values and its actions. The article illustrates how multinational corporations leverage the urgency of the climate crisis to maintain public legitimacy without altering the harmful practices that contribute to global warming.
