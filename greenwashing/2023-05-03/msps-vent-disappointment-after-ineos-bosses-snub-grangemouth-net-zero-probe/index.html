MSPs vent 'disappointment' after Ineos bosses snub Grangemouth net zero probe

Ineos declined to formally testify before a Scottish parliamentary inquiry into the just transition of the Grangemouth petrochemical hub, despite setting ambitious net-zero targets. This refusal highlights a critical disconnect between corporate environmental claims and transparent engagement with public oversight mechanisms. The company’s absence denies policymakers vital insight into actual workforce impacts and operational realities. Such non-cooperation raises serious questions about the authenticity of sustainability pledges, suggesting that public relations efforts may overshadow genuine accountability. This case is relevant to greenwashing because it demonstrates how firms can assert ecological commitments while evading the rigorous scrutiny necessary to verify them. By avoiding formal evidence, Ineos risks appearing to prioritize image over the substantive, verifiable actions required for a credible transition, undermining trust in corporate climate narratives.
