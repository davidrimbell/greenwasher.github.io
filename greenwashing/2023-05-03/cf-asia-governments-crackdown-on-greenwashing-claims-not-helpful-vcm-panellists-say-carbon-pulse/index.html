CF ASIA: Governments crackdown on greenwashing claims 'not helpful', VCM panellists say « Carbon Pulse

Governments are cracking down on greenwashing by regulating corporate climate claims. This effort aims to enforce stricter standards and prevent misleading environmental marketing. However, this regulatory push creates conflicting messages for global companies operating across various jurisdictions. Businesses face inconsistent rules that complicate their compliance strategies. This situation highlights the challenge of harmonizing greenwashing regulations. It underscores the need for clear, unified standards to effectively combat false ecological claims.
