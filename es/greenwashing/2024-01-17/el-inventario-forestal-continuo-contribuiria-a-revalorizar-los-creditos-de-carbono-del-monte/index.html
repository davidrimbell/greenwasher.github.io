El Inventario Forestal Continuo contribuiría a revalorizar los créditos de carbono del monte

The article explores the emerging carbon credit market in Galicia, highlighting how the proposed FES-CO2 fund aims to establish fixed pricing for carbon retention. A critical factor determining the value of these credits is uncertainty; therefore, the implementation of a Continuous Forest Inventory is crucial. By providing precise, up-to-date data on forest composition and carbon storage capabilities, this inventory reduces market ambiguity. Consequently, it revalues carbon assets for landowners by offering greater objectivity in calculating the additional carbon captured through specific projects compared to natural baselines. This regulatory shift serves as a vital case study for understanding greenwashing risks within carbon markets. When mechanisms lack transparency or robust verification standards, there is a danger that credits may be overvalued or based on inaccurate baseline assumptions. The emphasis on accurate measurement and the distinction between natural sequestration and additional project impacts underscores the necessity for rigorous scientific validation. Without such clarity, entities might exploit vague metrics to market environmental benefits that do not genuinely exist, thereby engaging in deceptive practices that undermine the integrity of climate action initiatives. Ultimately, the text warns against viewing forests as a sole solution to emission problems, emphasizing the need for reductions across all polluting sectors like transport and energy. This perspective is relevant to greenwashing discussions because it exposes the risk of "offsetting" becoming a license to pollute elsewhere. True sustainability requires genuine emission cuts in high-impact industries rather than relying exclusively on carbon storage claims. This holistic approach ensures that environmental marketing reflects real systemic change rather than superficial fixes, maintaining the credibility of corporate and governmental sustainability commitments.
