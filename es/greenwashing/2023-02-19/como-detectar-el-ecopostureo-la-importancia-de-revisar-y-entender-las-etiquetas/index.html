Cómo detectar el 'ecopostureo': la importancia de revisar y entender las etiquetas

The article highlights the disconnect between high consumer environmental concern and common greenwashing practices in the cosmetic sector. Brands often exploit ecological preferences through misleading packaging and vague labels, creating a false impression of sustainability without substantive changes to their ingredients or production methods. This deceptive marketing, termed "ecopostureo," relies on visual cues like green colors and terms such as "bio" or "natural" to confuse buyers. By adding minimal natural components to largely synthetic products, companies exploit regulatory gaps, ensuring their items appear eco-friendly while remaining fundamentally unsustainable and potentially harmful to skin health through toxic ingredient accumulation. Understanding these tactics is crucial for consumers to avoid being misled by superficial environmental branding. The text underscores the urgent need for clearer labeling regulations and better consumer tools to interpret ingredients accurately, ensuring that environmental claims reflect genuine sustainability rather than mere marketing strategies designed to capitalize on ecological anxiety.
