Adiós al "ecopostureo" en Europa: ¿qué anuncios estarán prohibidos?

The European Parliament has approved a new law targeting greenwashing by banning misleading environmental claims and "eco-posturing." This regulation protects consumers from deceptive marketing practices that distort purchasing decisions, ensuring that sustainability assertions are grounded in verified evidence rather than vague, unproven assertions. By prohibiting generic labels like "natural" or "eco" without rigorous proof, the legislation aims to establish transparency and trust in environmental marketing. It mandates that all sustainability comparisons and labels be fair, reliable, and based on recognized certification systems, effectively curbing the manipulation of consumer perception regarding a product's true ecological impact. Furthermore, the law combats early planned obsolescence by forbidding unfounded durability claims and unnecessary replacement pressures. This approach highlights the critical shift toward holding corporations accountable for authentic sustainability, emphasizing that genuine environmental responsibility must be substantiated by solid data and transparent labeling rather than superficial advertising tactics.
