La industria de celulosa y papel se une en un frente global contra el «greenwashing» y por la sostenibilidad

The Argentine Forest and Paper Manufacturers Association (AFCP) is forming strategic alliances with international bodies like ASPAPEL and Two Sides to position its sector as a leader in the circular economy. This collaboration aims to dismantle environmental myths through data-driven communication, asserting that the industry plays a pivotal role in the global transition toward absolute sustainability. By aligning with prestigious global organizations, the association seeks to standardize its practices and enhance its credibility on the international stage. Central to this effort is the active defense against greenwashing by correcting public perceptions based on scientific evidence. The industry highlights the growth of managed forests, directly contradicting narratives of deforestation, and promotes responsible management through rigorous international certifications. Additionally, modern plants are presented as innovative biorefineries that utilize biomass efficiently, ensuring that raw materials are legal, biodegradable, and sourced from sustainably managed areas to reassure consumers. This strategy is relevant to greenwashing because it attempts to preempt accusations of environmental fraud by establishing transparent, verifiable standards before critics can claim otherwise. By showcasing high recycling rates and renewable energy usage, the industry constructs a narrative of genuine eco-compliance. This proactive approach demonstrates how corporations use strategic alliances and technical data to reshape public opinion, turning potential environmental liabilities into markers of leadership and trustworthiness in the market.
