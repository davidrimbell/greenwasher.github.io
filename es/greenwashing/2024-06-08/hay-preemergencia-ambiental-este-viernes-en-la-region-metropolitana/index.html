Hay preemergencia ambiental este viernes en la Región Metropolitana

Authorities declared an environmental pre-emergency in Santiago due to hazardous particulate matter levels, restricting heavy green-badge trucks and older private vehicles from circulating during peak hours. This urgent measure aims to rapidly improve air quality and protect public health by significantly reducing vehicular emissions in the metropolitan region. The policy is relevant to greenwashing because it highlights the tension between environmental marketing claims, such as eco-friendly vehicle seals, and the reality that certain labeled vehicles still contribute to severe pollution requiring strict regulatory intervention. Thus, the article exposes how certification systems may not fully reflect actual environmental impact, reinforcing concerns that green labels can sometimes obscure significant ecological damage rather than guaranteeing genuine sustainability.
