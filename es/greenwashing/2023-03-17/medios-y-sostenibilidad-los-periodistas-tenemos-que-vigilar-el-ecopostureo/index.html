Medios y sostenibilidad: "Los periodistas tenemos que vigilar el ecopostureo"

The article highlights the critical role of journalists in combating greenwashing by exposing eco-posturing and corporate deception. Media professionals must balance vigilance with engagement, ensuring they reveal the falsehoods behind sustainability claims rather than merely reproducing corporate narratives. This scrutiny is essential to distinguish genuine environmental responsibility from mere marketing rhetoric. Journalists face immense pressure from powerful economic interests, which often drive extreme self-censorship. By prioritizing the public’s right to know over corporate agendas, reporters can avoid harming their credibility and the audience’s trust. Overcoming this self-imposed silence is portrayed as vital for maintaining journalistic integrity in a profit-driven landscape. Relevant to greenwashing, this discussion underscores that accurate reporting is the primary defense against misleading environmental claims. Without robust, uncensored journalism, deceptive practices remain hidden from public view. Therefore, independent media scrutiny is indispensable for holding corporations accountable and ensuring transparency in sustainability communications.
