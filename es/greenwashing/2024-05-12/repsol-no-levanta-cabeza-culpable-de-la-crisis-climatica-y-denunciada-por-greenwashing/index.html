Repsol no levanta cabeza: culpable de la crisis climática y denunciada por greenwashing

The article exposes Repsol as a major contributor to the climate crisis, highlighting a stark contradiction between its marketing as a sustainable company and its reality as Spain’s most polluting entity. Despite facing accusations of greenwashing, the energy giant continues to expand fossil fuel extraction and allocates the vast majority of its future investments to oil and gas. This strategy ignores urgent scientific warnings that new infrastructure will push global temperatures beyond safe limits, demonstrating a corporate commitment to business-as-usual rather than genuine environmental stewardship. Repsol’s greenwashing efforts are particularly evident in its promotion of biocombustibles, which activists claim mask the severe environmental and social impacts of palm oil production. By obscuring the link between these fuels and deforestation, the company engages in misleading communication that violates consumer protection standards. This deceptive practice allows Repsol to present itself as part of the solution while maintaining a business model dependent on high-emission activities, thereby undermining public trust and obscuring the true cost of its operations on the planet and society. This case is crucial to understanding greenwashing because it illustrates how corporations use selective transparency and "false solutions" to deflect responsibility. Rather than addressing the primary source of emissions—the combustion of their products—Repsol shifts blame to consumers and promotes unproven technologies. The resulting legal challenges and activist protests underscore the growing scrutiny of corporate environmental claims, revealing that without binding accountability, sustainability narratives often serve as public relations tools rather than drivers of actual decarbonization.
