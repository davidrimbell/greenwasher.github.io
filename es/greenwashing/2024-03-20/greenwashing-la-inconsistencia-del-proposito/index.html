Greenwashing, la inconsistencia del propósito

Companies often exaggerate their environmental efforts, prompting public skepticism about the credibility of corporate sustainability claims. This discrepancy between marketing and actual practices defines the widespread use of the term greenwashing. The article is relevant because it highlights how misleading communication erodes trust in corporate environmental responsibility.
