Greta Thunberg denuncia: “Estamos en la era de la gran maquinaria del ecoblanqueo”

Greta Thunberg confronts the climate emergency by leveraging expert testimonies to dismantle societal denial, emphasizing that decades of negligence have left humanity unprepared for ecological collapse. Her narrative underscores the urgency of the crisis as a defining threat to future daily life and collective survival. Crucially, Thunberg identifies the current era as dominated by the "great machine of greenwashing," where communication strategies obscure environmental reality rather than addressing it. This highlights how corporate and political narratives often serve to mislead the public, prioritizing image over genuine action and delaying necessary systemic changes. This analysis is vital to understanding greenwashing because it exposes the strategic use of language and media to maintain the status quo. By linking the rise of deceptive eco-claims directly to the broader failure of political will, the work illustrates how greenwashing functions as a barrier to effective climate policy and public awareness.
