Presidente Boric apunta contra "multinacionales" y el "capital financiero" en la Cumbre de Acción Climática | Puranoticia.cl

Chile’s President Gabriel Boric emphasizes that global climate goals remain unattainable without strictly holding powerful multinational corporations and international financial capital accountable. He argues that despite democratic commitments, a resilient minority sector often ignores multilateral agreements, undermining collective environmental efforts and democratic will. The speech highlights the urgent need to combat greenwashing, which continues to allow fossil fuel-dependent practices to persist under the guise of sustainability. Boric asserts that national pledges alone are insufficient; true progress requires enforcing rules on powerful economic entities that perpetuate misleading environmental claims. This addresses greenwashing by linking corporate deception directly to climate inaction and vulnerability. Chile’s severe experiences with fires, floods, and prolonged drought underscore the real-world consequences of such deception. Consequently, the article stresses that combating corporate manipulation is essential for effective planetary crisis management and achieving genuine ecological stability.
