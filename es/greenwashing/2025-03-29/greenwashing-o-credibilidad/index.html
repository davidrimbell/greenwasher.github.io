Greenwashing o credibilidad

True responsible communication requires unwavering commitment to truth and ethics, rather than superficial aesthetic efforts. Trust is the foundational element for any sustainable reputation, emphasizing that actions must speak louder than inspirational social media posts. Communication professionals recognize a critical gap in their preparedness, acknowledging that sustainability demands deep internal transformation across all business processes. This realization highlights the need for strategic knowledge, moving beyond mere participation in committees to genuine operational integration. Authenticity demands transparency about both successes and failures, rejecting the notion of sustainability as a temporary trend. This relevance to greenwashing lies in distinguishing genuine ethical conduct from marketing deception, proving that credibility stems from honest, comprehensive reporting rather than selective, flattering narratives.
