Asociaciones europeas de consumidores denuncian a empresas energéticas hacer greenwashing - Crónica de Cantabria

Consumer organizations in Europe and Spain have formally complained against major energy companies, accusing them of greenwashing through deceptive marketing. The main conclusion is that these firms are misleading the public by using vague ecological claims and imagery to disguise their continued reliance on fossil fuels, thereby eroding consumer trust and distorting the market for genuine sustainable energy solutions. The accusations highlight specific practices, such as claiming carbon neutrality while expanding extraction activities or labeling natural gas as a clean transition fuel despite its significant methane leakage risks. These narratives are scientifically invalid and fail to disclose the true environmental impact of the companies' operations, creating a false sense of sustainability that allows higher prices without real ecological benefits. This case is highly relevant to greenwashing as it demonstrates how corporations exploit vague terminology to align themselves with climate goals without making substantive operational changes. It underscores the urgent need for stricter regulatory oversight to prevent companies from using superficial environmental branding to mask their contribution to climate change, ensuring that consumers are not deceived by marketing tactics that prioritize profit over planetary health.
