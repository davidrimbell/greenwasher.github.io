ISS y Glass defienden a Repsol en la guerra del 'ecopostureo'

Major proxy advisors ISS and Glass Lewis recommend voting in favor of Repsol’s entire agenda, particularly its ecological transition strategy. This endorsement is significant as it occurs amidst a legal challenge from rival Iberdrola, which accuses Repsol of greenwashing through deceptive sustainability marketing. The case has drawn political attention, highlighting the tension between corporate environmental claims and regulatory scrutiny regarding climate responsibility. Despite general reservations about voting on environmental policies, advisors justify their support based on Repsol’s comprehensive climate risk disclosures and its net-zero emissions target by 2050. Their recommendations are crucial because they influence the voting behavior of institutional investors who hold the majority of shares, potentially validating Repsol’s current strategic approach against accusations of insincerity. This situation underscores the growing importance of proxy advisors in holding corporations accountable for their environmental assertions. It demonstrates how institutional pressure and legal challenges converge to define the boundaries of credible corporate sustainability, making greenwashing a tangible financial and reputational risk rather than merely a marketing issue.
