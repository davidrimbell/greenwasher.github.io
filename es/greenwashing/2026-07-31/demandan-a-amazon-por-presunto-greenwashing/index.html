Demandan a Amazon por presunto greenwashing

A demand claims Amazon misleads consumers about the environmental benefits of seafood sold on its platform, a practice known as greenwashing. Shoppers allege that labels like “sustainable” or “responsible sourcing” create a false impression of minimal ecological harm. This deceptive marketing relies on vague sustainability claims while withholding critical information about the actual origins of the products. The lawsuit highlights that many fishing vessels remain untracked, with some deliberately disabling tracking devices to hide their locations. Consequently, a significant portion of imported wild-caught seafood may not originate from responsible sources. Amazon is accused of marketing these items without providing the necessary transparency to prevent consumer deception. This case is relevant to greenwashing because it demonstrates how large retailers can exploit ambiguous environmental terminology. By using broad, positive labels without substantiation, companies can attract eco-conscious buyers while masking unsustainable practices. It underscores the difficulty consumers face in verifying true sustainability claims in complex supply chains.
